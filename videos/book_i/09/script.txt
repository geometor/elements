[bell:proposition] [style:brisk] Euclid's Elements, Book One, Proposition Nine. <pause> Bisect a given angle. <pause> Given angle B A C, we are to bisect it.

[bell:construction] [style:brisk] Take a point D on segment A B. <pause> From segment A C, cut off segment A E equal to segment A D. <pause> Construct segment D E between points D and E. <pause> On segment D E, construct the equilateral triangle D E F. <pause> Next, construct segment A F from vertex A to point F.

[bell:insight] [style:brisk] In triangles D A F and E A F, side D A equals side E A, segment A F is common, and base D F equals base E F from our equilateral triangle. <pause> Since all three pairs of corresponding sides are equal, angle D A F equals angle E A F. <pause> Therefore angle B A C has been bisected by segment A F, <pause> as was required to do.
