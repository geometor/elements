[bell:proposition] [style:brisk] Euclid's Elements, Book One, Proposition Two. <pause> Construct at a given point a segment equal to a given segment. <pause> Given point A and segment B C, we are to construct at point A a segment equal to B C.

[bell:construction] [style:brisk] From point A to point B, construct segment A B. <pause> On segment A B, construct the equilateral triangle D A B. <pause> Next, extend segments D A and D B continuously to E and F. <pause> Construct circle B C, with center B and defining radius point C, intersecting D F at G. <pause> Next, construct circle D G, with center D and defining radius point G, intersecting D E at L.

[bell:insight] [style:brisk] Since B is the center of circle B C, segment B C equals segment B G. <pause> Again, since D is the center of circle D G, segment D L equals segment D G. <pause> Within these, D A equals D B from our equilateral triangle, so subtracting equals from equals, the remainder A L equals the remainder B G. <pause> Because both A L and B C equal B G, things equal to the same thing are equal to one another. <pause> Therefore segment A L equals segment B C, and has been constructed at the given point A, <pause> as was required to do.
