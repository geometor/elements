Book Two, Proposition Six. If a segment be bisected, and a segment be added to it in a straight line, the rectangle contained by the whole with the added segment and the added segment, together with the square on the half, is equal to the square on the segment made up of the half and the added segment. <pause> Let the segment A B be bisected at C, and let B D be added to it in a straight line.

Because A C equals C B, the rectangle A L equals C H by One Thirty-Six. By One Forty-Three, the complements about the diagonal are equal, so C H equals H F, and therefore A L equals H F.
