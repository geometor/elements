Book Two, Proposition One. If there be two segments, and one of them be cut into any number of segments whatever, the rectangle contained by the two segments is equal to the rectangles contained by the uncut segment and each of the segments. <pause> Let A and B C be two segments, and let B C be cut at random at D and E.
