Book Two, Proposition Five. If a segment be cut into equal and unequal segments, the rectangle contained by the unequal segments of the whole together with the square on the segment between the points of section is equal to the square on the half. <pause> Let the segment A B be cut into equal segments at C and into unequal segments at D.
