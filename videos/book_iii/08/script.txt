Book Three, Proposition Eight. If a point be taken outside a circle and from the point segments be drawn through to the circle, one of which is through the center and the others at random, then of the segments falling on the concave arc, that through the center is greatest and the nearer to it is always greater than the more remote; while of those falling on the convex arc, that between the point and the diameter is least, the nearer to the least is always less than the more remote, and only two equal segments will fall on the circle from the point, one on each side of the least. <pause> Let A B C be a circle and D an exterior point, with secants D A through the center and D E, D F, and D C meeting the convex arc at G, K, L, and H.

[bell:construction]
[style: methodical, precise constructive cadence]
Take the center M of the circle A B C by Three One, and join the radii M E, M F, M C, M K, M L, and M H. Then on M D at M, construct angle D M B equal to angle K M D by One Twenty-Three, and join D B.

[bell:insight]
[style: luminous, deductive geometric clarity]
On the concave arc, radius A M equals E M, so D A equals E M plus M D, which exceeds D E by One Twenty. Since radii M E and M F are equal, M D is common, and angle E M D exceeds angle F M D, the Hinge Theorem, One Twenty-Four, gives D E greater than D F, and similarly D F greater than D C. <pause> On the convex arc, in triangle M K D, M K plus K D exceeds M D by One Twenty; subtracting equal radii M K and M G leaves D G less than D K. And because M K and K D meet inside triangle M L D, their sum is less than M L plus L D by One Twenty-One; subtracting equal radii M K and M L leaves D K less than D L, and D L less than D H. <pause> By One Four, triangles M K D and M B D are congruent, so D K equals D B. Any third equal segment D N would force D B to equal D N—nearer equal to more remote, which is impossible. <pause> Therefore, D A is greatest on the concave arc, D G is least on the convex arc, distances vary monotonically with inclination, and only two equal segments fall from D on the circle. As was required to prove.
