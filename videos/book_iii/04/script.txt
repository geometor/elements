Book Three, Proposition Four. If in a circle two segments cut one another which are not through the center, they do not bisect one another. <pause> Let A B C D be a circle, and in it let two chords A C and B D, which are not through the center, cut one another at E.

I say that A C and B D do not bisect one another. For, if possible, let them bisect one another, so that A E equals E C, and B E equals E D. <pause> Take the center F of the circle A B C D by Three One, and join F E.
