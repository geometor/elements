Hippocrates of Chios
====================

*Early Greek Geometer; First Author of an Elements*

First to compose a treatise of Elements (Στοιχεῖα); squared the lunes (menoeide) and reduced the duplication of the cube to finding two mean proportionals between two straight lines.
