Proclus Diadochus
=================

*Neoplatonic Philosopher & Commentator on Euclid's Elements*

Head of the Platonic Academy in Athens whose *Commentary on the First Book of Euclid's Elements* preserves the priceless Eudemian Summary of early Greek geometry alongside ancient critiques, alternative demonstrations, and lemmas from Geminus, Posidonius, Heron, Ptolemy, and Pappus.
