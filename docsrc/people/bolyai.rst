János Bolyai
============

*Co-Founder of Hyperbolic Non-Euclidean Geometry*

Independently discovered non-Euclidean geometry and published the celebrated Appendix scientiam spatii absolute veram exhibens (1832) in his father Farkas Bolyai's Tentamen.
