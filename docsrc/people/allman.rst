George Johnston Allman
======================

*Irish Mathematician & Historian of Greek Geometry*

Author of Greek Geometry from Thales to Euclid (1889), tracing the contributions of the Pythagoreans, Hippocrates, Archytas, Eudoxus, and Theaetetus.
