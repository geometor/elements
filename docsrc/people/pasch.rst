Moritz Pasch
============

*Pioneer of Modern Axiomatic Geometry*

In Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie (1882), identified the unstated order and betweenness assumptions in Euclid ('Pasch's Axiom') and initiated rigorous modern axiomatics.
