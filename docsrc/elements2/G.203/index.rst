G.203 perturbed proportion
==========================

.. rubric:: `V.def.18 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_v/definitions/#v-def-18>`_

.. enunciation::

   A:term:`**perturbed proportion** <perturbed-proportion>` arises when, there being three magnitudes and another set equal to them in multitude, as:term:`antecedent <G.181>` is to consequent among the first magnitudes, so is antecedent to consequent among the second magnitudes, while, as the consequent is to a third among the first magnitudes, so is a third to the antecedent among the second magnitudes.
