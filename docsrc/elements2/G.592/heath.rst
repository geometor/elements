G.592 construct pyramid comprehend — Heath
==========================================

.. rubric:: `XIII.13 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xiii/propositions/13/>`_

.. enunciation::

   *To construct a :term:`pyramid <G.553>`, to comprehend it in a given :term:`sphere <G.575>`, and to prove that the :term:`square <G.63>` on the :term:`diameter of the sphere <G.576>` is one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid.*

Let the :term:`diameter <G.61>` :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` of the given sphere be set out, and let it be cut at the :term:`point <G.1>` :g:`C <g1:C>` so that :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />`; let the :term:`semicircle <G.96>` :g:`( A D B ) <g1:( A D B )>` be described on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, let :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />` be drawn from the point :g:`C <g1:C>` :term:`at right angles <G.32>` to :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, and let :g:`/ D A / <g1:/ D A />` be joined; let the :term:`circle <G.10>` :g:`( E F G ) <g1:( E F G )>` which has its radius :eq:`equal <g1:/ E H / = g1:/ H F / = g1:/ H G / = g1:/ D C />` to :g:`/ D C / <g1:/ D C />` be set out, let the :eq:`equilateral <g1:/ E F / = g1:/ F G / = g1:/ G E />` :term:`triangle <G.11>` :g:`< E F G > <g1:< E F G >>` be :term:`inscribed in the circle <G.148>` :g:`( E F G ) <g1:( E F G )>`, :just:`[G.149] <G.149>` let the :term:`centre <G.14>` of the circle, the point :g:`H <g1:H>`, be taken, :just:`[G.98] <G.98>` let :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`, :g:`/ H F / <g1:/ H F />`, :g:`/ H G / <g1:/ H G />` be joined; from the point :g:`H <g1:H>` let :g:`/ H K / <g1:/ H K />` be set up :term:`at right angles to the plane <G.505>` of the circle :g:`( E F G ) <g1:( E F G )>`, :just:`[G.514] <G.514>` let :g:`/ H K / <g1:/ H K />` :eq:`equal <g1:/ H K / = g1:/ A C />` to the :term:`straight line <G.4>` :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` be cut off from :g:`/ H K / <g1:/ H K />`, and let :g:`/ K E / <g1:/ K E />`, :g:`/ K F / <g1:/ K F />`, :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`/ K H / <g1:/ K H />` is at right angles to the plane of the circle :g:`( E F G ) <g1:( E F G )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the :term:`plane <G.7>` of the circle :g:`( E F G ) <g1:( E F G )>`. :just:`[G.505] <G.505>`

.. subclause::

   But each of the straight lines :g:`/ H E / <g1:/ H E />`, :g:`/ H F / <g1:/ H F />`, :g:`/ H G / <g1:/ H G />` meets it:

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ H K / <g1:/ H K />` is at right angles to each of the straight lines :g:`/ H E / <g1:/ H E />`, :g:`/ H F / <g1:/ H F />`, :g:`/ H G / <g1:/ H G />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ A C / = g1:/ H K /; g1:/ C D / = g1:/ H E />` to :g:`/ H K / <g1:/ H K />`, and :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />` to :g:`/ H E / <g1:/ H E />`, and they contain :eq:`right angles <g1:< A C D ) = g1:< K H E )>`,

.. assertion:: [G.24] <G.24>

   therefore the base :g:`/ D A / <g1:/ D A />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ D A / = g1:/ K E />` to the base :g:`/ K E / <g1:/ K E />`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason each of the straight lines :g:`/ K F / <g1:/ K F />`, :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />` is also :eq:`equal <g1:/ K F / = g1:/ K G / = g1:/ D A />` to :g:`/ D A / <g1:/ D A />`; therefore the three straight lines :g:`/ K E / <g1:/ K E />`, :g:`/ K F / <g1:/ K F />`, :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />` are :eq:`equal <g1:/ K E / = g1:/ K F / = g1:/ K G />` to one another.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is triple of :g:`/ B C / <g1:/ B C />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`/ B C / <g1:/ B C />`, so is the square on :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />` to the square on :g:`/ D C / <g1:/ D C />`, as will be proved afterwards.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />` is triple of the square on :g:`/ D C / <g1:/ D C />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />` is also triple of the square on :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`, :just:`[G.590] <G.590>` and :g:`/ D C / <g1:/ D C />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ D C / = g1:/ E H />` to :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ D A / <g1:/ D A />` is also :eq:`equal <g1:/ D A / = g1:/ E F />` to :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`/ D A / <g1:/ D A />` was proved :eq:`equal <g1:/ D A / = g1:/ K E / = g1:/ K F / = g1:/ K G />` to each of the straight lines :g:`/ K E / <g1:/ K E />`, :g:`/ K F / <g1:/ K F />`, :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`, :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />`, :g:`/ G E / <g1:/ G E />` is also :eq:`equal <g1:/ E F / = g1:/ F G / = g1:/ G E / = g1:/ K E / = g1:/ K F / = g1:/ K G />` to each of the straight lines :g:`/ K E / <g1:/ K E />`, :g:`/ K F / <g1:/ K F />`, :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />`; therefore the four triangles :g:`< E F G > <g1:< E F G >>`, :g:`< K E F > <g1:< K E F >>`, :g:`< K F G > <g1:< K F G >>`, :g:`< K E G > <g1:< K E G >>` are :eq:`equilateral <g1:/ E F / = g1:/ F G / = g1:/ G E / = g1:/ K E / = g1:/ K F / = g1:/ K G />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore a pyramid has been constructed out of four :eq:`equilateral <g1:/ E F / = g1:/ F G / = g1:/ G E / = g1:/ K E / = g1:/ K F / = g1:/ K G />` triangles, the triangle :g:`< E F G > <g1:< E F G >>` being its base and the point :g:`K <g1:K>` its vertex.

It is next required to comprehend it in the given sphere and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere is one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid.

For let the straight line :g:`/ H L / <g1:/ H L />` be produced in a straight line with :g:`/ K H / <g1:/ K H />`, and let :g:`/ H L / <g1:/ H L />` be made :eq:`equal <g1:/ H L / = g1:/ C B />` to :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since, as :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` is to :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />` to :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />`, :just:`[G.218] <G.218>` while :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ A C / = g1:/ K H /; g1:/ C D / = g1:/ H E /; g1:/ C B / = g1:/ H L />` to :g:`/ K H / <g1:/ K H />`, :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />` to :g:`/ H E / <g1:/ H E />`, and :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />` to :g:`/ H L / <g1:/ H L />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`/ K H / <g1:/ K H />` is to :g:`/ H E / <g1:/ H E />`, so is :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />` to :g:`/ H L / <g1:/ H L />`; therefore the :term:`rectangle <G.81>` :g:`/ K H / <g1:/ K H />`, :g:`/ H L / <g1:/ H L />` is equal to the square on :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`. :just:`[G.228] <G.228>`

.. subclause::

   And each of the :term:`angles <G.22>` :g:`< K H E ) <g1:< K H E )>`, :g:`< E H L ) <g1:< E H L )>` is :eq:`right <g1:< K H E ) = g1:< E H L )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the semicircle described on :g:`/ K L / <g1:/ K L />` will pass through :g:`E <g1:E>` also. :just:`[G.218, G.139] <G.218, G.139>`

If then, :g:`/ K L / <g1:/ K L />` remaining fixed, the :term:`semicircle be carried round <G.591>` and restored to the same position from which it began to be moved, it will also pass through the points :g:`F <g1:F>`, :g:`G <g1:G>`, since, if :g:`/ F L / <g1:/ F L />`, :g:`/ L G / <g1:/ L G />` be joined, the angles at :g:`F <g1:F>`, :g:`G <g1:G>` similarly become right angles; and the pyramid will be comprehended in the given sphere.

.. subclause::

   For :g:`/ K L / <g1:/ K L />`, the diameter of the sphere, is :eq:`equal <g1:/ K L / = g1:/ A B />` to the diameter :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` of the given sphere, inasmuch as :g:`/ K H / <g1:/ K H />` was made :eq:`equal <g1:/ K H / = g1:/ A C /; g1:/ H L / = g1:/ C B />` to :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`, and :g:`/ H L / <g1:/ H L />` to :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />`.

I say next that the square on the diameter of the sphere is one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is triple of :g:`/ B C / <g1:/ B C />`; and, :term:`*convertendo* <G.200>`, :g:`/ B A / <g1:/ B A />` is one and a half times :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`/ B A / <g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`, so is the square on :g:`/ B A / <g1:/ B A />` to the square on :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on :g:`/ B A / <g1:/ B A />` is also one and a half times the square on :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />`.

.. subclause::

   And :g:`/ B A / <g1:/ B A />` is the diameter of the given sphere, and :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ A D / = g1:/ E F / = g1:/ F G / = g1:/ G E / = g1:/ K E / = g1:/ K F / = g1:/ K G />` to the side of the pyramid.

.. qed::

   Therefore the square on the diameter of the sphere is one and a half times the square on the side of the pyramid. :just:`Q. E. D.`
