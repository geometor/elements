G.196 componendo (composition of ratio) — Heath
===============================================

.. rubric:: `V.def.14 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_v/definitions/#v-def-14>`_

.. enunciation::

   :term:`**Composition of a ratio** <componendo>` means taking the :term:`antecedent <G.181>` together with the consequent as one in relation to the consequent by itself.
