G.206 there magnitudes others — Heath
=====================================

.. rubric:: `V.23 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_v/propositions/23/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If there be three magnitudes, and others equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two together are :term:`in the same ratio <G.176>`, and the :term:`proportion of them be perturbed <G.203>`, they will also be in the same ratio ex aequali.

Let there be three magnitudes :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`C <g1:C>`, and others equal to them in multitude, which, taken two and two together, are in the same proportion, namely :g:`D <g1:D>`, :g:`E <g1:E>`, :g:`F <g1:F>`; and let the proportion of them be perturbed, so that,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`B <g1:B>`, so is :g:`E <g1:E>` to :g:`F <g1:F>`,

.. assertion::

   and, as :g:`B <g1:B>` is to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so is :g:`D <g1:D>` to :g:`E <g1:E>`;

I say that, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so is :g:`D <g1:D>` to :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Book V, Proposition 23, Line Segments

Of :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`D <g1:D>` let :term:`equimultiples <multiple>` :g:`G <g1:G>`, :g:`H <g1:H>`, :g:`K <g1:K>` be taken, and of :g:`C <g1:C>`, :g:`E <g1:E>`, :g:`F <g1:F>` other, chance, equimultiples :g:`L <g1:L>`, :g:`M <g1:M>`, :g:`N <g1:N>`.

Then, since :g:`G <g1:G>`, :g:`H <g1:H>` are equimultiples of :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>`, and parts have the same ratio as the same multiples of them, :just:`[G.193] <G.193>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`B <g1:B>`, so is :g:`G <g1:G>` to :g:`H <g1:H>`.

For the same reason also,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`E <g1:E>` is to :g:`F <g1:F>`, so is :g:`M <g1:M>` to :g:`N <g1:N>`.

And, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`B <g1:B>`, so is :g:`E <g1:E>` to :g:`F <g1:F>`;

.. assertion:: [G.189] <G.189>

   therefore also, as :g:`G <g1:G>` is to :g:`H <g1:H>`, so is :g:`M <g1:M>` to :g:`N <g1:N>`.

Next, since, as :g:`B <g1:B>` is to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so is :g:`D <g1:D>` to :g:`E <g1:E>`,

.. assertion:: [G.195] <G.195>

   :term:`alternately <G.194>`, also, as :g:`B <g1:B>` is to :g:`D <g1:D>`, so is :g:`C <g1:C>` to :g:`E <g1:E>`.

And, since :g:`H <g1:H>`, :g:`K <g1:K>` are equimultiples of :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`D <g1:D>`, and parts have the same ratio as their equimultiples,

.. assertion:: [G.193] <G.193>

   therefore, as :g:`B <g1:B>` is to :g:`D <g1:D>`, so is :g:`H <g1:H>` to :g:`K <g1:K>`.

But, as :g:`B <g1:B>` is to :g:`D <g1:D>`, so is :g:`C <g1:C>` to :g:`E <g1:E>`;

.. assertion:: [G.189] <G.189>

   therefore also, as :g:`H <g1:H>` is to :g:`K <g1:K>`, so is :g:`C <g1:C>` to :g:`E <g1:E>`.

Again, since :g:`L <g1:L>`, :g:`M <g1:M>` are equimultiples of :g:`C <g1:C>`, :g:`E <g1:E>`,

.. assertion:: [G.193] <G.193>

   therefore, as :g:`C <g1:C>` is to :g:`E <g1:E>`, so is :g:`L <g1:L>` to :g:`M <g1:M>`.

But, as :g:`C <g1:C>` is to :g:`E <g1:E>`, so is :g:`H <g1:H>` to :g:`K <g1:K>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`H <g1:H>` is to :g:`K <g1:K>`, so is :g:`L <g1:L>` to :g:`M <g1:M>`, :just:`[G.189] <G.189>` and, alternately, as :g:`H <g1:H>` is to :g:`L <g1:L>`, so is :g:`K <g1:K>` to :g:`M <g1:M>`. :just:`[G.195] <G.195>`

But it was also proved that,

.. assertion::

   as :g:`G <g1:G>` is to :g:`H <g1:H>`, so is :g:`M <g1:M>` to :g:`N <g1:N>`.

.. subclause::

   Since, then, there are three magnitudes :g:`G <g1:G>`, :g:`H <g1:H>`, :g:`L <g1:L>`, and others equal to them in multitude :g:`K <g1:K>`, :g:`M <g1:M>`, :g:`N <g1:N>`, which taken two and two together are in the same ratio, and the proportion of them is perturbed,

.. assertion::

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <G.174>`, if :g:`G <g1:G>` is in excess of :g:`L <g1:L>`, :g:`K <g1:K>` is also in excess of :g:`N <g1:N>`; if :eq:`equal <g1:G = g1:L>`, :eq:`equal <g1:K = g1:N>`; and if less, less. :just:`[G.204] <G.204>`

And :g:`G <g1:G>`, :g:`K <g1:K>` are equimultiples of :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`D <g1:D>`, and :g:`L <g1:L>`, :g:`N <g1:N>` of :g:`C <g1:C>`, :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so is :g:`D <g1:D>` to :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
