G.125
=====

.. rubric:: `IV.7 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_iv/propositions/07/>`_

.. enunciation::

   About a given circle to circumscribe a square.

Let :g:`( A B C D ) <0098.g2:( A B C D )>` be the given circle;
thus it is required to circumscribe a square about the circle
:g:`( A B C D ) <0098.g2:( A B C D )>`.

Let two diameters :g:`/ A C / <0098.g2:/ A C />`, :g:`/ B D / <0098.g2:/ B D />` of the
circle :g:`( A B C D ) <0098.g2:( A B C D )>` be drawn at right angles
to one another, and through the points
:g:`A <0098.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0098.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0098.g2:C>`, :g:`D <0098.g2:D>` let :g:`/ F G / <0098.g2:/ F G />`, :g:`/ G H / <0098.g2:/ G H />`, :g:`/ H K / <0098.g2:/ H K />`, :g:`/ K F / <0098.g2:/ K F />` be
drawn touching the circle :g:`( A B C D ) <0098.g2:( A B C D )>`.
:just:`[G.94] <G.94>`

.. picture:: 0098.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition IV. 7 Diagram

Then, since :g:`/ F G / <0098.g2:/ F G />` touches the circle
:g:`( A B C D ) <0098.g2:( A B C D )>`,
and :g:`/ E A / <0098.g2:/ E A />` has been joined from the centre
:g:`E <0098.g2:E>` to the point of contact at :g:`A <0098.g2:A>`,

.. assertion:: [G.96] <G.96>

   therefore the angles at :g:`A <0098.g2:A>` are right.

For the same reason

.. assertion::

   the angles at the points :g:`B <0098.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0098.g2:C>`, :g:`D <0098.g2:D>` are also right.

Now, since the angle :g:`< A E B ) <0098.g2:< A E B )>` is right,
and the angle :g:`< E B G ) <0098.g2:< E B G )>` is also :eq:`right <0098.g2:< A E B ) = 0098.g2:< E B G )>`,

.. assertion:: [G.40] <G.40>

   therefore :g:`/ G H / <0098.g2:/ G H />` is parallel to :g:`/ A C / <0098.g2:/ A C />`.

For the same reason

.. assertion::

   :g:`/ A C / <0098.g2:/ A C />` is also parallel to :g:`/ F K / <0098.g2:/ F K />`,

so that :g:`/ G H / <0098.g2:/ G H />` is also parallel to :g:`/ F K / <0098.g2:/ F K />`. :just:`[G.43] <G.43>`

Similarly we can prove that

.. assertion::

   each of the straight lines :g:`/ G F / <0098.g2:/ G F />`, :g:`/ H K / <0098.g2:/ H K />` is parallel to :g:`/ B E D / <0098.g2:/ B E D />`.

Therefore :g:`< F G H K > <0098.g2:< F G H K >>`, :g:`< G H C A > <0098.g2:< G H C A >>`, :g:`< A C K F > <0098.g2:< A C K F >>`, :g:`< F G B D > <0098.g2:< F G B D >>`, :g:`< B H K D > <0098.g2:< B H K D >>` are parallelograms;
therefore :g:`/ G F / <0098.g2:/ G F />` is :eq:`equal <0098.g2:/ G F / = 0098.g2:/ H K />` to :g:`/ H K / <0098.g2:/ H K />`, and :g:`/ G H / <0098.g2:/ G H />` :eq:`to <0098.g2:/ G H / = 0098.g2:/ F K />` :g:`/ F K / <0098.g2:/ F K />`. :just:`[G.47] <G.47>`

And, since :g:`/ A C / <0098.g2:/ A C />` is :eq:`equal <0098.g2:/ A C / = 0098.g2:/ B D />` to :g:`/ B D / <0098.g2:/ B D />`,
and :g:`/ A C / <0098.g2:/ A C />` is also :eq:`equal <0098.g2:/ A C / = 0098.g2:/ G H / = 0098.g2:/ F K />` to each of the straight lines :g:`/ G H / <0098.g2:/ G H />`, :g:`/ F K / <0098.g2:/ F K />`,

.. subclause:: [G.47] <G.47>

   while :g:`/ B D / <0098.g2:/ B D />` is :eq:`equal <0098.g2:/ B D / = 0098.g2:/ G F / = 0098.g2:/ H K />` to each of the straight lines :g:`/ G F / <0098.g2:/ G F />`, :g:`/ H K / <0098.g2:/ H K />`,

therefore the quadrilateral :g:`< F G H K > <0098.g2:< F G H K >>` is :eq:`equilateral <0098.g2:/ G F / = 0098.g2:/ G H / = 0098.g2:/ H K / = 0098.g2:/ F K />`.

I say next that it is also right-angled.

For, since :g:`< G B E A > <0098.g2:< G B E A >>` is a parallelogram,
and the angle :g:`< A E B ) <0098.g2:< A E B )>` is right,
therefore the angle :g:`< A G B ) <0098.g2:< A G B )>` is also :eq:`right <0098.g2:< A E B ) = 0098.g2:< A G B )>`. :just:`[G.47] <G.47>`

Similarly we can prove that

.. assertion::

   the angles at :g:`< G H K ) <0098.g2:< G H K )>`, :g:`< H K F ) <0098.g2:< H K F )>`, :g:`< K F G ) <0098.g2:< K F G )>` are also :eq:`right <0098.g2:< A G B ) = 0098.g2:< G H K ) = 0098.g2:< H K F ) = 0098.g2:< K F G )>`.

Therefore :g:`< F G H K > <0098.g2:< F G H K >>` is right-angled.

But it was also proved :eq:`equilateral <0098.g2:/ G F / = 0098.g2:/ G H / = 0098.g2:/ H K / = 0098.g2:/ F K />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore it is a square;

and it has been circumscribed about the circle :g:`( A B C D ) <0098.g2:( A B C D )>`.

Therefore about the given circle a square has been
circumscribed.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. F.`
