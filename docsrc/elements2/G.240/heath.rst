G.240 segment apply parallelogram — Heath
=========================================

.. rubric:: `VI.28 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_vi/propositions/28/>`_

.. enunciation::

   To a given :term:`straight line <G.4>` to apply a parallelogram equal to a given :term:`rectilineal figure <G.12>` and deficient by a parallelogrammic :term:`figure <G.9>` similar to a given one : thus the given rectilineal figure must not be greater than the parallelogram described on the half of the straight line and similar to the defect.

Let :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line, :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>` the given rectilineal figure to which the figure to be applied to :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is required to be equal, not being greater than the parallelogram described on the half of :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` and similar to the defect, and :g:`< D > <g1:< D >>` the parallelogram to which the defect is required to be similar;

thus it is required to apply to the given straight line :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` a parallelogram equal to the given rectilineal figure :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>` and deficient by a parallelogrammic figure which is similar to :g:`< D > <g1:< D >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` be :eq:`bisected <g1:/ A E / = g1:/ E B />` at the :term:`point <G.1>` :g:`E <g1:E>`, and on :g:`/ E B / <g1:/ E B />` let :g:`< E B F G > <g1:< E B F G >>` be described similar and similarly situated to :g:`< D > <g1:< D >>` ; :just:`[G.229] <G.229>` let the parallelogram :g:`< A G > <g1:< A G >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   If then :g:`< A G > <g1:< A G >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< A G > = g1:< C >>` to :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>`, that which was enjoined will have been done ; for there has been applied to the given straight line :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` the parallelogram :g:`< A G > <g1:< A G >>` :eq:`equal <g1:< A G > = g1:< C >>` to the given rectilineal figure :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>` and deficient by a parallelogrammic figure :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>` which is similar to :g:`< D > <g1:< D >>`.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 28

.. subclause::

   But, if not, let :g:`< H E > <g1:< H E >>` be greater than :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>`.

.. assertion::

   Now :g:`< H E > <g1:< H E >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< H E > = g1:< G B >>` to :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>` is also greater than :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`< K L M N > <g1:< K L M N >>` be constructed at once equal to the excess by which :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>` is greater than :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>` and similar and similarly situated to :g:`< D > <g1:< D >>`. :just:`[G.237] <G.237>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< D > <g1:< D >>` is similar to :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>` ;

.. assertion:: [G.233] <G.233>

   therefore :g:`< K M > <g1:< K M >>` is also similar to :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let, then, :g:`/ K L / <g1:/ K L />` correspond to :g:`/ G E / <g1:/ G E />`, and :g:`/ L M / <g1:/ L M />` to :g:`/ G F / <g1:/ G F />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>` is equal to :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>`, :g:`< K M > <g1:< K M >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>` is greater than :g:`< K M > <g1:< K M >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore also :g:`/ G E / <g1:/ G E />` is greater than :g:`/ K L / <g1:/ K L />`, and :g:`/ G F / <g1:/ G F />` than :g:`/ L M / <g1:/ L M />`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`/ G O / <g1:/ G O />` be made :eq:`equal <g1:/ G O / = g1:/ K L />` to :g:`/ K L / <g1:/ K L />`, and :g:`/ G P / <g1:/ G P />` :eq:`equal <g1:/ G P / = g1:/ L M />` to :g:`/ L M / <g1:/ L M />` ; and let the parallelogram :g:`< O G P Q > <g1:< O G P Q >>` be completed ;

.. assertion::

   therefore it is :eq:`equal <g1:< O G P Q > = g1:< K M >>` and similar to :g:`< K M > <g1:< K M >>`.

.. assertion:: [G.233] <G.233>

   Therefore :g:`< G Q > <g1:< G Q >>` is also similar to :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>` ;

.. assertion:: [G.238] <G.238>

   therefore :g:`< G Q > <g1:< G Q >>` is about the same :term:`diameter <G.61>` with :g:`< G B > <g1:< G B >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`/ G Q B / <g1:/ G Q B />` be their diameter, and let the figure be described.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`< B G > <g1:< B G >>` is equal to :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>`, :g:`< K M > <g1:< K M >>`, and in them :g:`< G Q > <g1:< G Q >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< G Q > = g1:< K M >>` to :g:`< K M > <g1:< K M >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder, the :term:`gnomon <G.86>` :g:`< U W V > <g1:< U W V >>`, is :eq:`equal <g1:< U W V > = g1:< C >>` to the remainder :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`< P R > <g1:< P R >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< P R > = g1:< O S >>` to :g:`< O S > <g1:< O S >>`, let :g:`< Q B > <g1:< Q B >>` be added to each ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`< P B > <g1:< P B >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< P B > = g1:< O B >>` to the whole :g:`< O B > <g1:< O B >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< O B > <g1:< O B >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< O B > = g1:< T E >>` to :g:`< T E > <g1:< T E >>`, since the side :g:`/ A E / <g1:/ A E />` is also :eq:`equal <g1:/ A E / = g1:/ E B />` to the side :g:`/ E B / <g1:/ E B />` ; :just:`[G.65] <G.65>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< T E > <g1:< T E >>` is also :eq:`equal <g1:< T E > = g1:< P B >>` to :g:`< P B > <g1:< P B >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`< O S > <g1:< O S >>` be added to each ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`< T S > <g1:< T S >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< T S > = g1:< V W U >>` to the whole, the gnomon :g:`< V W U > <g1:< V W U >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the gnomon :g:`< V W U > <g1:< V W U >>` was proved :eq:`equal <g1:< V W U > = g1:< C >>` to :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< T S > <g1:< T S >>` is also :eq:`equal <g1:< T S > = g1:< C >>` to :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore to the given straight line :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` there has been applied the parallelogram :g:`< S T > <g1:< S T >>` :eq:`equal <g1:< S T > = g1:< C >>` to the given rectilineal figure :g:`< C > <g1:< C >>` and deficient by a parallelogrammic figure :g:`< Q B > <g1:< Q B >>` which is similar to :g:`< D > <g1:< D >>`. :just:`Q. E. F.`
