G.522
=====

.. rubric:: `XIII.15 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xiii/propositions/15/>`_

.. enunciation::

   Construct a cube and comprehend it in a sphere, like the pyramid; and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square on the side of the cube.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Book XIII, Proposition 15

.. step:: given

   the diameter :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` of the given sphere be set out

.. step:: construct

   Let it be cut at :g:`C <g1:C>` so that :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` is double of :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />`

.. step:: construct

   Let the semicircle :g:`( A D B ) <g1:( A D B )>` be described on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, let :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />` be drawn from :g:`C <g1:C>` at right angles to :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`

.. step:: construct

   **segment** :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />`

.. step:: construct

   Let the square :g:`< E F G H > <g1:< E F G H >>` having its side :eq:`equal <g1:/ E F / = g1:/ F G / = g1:/ G H / = g1:/ H E / = g1:/ D B />` to :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />` be set out, from :g:`E <g1:E>`, :g:`F <g1:F>`, :g:`G <g1:G>`, :g:`H <g1:H>` let :g:`/ E K / <g1:/ E K />`, :g:`/ F L / <g1:/ F L />`, :g:`/ G M / <g1:/ G M />`, :g:`/ H N / <g1:/ H N />` be drawn at right angles to the plane of the square :g:`< E F G H > <g1:< E F G H >>`, from :g:`/ E K / <g1:/ E K />`, :g:`/ F L / <g1:/ F L />`, :g:`/ G M / <g1:/ G M />`, :g:`/ H N / <g1:/ H N />` let :g:`/ E K / <g1:/ E K />`, :g:`/ F L / <g1:/ F L />`, :g:`/ G M / <g1:/ G M />`, :g:`/ H N / <g1:/ H N />` respectively be cut off :eq:`equal <g1:/ E K / = g1:/ F L / = g1:/ G M / = g1:/ H N / = g1:/ E F / = g1:/ F G / = g1:/ G H / = g1:/ H E />` to one of :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`, :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />`, :g:`/ G H / <g1:/ G H />`, :g:`/ H E / <g1:/ H E />`

.. step:: construct

   **segment** :g:`/ K L / <g1:/ K L />`, :g:`/ L M / <g1:/ L M />`, :g:`/ M N / <g1:/ M N />`, :g:`/ N K / <g1:/ N K />`

.. step:: assert

   Therefore the cube :g:`< F N > <g1:< F N >>` has been constructed which is contained by six equal squares.

.. step:: assert

   It is then required to comprehend it in the given sphere, and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square on the side of the cube.

.. step:: construct

   For let :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />`, :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />` be joined.

.. step:: assert [G.446] <G.446>

   Since :g:`< K E G ) <g1:< K E G )>` is right, because :g:`/ K E / <g1:/ K E />` is also at right angles to the plane :g:`< E F G H > <g1:< E F G H >>` and of course to :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />` also, therefore the semicircle described on :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />` will also pass through :g:`E <g1:E>`.

.. step:: assert

   Since :g:`/ G F / <g1:/ G F />` is at right angles to each of :g:`/ F L / <g1:/ F L />`, :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />`, :g:`/ G F / <g1:/ G F />` is also at right angles to the plane :g:`< E F L K > <g1:< E F L K >>`

.. step:: assert

   Hence also, if we join :g:`/ F K / <g1:/ F K />`, :g:`/ G F / <g1:/ G F />` will be at right angles to :g:`/ F K / <g1:/ F K />`

.. step:: assert

   And for this reason again the semicircle described on :g:`/ G K / <g1:/ G K />` will also pass through :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. step:: assert

   Similarly it will also pass through the remaining angular points of the cube.

.. step:: assert

   If then, :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />` remaining fixed, the semicircle be carried round and restored to the same position from which it began to be moved, the cube will be comprehended in a sphere.

.. step:: assert

   I say next that it is also comprehended in the given sphere.

.. step:: assert

   Since :g:`/ G F / <g1:/ G F />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ G F / = g1:/ F E />` to :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />`, and the angle at :g:`< E F G ) <g1:< E F G )>` is right, therefore the square on :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />` is double of the square on :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`.

.. step:: assert

   :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ E F / = g1:/ E K />` to :g:`/ E K / <g1:/ E K />`

.. step:: assert

   Therefore the square on :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />` is double of the square on :g:`/ E K / <g1:/ E K />`

.. step:: assert [G.60] <G.60>

   Hence the squares on :g:`/ G E / <g1:/ G E />`, :g:`/ E K / <g1:/ E K />`, that is the square on :g:`/ G K / <g1:/ G K />`, is triple of the square on :g:`/ E K / <g1:/ E K />`.

.. step:: assert

   And, since :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is triple of :g:`/ B C / <g1:/ B C />`, while, as :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`/ B C / <g1:/ B C />`, so is the square on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` to the square on :g:`/ B D / <g1:/ B D />`, therefore the square on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is triple of the square on :g:`/ B D / <g1:/ B D />`.

.. step:: assert

   The square on :g:`/ G K / <g1:/ G K />` was also proved triple of the square on :g:`/ K E / <g1:/ K E />`.

.. step:: assert

   :g:`/ K E / <g1:/ K E />` was made :eq:`equal <g1:/ K E / = g1:/ D B />` to :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />`

.. step:: assert

   Therefore :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />` is also :eq:`equal <g1:/ K G / = g1:/ A B />` to :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`.

.. step:: assert

   :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is the diameter of the given sphere

.. step:: assert

   Therefore :g:`/ K G / <g1:/ K G />` is also :eq:`equal <g1:/ K G / = g1:/ A B />` to the diameter of the given sphere.

.. step:: conclude

   Therefore the cube has been comprehended in the given sphere, and it has been demonstrated at the same time that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square on the side of the cube.
