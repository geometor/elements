G.189 ratios ratio — Heath
==========================

.. rubric:: `V.11 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_v/propositions/11/>`_

.. enunciation::

   :term:`Ratios <G.173>` which are the same with the :term:`same ratio <G.176>` are also the same with one another.

For, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`B <g1:B>`, so let :g:`C <g1:C>` be to :g:`D <g1:D>`, and, as :g:`C <g1:C>` is to :g:`D <g1:D>`, so let :g:`E <g1:E>` be to :g:`F <g1:F>`;

I say that, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`B <g1:B>`, so is :g:`E <g1:E>` to :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0164 Figure 1

For of :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`C <g1:C>`, :g:`E <g1:E>` let :term:`equimultiples <multiple>` :g:`G <g1:G>`, :g:`H <g1:H>`, :g:`K <g1:K>` be taken, and of :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`D <g1:D>`, :g:`F <g1:F>` other, chance, equimultiples :g:`L <g1:L>`, :g:`M <g1:M>`, :g:`N <g1:N>`.

Then since, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`B <g1:B>`, so is :g:`C <g1:C>` to :g:`D <g1:D>`, and of :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`C <g1:C>` equimultiples :g:`G <g1:G>`, :g:`H <g1:H>` have been taken, and of :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`D <g1:D>` other, chance, equimultiples :g:`L <g1:L>`, :g:`M <g1:M>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if :g:`G <g1:G>` is in excess of :g:`L <g1:L>`, :g:`H <g1:H>` is also in excess of :g:`M <g1:M>`, if :eq:`equal <g1:G = g1:L>`, :eq:`equal <g1:H = g1:M>`, and if less, less.

Again, since, as :g:`C <g1:C>` is to :g:`D <g1:D>`, so is :g:`E <g1:E>` to :g:`F <g1:F>`, and of :g:`C <g1:C>`, :g:`E <g1:E>` equimultiples :g:`H <g1:H>`, :g:`K <g1:K>` have been taken, and of :g:`D <g1:D>`, :g:`F <g1:F>` other, chance, equimultiples :g:`M <g1:M>`, :g:`N <g1:N>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if :g:`H <g1:H>` is in excess of :g:`M <g1:M>`, :g:`K <g1:K>` is also in excess of :g:`N <g1:N>`, if :eq:`equal <g1:H = g1:M>`, :eq:`equal <g1:K = g1:N>`, and if less, less.

But we saw that, if :g:`H <g1:H>` was in excess of :g:`M <g1:M>`, :g:`G <g1:G>` was also in excess of :g:`L <g1:L>`; if :eq:`equal <g1:H = g1:M>`, :eq:`equal <g1:G = g1:L>`; and if less, less;

.. assertion::

   so that, in addition, if :g:`G <g1:G>` is in excess of :g:`L <g1:L>`, :g:`K <g1:K>` is also in excess of :g:`N <g1:N>`, if :eq:`equal <g1:G = g1:L>`, :eq:`equal <g1:K = g1:N>`, and if less, less.

And :g:`G <g1:G>`, :g:`K <g1:K>` are equimultiples of :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`E <g1:E>`, while :g:`L <g1:L>`, :g:`N <g1:N>` are other, chance, equimultiples of :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`F <g1:F>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`B <g1:B>`, so is :g:`E <g1:E>` to :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.
