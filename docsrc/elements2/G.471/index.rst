G.471
=====

.. rubric:: `XI.26 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xi/propositions/26/>`_

.. enunciation::

   On a given straight line, and at a given point on it, to construct a solid angle equal to a given solid angle.

Let :g:`/ A B / <0331.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line, :g:`A <0331.g1:A>` the given point on it, and the angle at :g:`D <0331.g1:D>`, contained by the angles :g:`< E D C ) <0331.g1:< E D C )>`, :g:`< E D F ) <0331.g1:< E D F )>`, :g:`< F D C ) <0331.g1:< F D C )>`, the given solid angle ; thus it is required to construct on the straight line :g:`/ A B / <0331.g1:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`A <0331.g1:A>` on it, a solid angle equal to the solid angle at :g:`D <0331.g1:D>`.

.. picture:: 0331.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 26

For let a point :g:`F <0331.g1:F>` be taken at random on :g:`/ D F / <0331.g1:/ D F />`, let :g:`/ F G / <0331.g1:/ F G />` be drawn from :g:`F <0331.g1:F>` perpendicular to the plane through :g:`/ E D / <0331.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`/ D C / <0331.g1:/ D C />`, and let it meet the plane at :g:`G <0331.g1:G>`, :just:`[G.454] <G.454>` let :g:`/ D G / <0331.g1:/ D G />` be joined, let there be constructed on the straight line :g:`/ A B / <0331.g1:/ A B />` and at the point :g:`A <0331.g1:A>` on it the angle :g:`< B A L ) <0331.g1:< B A L )>` :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< B A L ) = 0331.g1:< E D C )>` to the angle :g:`< E D C ) <0331.g1:< E D C )>`, and the angle :g:`< B A K ) <0331.g1:< B A K )>` :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< B A K ) = 0331.g1:< E D G )>` to the angle :g:`< E D G ) <0331.g1:< E D G )>`, :just:`[G.34] <G.34>` let :g:`/ A K / <0331.g1:/ A K />` be made :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ A K / = 0331.g1:/ D G />` to :g:`/ D G / <0331.g1:/ D G />`,
let :g:`/ K H / <0331.g1:/ K H />` be set up from the point :g:`K <0331.g1:K>` at right angles to the plane through :g:`/ B A / <0331.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`/ A L / <0331.g1:/ A L />`, :just:`[G.455] <G.455>` let :g:`/ K H / <0331.g1:/ K H />` be made :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ K H / = 0331.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`/ G F / <0331.g1:/ G F />`, and let :g:`/ H A / <0331.g1:/ H A />` be joined ; I say that the solid angle at :g:`A <0331.g1:A>`, contained by the angles :g:`< B A L ) <0331.g1:< B A L )>`, :g:`< B A H ) <0331.g1:< B A H )>`, :g:`< H A L ) <0331.g1:< H A L )>` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< B A L ) = 0331.g1:< E D C ); 0331.g1:< B A H ) = 0331.g1:< E D F ); 0331.g1:< H A L ) = 0331.g1:< F D C )>` to the solid angle at :g:`D <0331.g1:D>` contained by the angles :g:`< E D C ) <0331.g1:< E D C )>`, :g:`< E D F ) <0331.g1:< E D F )>`, :g:`< F D C ) <0331.g1:< F D C )>`.

For let :g:`/ A B / <0331.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ D E / <0331.g1:/ D E />` be cut off :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ A B / = 0331.g1:/ D E />` to one another, and let :g:`/ H B / <0331.g1:/ H B />`, :g:`/ K B / <0331.g1:/ K B />`, :g:`/ F E / <0331.g1:/ F E />`, :g:`/ G E / <0331.g1:/ G E />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since :g:`/ F G / <0331.g1:/ F G />` is at right angles to the plane of reference, it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference ; :just:`[G.446] <G.446>`

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the angles :g:`< F G D ) <0331.g1:< F G D )>`, :g:`< F G E ) <0331.g1:< F G E )>` is :eq:`right <0331.g1:< F G D ) = 0331.g1:< F G E )>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason each of the angles :g:`< H K A ) <0331.g1:< H K A )>`, :g:`< H K B ) <0331.g1:< H K B )>` is also :eq:`right <0331.g1:< H K A ) = 0331.g1:< H K B )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the two sides :g:`/ K A / <0331.g1:/ K A />`, :g:`/ A B / <0331.g1:/ A B />` are :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ K A / = 0331.g1:/ G D /; 0331.g1:/ A B / = 0331.g1:/ D E />` to the two sides :g:`/ G D / <0331.g1:/ G D />`, :g:`/ D E / <0331.g1:/ D E />` respectively, and they contain equal angles,

.. assertion:: [G.11] <G.11>

   therefore the base :g:`/ K B / <0331.g1:/ K B />` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ K B / = 0331.g1:/ G E />` to the base :g:`/ G E / <0331.g1:/ G E />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`/ K H / <0331.g1:/ K H />` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ K H / = 0331.g1:/ G F />` to :g:`/ G F / <0331.g1:/ G F />`, and they contain right angles ;

.. assertion:: [G.11] <G.11>

   therefore :g:`/ H B / <0331.g1:/ H B />` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ H B / = 0331.g1:/ F E />` to :g:`/ F E / <0331.g1:/ F E />`.

.. subclause::

   Again, since the two sides :g:`/ A K / <0331.g1:/ A K />`, :g:`/ K H / <0331.g1:/ K H />` are :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ A K / = 0331.g1:/ D G /; 0331.g1:/ K H / = 0331.g1:/ G F />` to the two sides :g:`/ D G / <0331.g1:/ D G />`, :g:`/ G F / <0331.g1:/ G F />`, and they contain right angles,

.. assertion:: [G.11] <G.11>

   therefore the base :g:`/ A H / <0331.g1:/ A H />` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ A H / = 0331.g1:/ F D />` to the base :g:`/ F D / <0331.g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`/ A B / <0331.g1:/ A B />` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ A B / = 0331.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`/ D E / <0331.g1:/ D E />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the two sides :g:`/ H A / <0331.g1:/ H A />`, :g:`/ A B / <0331.g1:/ A B />` are :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ H A / = 0331.g1:/ D F /; 0331.g1:/ A B / = 0331.g1:/ D E />` to the two sides :g:`/ D F / <0331.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`/ D E / <0331.g1:/ D E />`.

.. subclause::

   And the base :g:`/ H B / <0331.g1:/ H B />` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:/ H B / = 0331.g1:/ F E />` to the base :g:`/ F E / <0331.g1:/ F E />` ;

.. assertion:: [G.15] <G.15>

   therefore the angle :g:`< B A H ) <0331.g1:< B A H )>` is :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< B A H ) = 0331.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle :g:`< E D F ) <0331.g1:< E D F )>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the angle :g:`< H A L ) <0331.g1:< H A L )>` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< H A L ) = 0331.g1:< F D C )>` to the angle :g:`< F D C ) <0331.g1:< F D C )>`.

.. subclause::

   And the angle :g:`< B A L ) <0331.g1:< B A L )>` is also :eq:`equal <0331.g1:< B A L ) = 0331.g1:< E D C )>` to the angle :g:`< E D C ) <0331.g1:< E D C )>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore on the straight line :g:`/ A B / <0331.g1:/ A B />`, and at the point :g:`A <0331.g1:A>` on it, a solid angle has been constructed equal to the given solid angle at :g:`D <0331.g1:D>`. :just:`Q. E. F.`
