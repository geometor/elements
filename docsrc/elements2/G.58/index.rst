G.58
====

.. rubric:: `I.45 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_i/propositions/45/>`_

.. enunciation::

   To construct, in a given rectilineal angle, a parallelogram equal to a given rectilineal figure.\ [#f1]_

Let :g:`< A B C D > <0359.g1:< A B C D >>` be the given rectilineal figure and :g:`< E ) <0359.g1:< E )>` the given rectilineal angle;

thus it is required to construct, in the given angle :g:`< E ) <0359.g1:< E )>`, a parallelogram :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< K F L M > = 0359.g1:< A B C D >>` to the rectilineal figure :g:`< A B C D > <0359.g1:< A B C D >>`.

.. picture:: 0359.g1.svg
   :alt: Folio 0359 Figure 1 - Proposition 45

Let :g:`/ D B / <0359.g1:/ D B />` be joined, and let the parallelogram :g:`< F K H G > <0359.g1:< F K H G >>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< F K H G > = 0359.g1:< A B D >>` to the triangle :g:`< A B D > <0359.g1:< A B D >>`, in the angle :g:`< H K F ) <0359.g1:< H K F )>` which is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< H K F ) = 0359.g1:< E )>` to :g:`< E ) <0359.g1:< E )>`; :just:`[G.55] <G.55>`

let the parallelogram :g:`< G H M L > <0359.g1:< G H M L >>` :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< G H M L > = 0359.g1:< D B C >>` to the triangle :g:`< D B C > <0359.g1:< D B C >>` be applied to the straight line :g:`/ G H / <0359.g1:/ G H />`, in the angle :g:`< G H M ) <0359.g1:< G H M )>` which is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< G H M ) = 0359.g1:< E )>` to :g:`< E ) <0359.g1:< E )>`. :just:`[G.57] <G.57>`

Then, since the angle :g:`< E ) <0359.g1:< E )>` is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< E ) = 0359.g1:< H K F ) = 0359.g1:< G H M )>` to each of the angles :g:`< H K F ) <0359.g1:< H K F )>`, :g:`< G H M ) <0359.g1:< G H M )>`,

.. assertion:: [G.1] <G.1>

   the angle :g:`< H K F ) <0359.g1:< H K F )>` is also :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< H K F ) = 0359.g1:< G H M )>` to the angle :g:`< G H M ) <0359.g1:< G H M )>`.

Let the angle :g:`< K H G ) <0359.g1:< K H G )>` be added to each; therefore the angles :g:`< F K H ) <0359.g1:< F K H )>`, :g:`< K H G ) <0359.g1:< K H G )>` are equal to the angles :g:`< K H G ) <0359.g1:< K H G )>`, :g:`< G H M ) <0359.g1:< G H M )>`.

But the angles :g:`< F K H ) <0359.g1:< F K H )>`, :g:`< K H G ) <0359.g1:< K H G )>` are equal to two right angles; :just:`[G.42] <G.42>`

therefore the angles :g:`< K H G ) <0359.g1:< K H G )>`, :g:`< G H M ) <0359.g1:< G H M )>` are also equal to two right angles.

Thus, with a straight line :g:`/ G H / <0359.g1:/ G H />`, and at the point :g:`H <0359.g1:H>` on it, two straight lines :g:`/ K H / <0359.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`/ H M / <0359.g1:/ H M />` not lying on the same side make the adjacent angles equal to two right angles;

.. assertion:: [G.24] <G.24>

   therefore :g:`/ K H / <0359.g1:/ K H />` is in a straight line with :g:`/ H M / <0359.g1:/ H M />`.

And, since the straight line :g:`/ H G / <0359.g1:/ H G />` falls upon the parallels :g:`/ K M / <0359.g1:/ K M />`, :g:`/ F G / <0359.g1:/ F G />`, the alternate angles :g:`< M H G ) <0359.g1:< M H G )>`, :g:`< H G F ) <0359.g1:< H G F )>` are :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< M H G ) = 0359.g1:< H G F )>` to one another. :just:`[G.42] <G.42>`

Let the angle :g:`< H G L ) <0359.g1:< H G L )>` be added to each; therefore the angles :g:`< M H G ) <0359.g1:< M H G )>`, :g:`< H G L ) <0359.g1:< H G L )>` are equal to the angles :g:`< H G F ) <0359.g1:< H G F )>`, :g:`< H G L ) <0359.g1:< H G L )>`. :just:`[G.21] <G.21>`

But the angles :g:`< M H G ) <0359.g1:< M H G )>`, :g:`< H G L ) <0359.g1:< H G L )>` are equal to two right angles; :just:`[G.42] <G.42>`

therefore the angles :g:`< H G F ) <0359.g1:< H G F )>`, :g:`< H G L ) <0359.g1:< H G L )>` are also equal to two right angles. :just:`[G.1] <G.1>`

.. assertion:: [G.24] <G.24>

   Therefore :g:`/ F G / <0359.g1:/ F G />` is in a straight line with :g:`/ G L / <0359.g1:/ G L />`.

And, since :g:`/ F K / <0359.g1:/ F K />` is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:/ F K / = 0359.g1:/ H G / = 0359.g1:/ M L />` and parallel to :g:`/ H G / <0359.g1:/ H G />`, :just:`[G.47] <G.47>`

and :g:`/ H G / <0359.g1:/ H G />` to :g:`/ M L / <0359.g1:/ M L />` also, :g:`/ K F / <0359.g1:/ K F />` is also :eq:`equal <0359.g1:/ K F / = 0359.g1:/ M L />` and parallel to :g:`/ M L / <0359.g1:/ M L />`; :just:`[G.1, G.43] <G.1, G.43>`

and the straight lines :g:`/ K M / <0359.g1:/ K M />`, :g:`/ F L / <0359.g1:/ F L />` join them (at their extremities); therefore :g:`/ K M / <0359.g1:/ K M />`, :g:`/ F L / <0359.g1:/ F L />` are also :eq:`equal <0359.g1:/ K M / = 0359.g1:/ F L />` and parallel. :just:`[G.46] <G.46>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`< K F L M > <0359.g1:< K F L M >>` is a parallelogram.

And, since the triangle :g:`< A B D > <0359.g1:< A B D >>` is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< A B D > = 0359.g1:< F K H G >; 0359.g1:< D B C > = 0359.g1:< G H M L >>` to the parallelogram :g:`< F K H G > <0359.g1:< F K H G >>`, and :g:`< D B C > <0359.g1:< D B C >>` to :g:`< G H M L > <0359.g1:< G H M L >>`,

.. assertion::

   the whole rectilineal figure :g:`< A B C D > <0359.g1:< A B C D >>` is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< A B C D > = 0359.g1:< K F L M >>` to the whole parallelogram :g:`< K F L M > <0359.g1:< K F L M >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the parallelogram :g:`< K F L M > <0359.g1:< K F L M >>` has been constructed :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< K F L M > = 0359.g1:< A B C D >>` to the given rectilineal figure :g:`< A B C D > <0359.g1:< A B C D >>`, in the angle :g:`< F K M ) <0359.g1:< F K M )>` which is :eq:`equal <0359.g1:< F K M ) = 0359.g1:< E )>` to the given angle :g:`< E ) <0359.g1:< E )>`. :just:`Q. E. F.`

.. [#f1] **rectilineal figure**, in the Greek “rectilineal” simply, without “figure,” εὐθύγραμμον being here used as a substantive, like the similarly formed :term:`παραλληλόγραμμον <parallelogrammon>`.
