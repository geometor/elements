G.519 — Heath
=============

.. rubric:: `XIII.12 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xiii/propositions/12/>`_

.. enunciation::

   *If an equilateral triangle be inscribed in a circle, the square on the side of the triangle is triple of the square on the radius of the circle.*

Let :g:`( A B C ) <g1:( A B C )>` be a circle, and let the :eq:`equilateral <g1:/ A B / = g1:/ B C / = g1:/ C A />` triangle :g:`< A B C > <g1:< A B C >>` be inscribed in it; I say that the square on one side of the triangle :g:`< A B C > <g1:< A B C >>` is triple of the square on the radius of the circle.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 12 Diagram

For let the centre :g:`D <g1:D>` of the circle :g:`( A B C ) <g1:( A B C )>` be taken, let :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />` be joined and carried through to :g:`E <g1:E>`, and let :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Then, since the triangle :g:`< A B C > <g1:< A B C >>` is :eq:`equilateral <g1:/ A B / = g1:/ B C / = g1:/ C A />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the circumference :g:`( B E C ) <g1:( B E C )>` is a third part of the circumference of the circle :g:`( A B C ) <g1:( A B C )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the circumference :g:`( B E ) <g1:( B E )>` is a sixth part of the circumference of the circle; therefore the straight line :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />` belongs to a hexagon; therefore it is :eq:`equal <g1:/ B E / = g1:/ D E />` to the radius :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`. :just:`[G.133] <G.133>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`/ A E / <g1:/ A E />` is double of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`,

.. assertion::

   the square on :g:`/ A E / <g1:/ A E />` is quadruple of the square on :g:`/ E D / <g1:/ E D />`, that is, of the square on :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`.

.. subclause::

   But the square on :g:`/ A E / <g1:/ A E />` is equal to the squares on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`; :just:`[G.110, G.60] <G.110, G.60>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />` are quadruple of the square on :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, :term:`*separando* <separando>`, the square on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is triple of the square on :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ B E / = g1:/ D E />` to :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is triple of the square on :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the square on the side of the triangle is triple of the square on the radius.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
