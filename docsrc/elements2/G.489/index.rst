G.489
=====

.. rubric:: `XII.3 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xii/propositions/03/>`_

.. enunciation::

   Any pyramid which has a triangular base is divided into two pyramids equal and similar to one another, similar to the whole and having triangular bases, and into two equal prisms; and the two prisms are greater than the half of the whole pyramid.

Let there be a pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< A B C > <0382.g1:< A B C >>` is the base and the point :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertex; I say that the pyramid :g:`< A B C D > <0382.g1:< A B C D >>` is divided into two pyramids equal to one another, having triangular bases and similar to the whole pyramid, and into two equal prisms; and the two prisms are greater than the half of the whole pyramid.

.. picture:: 0382.g1.svg
   :alt: Pyramid divided into two pyramids and two prisms

For let :g:`/ A B / <0382.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B C / <0382.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`/ C A / <0382.g1:/ C A />`, :g:`/ A D / <0382.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`/ D B / <0382.g1:/ D B />`, :g:`/ D C / <0382.g1:/ D C />` be :eq:`bisected <0382.g1:/ A E / = 0382.g1:/ E B /; 0382.g1:/ B F / = 0382.g1:/ F C /; 0382.g1:/ C G / = 0382.g1:/ G A /; 0382.g1:/ A H / = 0382.g1:/ H D /; 0382.g1:/ D K / = 0382.g1:/ K B /; 0382.g1:/ D L / = 0382.g1:/ L C />` at the points :g:`E <0382.g1:E>`, :g:`F <0382.g1:F>`, :g:`G <0382.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0382.g1:H>`, :g:`K <0382.g1:K>`, :g:`L <0382.g1:L>`, and let :g:`/ H E / <0382.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`/ E G / <0382.g1:/ E G />`, :g:`/ G H / <0382.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`/ H K / <0382.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`/ K L / <0382.g1:/ K L />`, :g:`/ L H / <0382.g1:/ L H />`, :g:`/ K F / <0382.g1:/ K F />`, :g:`/ F G / <0382.g1:/ F G />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`/ A E / <0382.g1:/ A E />` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ A E / = 0382.g1:/ E B /; 0382.g1:/ A H / = 0382.g1:/ D H />` to :g:`/ E B / <0382.g1:/ E B />`, and :g:`/ A H / <0382.g1:/ A H />` to :g:`/ D H / <0382.g1:/ D H />`,

.. assertion:: [G.164] <G.164>

   therefore :g:`/ E H / <0382.g1:/ E H />` is parallel to :g:`/ D B / <0382.g1:/ D B />`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason :g:`/ H K / <0382.g1:/ H K />` is also parallel to :g:`/ A B / <0382.g1:/ A B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`< H E B K > <0382.g1:< H E B K >>` is a parallelogram; therefore :g:`/ H K / <0382.g1:/ H K />` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ H K / = 0382.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`/ E B / <0382.g1:/ E B />`. :just:`[G.47] <G.47>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`/ E B / <0382.g1:/ E B />` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ E B / = 0382.g1:/ E A />` to :g:`/ E A / <0382.g1:/ E A />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ A E / <0382.g1:/ A E />` is also :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ A E / = 0382.g1:/ H K />` to :g:`/ H K / <0382.g1:/ H K />`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`/ A H / <0382.g1:/ A H />` is also :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ A H / = 0382.g1:/ H D />` to :g:`/ H D / <0382.g1:/ H D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the two sides :g:`/ E A / <0382.g1:/ E A />`, :g:`/ A H / <0382.g1:/ A H />` are :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ E A / = 0382.g1:/ K H /; 0382.g1:/ A H / = 0382.g1:/ H D />` to the two sides :g:`/ K H / <0382.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`/ H D / <0382.g1:/ H D />` respectively, and the angle :g:`< E A H ) <0382.g1:< E A H )>` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< E A H ) = 0382.g1:< K H D )>` to the angle :g:`< K H D ) <0382.g1:< K H D )>`; therefore the base :g:`/ E H / <0382.g1:/ E H />` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ E H / = 0382.g1:/ K D />` to the base :g:`/ K D / <0382.g1:/ K D />`. :just:`[G.11] <G.11>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`< A E H > <0382.g1:< A E H >>` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< A E H > = 0382.g1:< H K D >>` and similar to the triangle :g:`< H K D > <0382.g1:< H K D >>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the triangle :g:`< A H G > <0382.g1:< A H G >>` is also :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< A H G > = 0382.g1:< H L D >>` and similar to the triangle :g:`< H L D > <0382.g1:< H L D >>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since two straight lines :g:`/ E H / <0382.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`/ H G / <0382.g1:/ H G />` meeting one another are parallel to two straight lines :g:`/ K D / <0382.g1:/ K D />`, :g:`/ D L / <0382.g1:/ D L />` meeting one another, and are not in the same plane, they will contain equal angles. :just:`[G.453] <G.453>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`< E H G ) <0382.g1:< E H G )>` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< E H G ) = 0382.g1:< K D L )>` to the angle :g:`< K D L ) <0382.g1:< K D L )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the two straight lines :g:`/ E H / <0382.g1:/ E H />`, :g:`/ H G / <0382.g1:/ H G />` are :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ E H / = 0382.g1:/ K D /; 0382.g1:/ H G / = 0382.g1:/ D L />` to the two :g:`/ K D / <0382.g1:/ K D />`, :g:`/ D L / <0382.g1:/ D L />` respectively, and the angle :g:`< E H G ) <0382.g1:< E H G )>` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< E H G ) = 0382.g1:< K D L )>` to the angle :g:`< K D L ) <0382.g1:< K D L )>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`/ E G / <0382.g1:/ E G />` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ E G / = 0382.g1:/ K L />` to the base :g:`/ K L / <0382.g1:/ K L />`; :just:`[G.11] <G.11>` therefore the triangle :g:`< E H G > <0382.g1:< E H G >>` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< E H G > = 0382.g1:< K D L >>` and similar to the triangle :g:`< K D L > <0382.g1:< K D L >>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the triangle :g:`< A E G > <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is also :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< A E G > = 0382.g1:< H K L >>` and similar to the triangle :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< A E G > <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is the base and the point :g:`H <0382.g1:H>` the vertex is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< A E G H > = 0382.g1:< H K L D >>` and similar to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>` is the base and the point :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertex. :just:`[G.474] <G.474>`

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`/ H K / <0382.g1:/ H K />` has been drawn parallel to :g:`/ A B / <0382.g1:/ A B />`, one of the sides of the triangle :g:`< A D B > <0382.g1:< A D B >>`,

.. assertion::

   the triangle :g:`< A D B > <0382.g1:< A D B >>` is equiangular to the triangle :g:`< D H K > <0382.g1:< D H K >>`, :just:`[G.42] <G.42>` and they have their sides proportional; therefore the triangle :g:`< A D B > <0382.g1:< A D B >>` is similar to the triangle :g:`< D H K > <0382.g1:< D H K >>`. :just:`[G.170] <G.170>`

.. assertion::

   For the same reason the triangle :g:`< D B C > <0382.g1:< D B C >>` is also similar to the triangle :g:`< D K L > <0382.g1:< D K L >>`, and the triangle :g:`< A D C > <0382.g1:< A D C >>` to the triangle :g:`< D L H > <0382.g1:< D L H >>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two straight lines :g:`/ B A / <0382.g1:/ B A />`, :g:`/ A C / <0382.g1:/ A C />` meeting one another are parallel to the two straight lines :g:`/ K H / <0382.g1:/ K H />`, :g:`/ H L / <0382.g1:/ H L />` meeting one another, not in the same plane, they will contain equal angles. :just:`[G.453] <G.453>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the angle :g:`< B A C ) <0382.g1:< B A C )>` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< B A C ) = 0382.g1:< K H L )>` to the angle :g:`< K H L ) <0382.g1:< K H L )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`/ B A / <0382.g1:/ B A />` is to :g:`/ A C / <0382.g1:/ A C />`, so is :g:`/ K H / <0382.g1:/ K H />` to :g:`/ H L / <0382.g1:/ H L />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the triangle :g:`< A B C > <0382.g1:< A B C >>` is similar to the triangle :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< A B C > <0382.g1:< A B C >>` is the base and the point :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertex is similar to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>` is the base and the point :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertex.

.. subclause::

   But the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>` is the base and the point :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertex was proved similar to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< A E G > <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is the base and the point :g:`H <0382.g1:H>` the vertex.

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the pyramids :g:`< A E G H > <0382.g1:< A E G H >>`, :g:`< H K L D > <0382.g1:< H K L D >>` is similar to the whole pyramid :g:`< A B C D > <0382.g1:< A B C D >>`.

.. subclause::

   Next, since :g:`/ B F / <0382.g1:/ B F />` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:/ B F / = 0382.g1:/ F C />` to :g:`/ F C / <0382.g1:/ F C />`,

.. assertion::

   the parallelogram :g:`< E B F G > <0382.g1:< E B F G >>` is double of the triangle :g:`< G F C > <0382.g1:< G F C >>`.

.. subclause::

   And since, if there be two prisms of equal height, and one have a parallelogram as base, and the other a triangle, and if the parallelogram be double of the triangle, the prisms are equal, :just:`[G.486] <G.486>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the prism contained by the two triangles :g:`< B K F > <0382.g1:< B K F >>`, :g:`< E H G > <0382.g1:< E H G >>`, and the three parallelograms :g:`< E B F G > <0382.g1:< E B F G >>`, :g:`< E B K H > <0382.g1:< E B K H >>`, :g:`< H K F G > <0382.g1:< H K F G >>` is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< H G E K F B > = 0382.g1:< G C F H L K >>` to the prism contained by the two triangles :g:`< G F C > <0382.g1:< G F C >>`, :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>` and the three parallelograms :g:`< K F C L > <0382.g1:< K F C L >>`, :g:`< L C G H > <0382.g1:< L C G H >>`, :g:`< H K F G > <0382.g1:< H K F G >>`.

.. assertion::

   And it is manifest that each of the prisms, namely that in which the parallelogram :g:`< E B F G > <0382.g1:< E B F G >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`/ H K / <0382.g1:/ H K />` is its opposite, and that in which the triangle :g:`< G F C > <0382.g1:< G F C >>` is the base and the triangle :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>` its opposite, is greater than each of the pyramids of which the triangles :g:`< A E G > <0382.g1:< A E G >>`, :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>` are the bases and the points :g:`H <0382.g1:H>`, :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertices,

.. assertion::

   inasmuch as, if we join the straight lines :g:`/ E F / <0382.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`/ E K / <0382.g1:/ E K />`, the prism in which the parallelogram :g:`< E B F G > <0382.g1:< E B F G >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`/ H K / <0382.g1:/ H K />` its opposite is greater than the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< E B F > <0382.g1:< E B F >>` is the base and the point :g:`K <0382.g1:K>` the vertex.

.. subclause::

   But the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< E B F > <0382.g1:< E B F >>` is the base and the point :g:`K <0382.g1:K>` the vertex is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< K E F B > = 0382.g1:< A E G H >>` to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< A E G > <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is the base and the point :g:`H <0382.g1:H>` the vertex; for they are contained by equal and similar planes.

.. assertion::

   Hence also the prism in which the parallelogram :g:`< E B F G > <0382.g1:< E B F G >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`/ H K / <0382.g1:/ H K />` its opposite is greater than the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< A E G > <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is the base and the point :g:`H <0382.g1:H>` the vertex.

.. subclause::

   But the prism in which the parallelogram :g:`< E B F G > <0382.g1:< E B F G >>` is the base and the straight line :g:`/ H K / <0382.g1:/ H K />` its opposite is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< H G E K F B > = 0382.g1:< G C F H L K >>` to the prism in which the triangle :g:`< G F C > <0382.g1:< G F C >>` is the base and the triangle :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>` its opposite, and the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< A E G > <0382.g1:< A E G >>` is the base and the point :g:`H <0382.g1:H>` the vertex is :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< A E G H > = 0382.g1:< H K L D >>` to the pyramid of which the triangle :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>` is the base and the point :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertex.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the said two prisms are greater than the said two pyramids of which the triangles :g:`< A E G > <0382.g1:< A E G >>`, :g:`< H K L > <0382.g1:< H K L >>` are the bases and the points :g:`H <0382.g1:H>`, :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertices.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the whole pyramid, of which the triangle :g:`< A B C > <0382.g1:< A B C >>` is the base and the point :g:`D <0382.g1:D>` the vertex, has been divided into two pyramids :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< A E G H > = 0382.g1:< H K L D >>` to one another and into two :eq:`equal <0382.g1:< H G E K F B > = 0382.g1:< G C F H L K >>` prisms, and the two prisms are greater than the half of the whole pyramid.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.
