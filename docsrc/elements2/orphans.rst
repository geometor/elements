I.def.20

**Heath ID:** `I.def.20 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_i/definitions/#i-def-20>`_

Of trilateral figures, an :term:`**equilateral triangle** <equilateral-triangle>` is that which has its three sides equal, an :term:`**isosceles triangle** <isosceles-triangle>` that which has two of its sides alone equal, and a :term:`**scalene triangle** <scalene-triangle>` that which has its three sides unequal.

----

.. _I.def.21:

I.def.21

**Heath ID:** `I.def.21 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_i/definitions/#i-def-21>`_

Further, of trilateral figures, a :term:`**right-angled triangle** <right-angled-triangle>` is that which has a right angle, an :term:`**obtuse-angled triangle** <obtuse-angled-triangle>` that which has an obtuse angle, and an :term:`**acute-angled triangle** <acute-angled-triangle>` that which has its three angles acute.

----

.. _I.def.22:

I.def.22
--------

**Heath ID:** `I.def.22 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_i/definitions/#i-def-22>`_

Of quadrilateral figures, a :term:`**square** <square>` is that which is both equilateral and right-angled ; an :term:`**oblong** <oblong>` that which is right-angled but not equilateral ; a :term:`**rhombus** <rhombus>` that which is equilateral but not right-angled ; and a :term:`**rhomboid** <rhomboid>` that which has its opposite sides and angles equal to one another but is neither equilateral nor right-angled. And let quadrilaterals other than these be called :term:`**trapezia** <trapezium>`.
