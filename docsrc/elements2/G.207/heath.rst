G.207 magnitude ratio fifth — Heath
===================================

.. rubric:: `V.24 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_v/propositions/24/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If a first magnitude have to a second the :term:`same ratio <G.176>` as a third has to a fourth, and also a fifth have to the second the same ratio as a sixth to the fourth, the first and fifth added together will have to the second the same ratio as the third and sixth have to the fourth.

Let a first magnitude :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` have to a second :g:`C <g1:C>` the same ratio as a third :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` has to a fourth :g:`F <g1:F>`; and let also a fifth :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />` have to the second :g:`C <g1:C>` the same ratio as a sixth :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />` has to the fourth :g:`F <g1:F>`;

I say that the first and fifth added together, :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />`, will have to the second :g:`C <g1:C>` the same ratio as the third and sixth, :g:`/ D H / <g1:/ D H />`, has to the fourth :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Book V, Proposition 24, Line Segments

For since, as :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />` is to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so is :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />` to :g:`F <g1:F>`,

.. assertion::

   :term:`inversely <G.182>`, as :g:`C <g1:C>` is to :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />`, so is :g:`F <g1:F>` to :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`.

.. subclause::

   Since, then, as :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so is :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` to :g:`F <g1:F>`, and, as :g:`C <g1:C>` is to :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />`, so is :g:`F <g1:F>` to :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`,

.. assertion:: [G.205] <G.205>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <G.174>`, as :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />`, so is :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` to :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`.

And, since the magnitudes are :term:`proportional <G.180>` :term:`*separando* <G.197>`, they will also be proportional :term:`*componendo* <G.196>`; :just:`[G.199] <G.199>`

.. assertion::

   therefore, as :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` is to :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />`, so is :g:`/ D H / <g1:/ D H />` to :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`.

But also, as :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />` is to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so is :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />` to :g:`F <g1:F>`;

.. assertion:: [G.205] <G.205>

   therefore, :term:`*ex aequali* <G.174>`, as :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` is to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so is :g:`/ D H / <g1:/ D H />` to :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
