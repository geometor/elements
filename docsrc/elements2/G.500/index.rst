G.500
=====

.. rubric:: `XII.13 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xii/propositions/13/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If a cylinder be cut by a plane which is parallel to its opposite planes, then, as the cylinder is to the cylinder, so will the axis be to the axis.

For let the cylinder :g:`< A D > <0421.g1:< A D >>` be cut by the plane :g:`( G H ) <0421.g1:( G H )>` which is parallel to the opposite planes :g:`( A B ) <0421.g1:( A B )>`, :g:`( C D ) <0421.g1:( C D )>`, and let the plane :g:`( G H ) <0421.g1:( G H )>` meet the axis at the point :g:`K <0421.g1:K>`; I say that, as the cylinder :g:`< B G > <0421.g1:< B G >>` is to the cylinder :g:`< G D > <0421.g1:< G D >>`, so is the axis :g:`/ E K / <0421.g1:/ E K />` to the axis :g:`/ K F / <0421.g1:/ K F />`.

.. picture:: 0421.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 13

For let the axis :g:`/ E F / <0421.g1:/ E F />` be produced in both directions to the points :g:`L <0421.g1:L>`, :g:`M <0421.g1:M>`, and let there be set out any number whatever of axes :g:`/ E N / <0421.g1:/ E N />`, :g:`/ N L / <0421.g1:/ N L />` :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ E N / = 0421.g1:/ N L / = 0421.g1:/ E K />` to the axis :g:`/ E K / <0421.g1:/ E K />`, and any number whatever :g:`/ F O / <0421.g1:/ F O />`, :g:`/ O M / <0421.g1:/ O M />` :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ F O / = 0421.g1:/ O M / = 0421.g1:/ F K />` to :g:`/ F K / <0421.g1:/ F K />`; and let the cylinder :g:`< P W > <0421.g1:< P W >>` on the axis :g:`/ L M / <0421.g1:/ L M />` be conceived of which the circles :g:`( P Q ) <0421.g1:( P Q )>`, :g:`( V W ) <0421.g1:( V W )>` are the bases.

Let planes be carried through the points :g:`N <0421.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0421.g1:O>` parallel to :g:`( A B ) <0421.g1:( A B )>`, :g:`( C D ) <0421.g1:( C D )>` and to the bases of the cylinder :g:`< P W > <0421.g1:< P W >>`, and let them produce the circles :g:`( R S ) <0421.g1:( R S )>`, :g:`( T U ) <0421.g1:( T U )>` about the centres :g:`N <0421.g1:N>`, :g:`O <0421.g1:O>`.

.. subclause::

   Then, since the axes :g:`/ L N / <0421.g1:/ L N />`, :g:`/ N E / <0421.g1:/ N E />`, :g:`/ E K / <0421.g1:/ E K />` are :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ L N / = 0421.g1:/ N E / = 0421.g1:/ E K />` to one another,

.. assertion::

   therefore the cylinders :g:`< Q R > <0421.g1:< Q R >>`, :g:`< R B > <0421.g1:< R B >>`, :g:`< B G > <0421.g1:< B G >>` are to one another as their bases. :just:`[G.497] <G.497>`

.. subclause::

   But the bases are :eq:`equal <0421.g1:( P Q ) = 0421.g1:( R S ) = 0421.g1:( A B ) = 0421.g1:( G H )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the cylinders :g:`< Q R > <0421.g1:< Q R >>`, :g:`< R B > <0421.g1:< R B >>`, :g:`< B G > <0421.g1:< B G >>` are also :eq:`equal <0421.g1:< Q R > = 0421.g1:< R B > = 0421.g1:< B G >>` to one another.

.. subclause::

   Since then the axes :g:`/ L N / <0421.g1:/ L N />`, :g:`/ N E / <0421.g1:/ N E />`, :g:`/ E K / <0421.g1:/ E K />` are :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ L N / = 0421.g1:/ N E / = 0421.g1:/ E K />` to one another, and the cylinders :g:`< Q R > <0421.g1:< Q R >>`, :g:`< R B > <0421.g1:< R B >>`, :g:`< B G > <0421.g1:< B G >>` are also :eq:`equal <0421.g1:< Q R > = 0421.g1:< R B > = 0421.g1:< B G >>` to one another, and the multitude of the former is equal to the multitude of the latter,

.. assertion::

   therefore, whatever multiple the axis :g:`/ K L / <0421.g1:/ K L />` is of the axis :g:`/ E K / <0421.g1:/ E K />`, the same multiple also will the cylinder :g:`< Q G > <0421.g1:< Q G >>` be of the cylinder :g:`< G B > <0421.g1:< G B >>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason, whatever multiple the axis :g:`/ M K / <0421.g1:/ M K />` is of the axis :g:`/ K F / <0421.g1:/ K F />`, the same multiple also is the cylinder :g:`< W G > <0421.g1:< W G >>` of the cylinder :g:`< G D > <0421.g1:< G D >>`.

.. assertion::

   And, if the axis :g:`/ K L / <0421.g1:/ K L />` is :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ K L / = 0421.g1:/ K M />` to the axis :g:`/ K M / <0421.g1:/ K M />`, the cylinder :g:`< Q G > <0421.g1:< Q G >>` will also be :eq:`equal <0421.g1:< Q G > = 0421.g1:< G W >>` to the cylinder :g:`< G W > <0421.g1:< G W >>`, if the axis is greater than the axis, the cylinder will also be greater than the cylinder, and if less, less.

.. subclause::

   Thus, there being four magnitudes, the axes :g:`/ E K / <0421.g1:/ E K />`, :g:`/ K F / <0421.g1:/ K F />` and the cylinders :g:`< B G > <0421.g1:< B G >>`, :g:`< G D > <0421.g1:< G D >>`, there have been taken equimultiples of the axis :g:`/ E K / <0421.g1:/ E K />` and of the cylinder :g:`< B G > <0421.g1:< B G >>`, namely the axis :g:`/ L K / <0421.g1:/ L K />` and the cylinder :g:`< Q G > <0421.g1:< Q G >>`, and equimultiples of the axis :g:`/ K F / <0421.g1:/ K F />` and of the cylinder :g:`< G D > <0421.g1:< G D >>`, namely the axis :g:`/ K M / <0421.g1:/ K M />` and the cylinder :g:`< G W > <0421.g1:< G W >>`; and it has been proved that, if the axis :g:`/ K L / <0421.g1:/ K L />` is in excess of the axis :g:`/ K M / <0421.g1:/ K M />`, the cylinder :g:`< Q G > <0421.g1:< Q G >>` is also in excess of the cylinder :g:`< G W > <0421.g1:< G W >>`, if :eq:`equal <0421.g1:/ K L / = 0421.g1:/ K M />`, :eq:`equal <0421.g1:< Q G > = 0421.g1:< G W >>`, and if less, less.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, as the axis :g:`/ E K / <0421.g1:/ E K />` is to the axis :g:`/ K F / <0421.g1:/ K F />`, so is the cylinder :g:`< B G > <0421.g1:< B G >>` to the cylinder :g:`< G D > <0421.g1:< G D >>`. :just:`[G.138] <G.138>`

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
