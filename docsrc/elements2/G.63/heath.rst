G.63 — Heath
============

.. rubric:: `II.2 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_ii/propositions/02/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If a straight line be cut at random, the rectangle contained by the whole and both of the segments is equal to the square on the whole.

For let the straight line :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` be cut at random at the point :g:`C <g1:C>`;

I say that the rectangle contained by :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B C / <g1:/ B C />` together with the rectangle contained by :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` is equal to the square on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram

For let the square :g:`< A D E B > <g1:< A D E B >>` be described on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` :just:`[G.59] <G.59>`, and let :g:`/ C F / <g1:/ C F />` be drawn through :g:`C <g1:C>` parallel to either :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />` or :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`. :just:`[G.44] <G.44>`

.. assertion::

   Then :g:`< A E > <g1:< A E >>` is equal to :g:`< A F > <g1:< A F >>`, :g:`< C E > <g1:< C E >>`.

Now :g:`< A E > <g1:< A E >>` is the square on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`;

.. subclause::

   :g:`< A F > <g1:< A F >>` is the rectangle contained by :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`, for it is contained by :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />`, :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`, and :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ A D / = g1:/ A B />` to :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`;

.. subclause::

   and :g:`< C E > <g1:< C E >>` is the rectangle :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B C / <g1:/ B C />`, for :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ B E / = g1:/ A B />` to :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the rectangle :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` together with the rectangle :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B C / <g1:/ B C />` is equal to the square on :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
