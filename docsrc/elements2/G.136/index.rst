G.136
=====

.. rubric:: `V.2 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_v/propositions/02/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If a first magnitude be the same multiple of a second that a third is of a fourth, and a fifth also be the same multiple of the second that a sixth is of the fourth, the sum of the first and fifth is the same multiple of the second that the sum of the third and sixth is of the fourth.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 2 diagram

.. step:: given

   a first magnitude, :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, be the same multiple of a second, :g:`C <g1:C>`, that a third, :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, is of a fourth, :g:`F <g1:F>`

.. step:: assert

   Let a fifth, :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />`, also be the same multiple of the second, :g:`C <g1:C>`, that a sixth, :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`, is of the fourth :g:`F <g1:F>`

.. step:: assert

   Since :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` is the same multiple of :g:`C <g1:C>` that :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` is of :g:`F <g1:F>` → therefore, as many magnitudes as there are in :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />` equal to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so many also are there in :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` equal to :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. step:: assert

   For the same reason also,

.. step:: assert

   As many as there are in :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />` equal to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so many are there also in :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />` equal to :g:`F <g1:F>`;

.. step:: assert

   Therefore, as many as there are in the whole :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` equal to :g:`C <g1:C>`, so many also are there in the whole :g:`/ D H / <g1:/ D H />` equal to :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. step:: assert

   Therefore, whatever multiple :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` is of :g:`C <g1:C>`, that multiple also is :g:`/ D H / <g1:/ D H />` of :g:`F <g1:F>`.

.. step:: conclude

   Therefore the sum of the first and fifth, :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />`, is the same multiple of the second, :g:`C <g1:C>`, that the sum of the third and sixth, :g:`/ D H / <g1:/ D H />`, is of the fourth, :g:`F <g1:F>`.
