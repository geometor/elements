G.309 square numbers there — Heath
==================================

.. rubric:: `VIII.11 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_viii/propositions/11/>`_

.. enunciation::

   Between two :term:`square numbers <G.308>` there is one mean :term:`proportional <G.180>` :term:`number <G.248>`, and the :term:`square <G.63>` has to the square the :term:`ratio <G.173>` duplicate of that which the side has to the side.

Let :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>` be square numbers, and let :g:`C <g1:C>` be the side of :g:`A <g1:A>`, and :g:`D <g1:D>` of :g:`B <g1:B>`;

I say that between :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>` there is one mean proportional number, and :g:`A <g1:A>` has to :g:`B <g1:B>` the ratio duplicate of that which :g:`C <g1:C>` has to :g:`D <g1:D>`.

For let :g:`C <g1:C>` by :term:`multiplying <G.269>` :g:`D <g1:D>` make :g:`E <g1:E>`.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Book VIII, Proposition 11

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`A <g1:A>` is a square and :g:`C <g1:C>` is its side,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`C <g1:C>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`A <g1:A>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also

.. assertion::

   :g:`D <g1:D>` by multiplying itself has made :g:`B <g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`C <g1:C>` by multiplying the numbers :g:`C <g1:C>`, :g:`D <g1:D>` has made :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`E <g1:E>` respectively,

.. assertion:: [G.271] <G.271>

   therefore, as :g:`C <g1:C>` is to :g:`D <g1:D>`, so is :g:`A <g1:A>` to :g:`E <g1:E>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also,

.. assertion:: [G.272] <G.272>

   as :g:`C <g1:C>` is to :g:`D <g1:D>`, so is :g:`E <g1:E>` to :g:`B <g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`E <g1:E>`, so is :g:`E <g1:E>` to :g:`B <g1:B>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore between :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>` there is one mean proportional number.

I say next that :g:`A <g1:A>` also has to :g:`B <g1:B>` the ratio duplicate of that which :g:`C <g1:C>` has to :g:`D <g1:D>`.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`E <g1:E>`, :g:`B <g1:B>` are three numbers in proportion,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`A <g1:A>` has to :g:`B <g1:B>` the ratio duplicate of that which :g:`A <g1:A>` has to :g:`E <g1:E>`. :just:`[G.230] <G.230>`

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`A <g1:A>` is to :g:`E <g1:E>`, so is :g:`C <g1:C>` to :g:`D <g1:D>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`A <g1:A>` has to :g:`B <g1:B>` the ratio duplicate of that which the side :g:`C <g1:C>` has to :g:`D <g1:D>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
