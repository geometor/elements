G.51¶

I.38

Triangles which are on equal bases and in the same parallels are equal to one another.

Let < A B C >, < D E F > be triangles on equal bases / B C /, / E F / and in the same parallels / B F /, / A D /;

I say that the triangle < A B C > is equal to the triangle < D E F >.

For let [ A D ] be produced in both directions to G, H;

through B let / B G / be drawn parallel to / C A /, [G.44]

and through F let / F H / be drawn parallel to / D E /.

Then each of the figures < G B C A >, < D E F H > is a parallelogram; and < G B C A > is equal to < D E F H >;

for they are on equal bases / B C /, / E F / and in the same parallels / B F /, / G H /. [G.49]

Moreover the triangle < A B C > is half of the parallelogram < G B C A >; for the diameter / A B / bisects it. [G.47]

And the triangle < F E D > is half of the parallelogram < D E F H >; for the diameter / D F / bisects it. [G.47]

[But the halves of equal things are equal to one another.]

Therefore the triangle < A B C > is equal to the triangle < D E F >.
Therefore etc. Q. E. D.
