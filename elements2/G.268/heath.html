G.268 — Heath¶

VIII.26

Similar plane numbers have to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

Let A, B be similar plane numbers;

I say that A has to B the ratio which a square number has to a square number.

For, since A, B are similar plane numbers,
[G.260]therefore one mean proportional number falls between A, B.

Let it so fall, and let it be C; and let D, E, F, the least numbers of those which have the same ratio with A, C, B, be taken; [G.234, G.242]

[G.242]therefore the extremes of them D, F are square.
And since, as D is to F, so is A to B, and D, F are square,
therefore A has to B the ratio which a square number has to a square number.
Q. E. D.
