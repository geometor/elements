G.436¶

X.109

If from a medial area a rational area be subtracted, there arise two other irrational straight lines, either a first apotome of a medial straight line or a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole.
medial_area_rational()
prove the “side” of the remainder < E C > becomes one of two irrational straight lines, either a first apotome of a medial straight line or a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole
construct From the medial area < B C > let the rational area < B D > be subtracted.
assert Let a rational straight line / F G / be set out, and let the areas be similarly applied.
assert It follows then that / F H / is rational and incommensurable in length with / F G /, while / K F / is rational and commensurable in length with / F G /;
∵ magnitudes_commensurable_incommensurable, rational_straight_lineassert Therefore / F H /, / F K / are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore / K H / is an apotome, and / F K / the annex to it.
assert Now the square on / H F / is greater than the square on / F K / either by the square on a straight line commensurable with / H F / or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.
assert If then the square on / H F / is greater than the square on / F K / by the square on a straight line commensurable with / H F /, while the annex / F K / is commensurable in length with the rational straight line / F G / set out,
∵ annex_commensurable_length_defassert / K H / is a second apotome.
assert / F G / is rational;
∵ area_contained_rational_x_92assert So that the “side” of < L H >, that is, of < E C >, is a first apotome of a medial straight line.
assert But, if the square on / H F / is greater than the square on / F K / by the square on a straight line incommensurable with / H F /, while the annex / F K / is commensurable in length with the rational straight line / F G / set out,
∵ annex_commensurable_fifth_def, area_contained_rational_x_95assert / K H / is a fifth apotome; so that the “side” of < E C > is a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole.
assert In this case we have to classify
assert $$\sqrt{\sqrt{k} \cdot \rho^2 - \lambda\rho^2}.$$
assert Suppose that
assert $$\sigma u = \sqrt{k} \cdot \rho^2,$$
assert $$\sigma v = \lambda\rho^2.$$
assert Thus, $\sigma u$ being medial and $\sigma v$ rational, $u$ is rational and $\smile \sigma$, while $v$ is rational and $\mathbin{\Cap} \sigma$.
assert Thus, as before, $u, v$ are rational and $\sim$, so that $(u - v)$ is an apotome.
assert Now either
assert

  1. $\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \mathbin{\Cap} u$, or (2) $\sqrt{u^2 - v^2} \smile u$, while in both cases $v$ is commensurable with $\sigma$.

∵ area_contained_rational_x_92, area_contained_rational_x_95conclude Therefore $(u - v)$ is either (1) a second apotome, or (2) a fifth apotome, and hence in case (1) $\sqrt{\sigma (u - v)}$ is the first apotome of a medial straight line, and in case (2) $\sqrt{\sigma (u - v)}$ is the “side” of a medial, minus a rational, area.
