G.513 elevated point draw¶

XI.11

From a given elevated point to draw a segment perpendicular to a given plane.
elevated_point_draw(A) → / A F /
given A be the given elevated point, the plane of reference the given plane
construct from the given elevated point A / A F / has been drawn perpendicular to the plane of reference
given A be the given elevated point, the plane of reference the given plane
construct Let any segment / B C / be drawn
construct perpendicular_from: At random, in the plane of reference, and let / A D / be drawn from A perpendicular to / B C /.
assert If then / A D / is also perpendicular to the plane of reference, that which was enjoined will have been done.
construct perpendicular_at: But, if not, let / D E / be drawn from D at right angles to / B C / and in the plane of reference, let / A F / be drawn from A perpendicular to / D E /, and let / G H / be drawn through F parallel to / B C /.
∵ segment_set_rightassert Since / B C / is at right angles to each of / D A /, / D E / → therefore / B C / is also at right angles to the plane through / E D /, / D A /.
assert / G H / is parallel to it;
∵ segments_parallel_rightassert But, if two segments be parallel, and one of them be at right angles to any plane, the remaining one will also be at right angles to the same plane
assert Therefore / G H / is also at right angles to the plane through / E D /, / D A /.
∵ segment_right_angles_defassert Therefore / G H / is also at right angles to all the segments which meet it and are in the plane through / E D /, / D A /.
assert / A F / meets it and is in the plane through / E D /, / D A /;
assert Therefore / G H / is at right angles to / F A /, so that / F A / is also at right angles to / G H /.
assert / A F / is also at right angles to / D E /;
assert Therefore / A F / is at right angles to each of / G H /, / D E /.
∵ segment_set_rightassert But, if a segment be set up at right angles to two segments which cut one another, at the point of section, it will also be at right angles to the plane through them
assert Therefore / F A / is at right angles to the plane through / E D /, / G H /.
assert The plane through / E D /, / G H / is the plane of reference;
assert Therefore / A F / is at right angles to the plane of reference.
conclude Therefore from the given elevated point A / A F / has been drawn perpendicular to the plane of reference.
