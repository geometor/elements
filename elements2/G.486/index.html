G.486¶

XI.39

If there be two prisms of equal height, and one have a parallelogram as base and the other a triangle, and if the parallelogram be double of the triangle, the prisms is equal.
given < A B C D E F >, < G H K L M N > be two prisms of equal height, let one have the parallelogram < A F > as base, the other < G H K >
assert Let the parallelogram < A F > be double of < G H K >
assert The prism < A B C D E F > is equal to the prism < G H K L M N >.
assert For let the solids < A O >, < G P > be completed.
[G.47]assert Since the parallelogram < A F > is double of < G H K >, while the parallelogram < H K > is also double of < G H K >.
assert Therefore the parallelogram < A F > is equal to the parallelogram < H K >.
[G.478]assert Parallelepipedal solids which are on equal bases and of the same height are equal to one another .
assert Therefore the solid < A O > is equal to the solid < G P >.
[G.475]assert The prism < A B C D E F > is half of the solid < A O >, and the prism < G H K L M N > is half of the solid < G P > .
conclude Therefore the prism < A B C D E F > is equal to the prism < G H K L M N >.
