G.586 equilateral equiangular pentagon¶

XIII.8

If in an equilateral and equiangular pentagon segments subtend two angles taken in order, they cut one another in extreme and mean ratio, and their greater segments are equal to the side of the pentagon.
equilateral_equiangular_pentagon()
prove each of them has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at H, and their greater segments are equal to the side of the pentagon
assert In the equilateral and equiangular pentagon < A B C D E > let / A C /, / B E /, cutting one another at H, subtend two angles taken in order, the angles at A, B
construct circumscribe_circle_about_pentagon: Let ( A B C D E ) be circumscribed about the pentagon < A B C D E >.
assert / E A /, / A B / = the two / A B /, / B C /, and they contain equal angles,
∵ sas_congruenceassert / B E / = / A C /, < A B E > is equal to < A B C >, and the remaining angles will be equal to the remaining angles, namely those which the equal sides subtend
∵ triangle_angle_sumassert < B A C ) = < A B E ); therefore < A H E ) is double of < B A H )
∵ equal_chords_equal_arcs, equal_circles_anglesassert < E A C ) is also double of < B A C ), inasmuch as the arc ( E D C ) is also double of the arc ( C B )
∵ equal_angles_isoscelesassert < H A E ) = < A H E ); hence / H E / = / E A /, that is, to / A B /
∵ isosceles_base_anglesassert since / B A / = / A E /, < A B E ) = < A E B )
assert < A B E ) = < B A H )
assert < B E A ) = < B A H )
assert < A B E ) is common to the two triangles < A B E > and < A B H >
∵ triangle_angle_sum, equiangular_triangles_sidesassert < B A E ) = < A H B ); therefore < A B E > is equiangular with < A B H >; therefore, proportionally, as / E B / is to / B A /, so is / A B / to / B H /
assert / B A / = / E H /
assert as / B E / is to / E H /, so is / E H / to / H B /
assert / B E / > / E H /
∵ magnitude_ratio_greater_v_14assert / E H / is also greater than / H B /
assert / B E / has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at H, and the greater segment / H E / = the side of the pentagon
∴ Similarly we can prove that / A C / has also been cut in extreme and mean ratio at H, and its greater segment / C H / = the side of the pentagon
