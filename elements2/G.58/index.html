G.58¶

I.45

To construct, in a given rectilineal angle, a parallelogram equal to a given rectilineal figure.[1]

Let < A B C D > be the given rectilineal figure and < E ) the given rectilineal angle;

thus it is required to construct, in the given angle < E ), a parallelogram equal to the rectilineal figure < A B C D >.

Let / D B / be joined, and let the parallelogram < F K H G > be constructed equal to the triangle < A B D >, in the angle < H K F ) which is equal to < E ); [G.55]

let the parallelogram < G H M L > equal to the triangle < D B C > be applied to the straight line / G H /, in the angle < G H M ) which is equal to < E ). [G.57]

Then, since the angle < E ) is equal to each of the angles < H K F ), < G H M ),

[G.1]the angle < H K F ) is also equal to the angle < G H M ).

Let the angle < K H G ) be added to each; therefore the angles < F K H ), < K H G ) are equal to the angles < K H G ), < G H M ).

But the angles < F K H ), < K H G ) are equal to two right angles; [G.42]

therefore the angles < K H G ), < G H M ) are also equal to two right angles.

Thus, with a straight line / G H /, and at the point H on it, two straight lines / K H /, / H M / not lying on the same side make the adjacent angles equal to two right angles;

[G.24]therefore / K H / is in a straight line with / H M /.

And, since the straight line / H G / falls upon the parallels / K M /, / F G /, the alternate angles < M H G ), < H G F ) are equal to one another. [G.42]

Let the angle < H G L ) be added to each; therefore the angles < M H G ), < H G L ) are equal to the angles < H G F ), < H G L ). [G.21]

But the angles < M H G ), < H G L ) are equal to two right angles; [G.42]

therefore the angles < H G F ), < H G L ) are also equal to two right angles. [G.1]

[G.24]Therefore / F G / is in a straight line with / G L /.

And, since / F K / is equal and parallel to / H G /, [G.47]

and / H G / to / M L / also, / K F / is also equal and parallel to / M L /; [G.1, G.43]

and the straight lines / K M /, / F L / join them (at their extremities); therefore / K M /, / F L / are also equal and parallel. [G.46]

Therefore < K F L M > is a parallelogram.

And, since the triangle < A B D > is equal to the parallelogram < F K H G >, and < D B C > to < G H M L >,

the whole rectilineal figure < A B C D > is equal to the whole parallelogram < K F L M >.
Therefore the parallelogram < K F L M > has been constructed equal to the given rectilineal figure < A B C D >, in the angle < F K M ) which is equal to the given angle < E ). Q. E. F.
[1]

rectilineal figure, in the Greek “rectilineal” simply, without “figure,” εὐθύγραμμον being here used as a substantive, like the similarly formed παραλληλόγραμμον.
