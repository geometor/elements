G.58¶

I.45

Construct, in a given angle, a parallelogram equal in area to a given polygon.
parallelogram_from_polygon(< A B C D >, < E )) → < K F L M >
given < A B C D > be the given rectilineal figure and < E )
construct construct, in the given angle < E ), a parallelogram equal to the rectilineal figure < A B C D >
given < A B C D > be the given rectilineal figure and < E )
construct segment(D, B) → / D B /
construct parallelogram_from_triangle(< A B D >, < E )) → < F K H G > (equal, < H K F ) equal)
construct apply_parallelogram_on_segment(/ G H /, < D B C >, < E )) → < G H M L > (equal, < G H M ) equal)
∵ equal_transitiveassert Since < E ) is equal to each of < H K F ), < G H M ) → < H K F ) is also equal to < G H M ).
construct Let < K H G ) be added to each
assert Therefore < F K H ), < K H G ) are equal to < K H G ), < G H M ).
∵ parallel_transversal_anglesassert < F K H ), < K H G ) are equal to two right angles
assert Therefore < K H G ), < G H M ) are also equal to two right angles.
assert Thus, with a straight line / G H /, and at H on it, two straight lines / K H /, / H M / not lying on the same side make the adjacent angles equal to two right angles
∵ supplementary_angles_collinearassert Therefore / K H / is in a straight line with / H M /.
∵ parallel_transversal_anglesassert And, since / H G / falls upon the parallels / K M /, / F G /, the alternate angles < M H G ), < H G F ) are equal to one another.
construct Let < H G L ) be added to each
∵ add_equalsassert Therefore < M H G ), < H G L ) are equal to < H G F ), < H G L ).
∵ parallel_transversal_anglesassert < M H G ), < H G L ) are equal to two right angles
∵ equal_transitiveassert Therefore < H G F ), < H G L ) are also equal to two right angles.
∵ supplementary_angles_collinearassert Therefore / F G / is in a straight line with / G L /.
∵ parallelogram_propertiesassert And, since / F K / is equal and parallel to / H G /.
∵ equal_transitive, parallel_transitiveassert And / H G / to / M L / also, / K F / is also equal and parallel to / M L /
assert And / K M /, / F L / join them (at their extremities)
∵ join_equal_parallelsassert Therefore / K M /, / F L / are also equal and parallel.
assert Therefore < K F L M > is a parallelogram.
assert And, since < A B D > is equal to the parallelogram < F K H G >, and < D B C > to < G H M L >,
conclude The whole rectilineal figure < A B C D > is equal to the whole parallelogram < K F L M >.
