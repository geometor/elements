G.538 parallelepipedal solid cut¶

XI.28

If a parallelepipedal solid be cut by a plane through the diagonals of the opposite planes, the solid is bisected by the plane.
parallelepipedal_solid_cut_xi_28()
prove the solid < A B > will be bisected by the plane < C D E F >
construct Let the parallelepipedal solid < A B > be cut by the plane < C D E F > through the diagonals / C F /, / D E / of opposite planes
∵ parallelogram_propertiesassert < C G F > is equal to < C F B >, and < A D E > to < D E H >, while the parallelogram < C A > is also equal to the parallelogram < E B >, for they are opposite, and < G E > to < C H >
assert the prism contained by the two triangles < C G F >, < A D E > and the three parallelograms < G E >, < A C >, < C E > is also equal to the prism contained by the two triangles < C F B >, < D E H > and the three parallelograms < C H >, < B E >, < C E >
∵ equal_similar_solid_defassert For they are contained by planes equal both in multitude and in magnitude
∵ Q. E. D.∴ the whole solid < A B > is bisected by the plane < C D E F >
