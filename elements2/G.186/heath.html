G.186 — Heath¶

VI.21

Figures which are similar to the same rectilineal figure are also similar to one another.

For let each of the rectilineal figures A, B be similar to C;

I say that A is also similar to B.

For, since A is similar to C,
it is equiangular with it and has the sides about the equal angles proportional. [G.170]
Again, since B is similar to C,
it is equiangular with it and has the sides about the equal angles proportional.
Therefore each of the figures A, B is equiangular with C and with C has the sides about the equal angles proportional;
therefore A is similar to B.
Q. E. D.
