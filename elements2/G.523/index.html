XIII.16

Construct an icosahedron and comprehend it in a sphere, like the aforesaid figures; and to prove that the side of the icosahedron is the irrational straight line called minor.
construct_icosahedron_comprehend() → A'
construct Porism. From this it is manifest that the square on the diameter of the sphere is five times the square on the radius of the circle from which the icosahedron has been described, and that the diameter of the sphere is composed of the side of the hexagon and two of the sides of the decagon inscribed in the same circle
construct Let the diameter / A B / of the given sphere be set out, and let it be cut at C so that / A C / is quadruple of / C B /
construct Let the semicircle ( A D B ) be described on / A B /
construct perpendicular_at(/ A B /, C) → / C D /
construct segment(D, B) → / D B /
construct segment() → ( E F G H K ), segment(D, B) → / D B /, segment() → < E F G H K >, segment() → ( E F G H K ), segment() → ( E F ), segment() → ( F G ), segment() → ( G H ), segment() → ( H K ), segment() → ( K E ), segment() → L, segment() → M, segment() → N, segment() → O, segment() → P, segment(L, M) → / L M /, segment(M, N) → / M N /, segment(N, O) → / N O /, segment(O, P) → / O P /, segment(P, L) → / P L /, segment(E, P) → / E P /
assert Therefore the pentagon < L M N O P > is also equilateral, and / E P / belongs to a decagon.
construct Now from E, F, G, H, K let / E Q /, / F R /, / G S /, / H T /, / K U / be set up at right angles to the plane of the circle, and let them be equal to the radius of ( E F G H K ), let / Q R /, / R S /, / S T /, / T U /, / U Q /, / Q L /, / L R /, / R M /, / M S /, / S N /, / N T /, / T O /, / O U /, / U P /, / P Q / be joined.
∵ straight_lines_rightassert Since each of / E Q /, / K U / is at right angles to the same plane → therefore / E Q / is parallel to / K U /.
assert It is also equal to it
∵ join_equal_parallelsassert And the straight lines joining those extremities of equal and parallel straight lines which are in the same direction are equal and parallel.
assert Therefore / Q U / is equal and parallel to / E K /.
assert / E K / belongs to an equilateral pentagon;
assert Therefore / Q U / also belongs to the equilateral pentagon inscribed in ( E F G H K ).
assert For the same reason each of / Q R /, / R S /, / S T /, / T U / also belongs to the equilateral pentagon inscribed in ( E F G H K );
assert Therefore the pentagon < Q R S T U > is equilateral.
assert And, since / Q E / belongs to a hexagon, and / E P / to a decagon, and < Q E P ) is right,
assert Therefore / Q P / belongs to a pentagon;
∵ equilateral_pentagon_inscribedassert For the square on the side of the pentagon is equal to the square on the side of the hexagon and the square on the side of the decagon inscribed in the same circle.
assert For the same reason / P U / is also a side of a pentagon.
assert / Q U / also belongs to a pentagon;
assert Therefore < Q P U > is equilateral.
assert For the same reason each of < Q L R >, < R M S >, < S N T >, < T O U > is also equilateral.
assert And, since each of / Q L /, / Q P / was proved to belong to a pentagon, and / L P / also belongs to a pentagon,
assert Therefore < Q L P > is equilateral.
assert For the same reason each of < L R M >, < M S N >, < N T O >, < O U P > is also equilateral.
construct Let the center of ( E F G H K ), V, be taken
construct From V let / V Z / be set up at right angles to the plane of the circle, let it be produced in the other direction, as / V X /, let there be cut off / V W /, the side of a hexagon, and each of / V X /, / W Z /, being sides of a decagon, and let / Q Z /, / Q W /, / U Z /, / E V /, / L V /, / L X /, / X M / be joined.
∵ straight_lines_rightassert Since each of / V W /, / Q E / is at right angles to the plane of the circle → therefore / V W / is parallel to / Q E /.
assert They are also equal
∵ join_equal_parallelsassert Therefore / E V /, / Q W / are also equal and parallel.
assert / E V / belongs to a hexagon;
assert Therefore / Q W / also belongs to a hexagon.
assert And, since / Q W / belongs to a hexagon, and / W Z / to a decagon, and < Q W Z ) is right,
∵ equilateral_pentagon_inscribedassert Therefore / Q Z / belongs to a pentagon.
∵ inscribe_hexagon_in_circleassert For the same reason / U Z / also belongs to a pentagon, inasmuch as, if we join / V K /, / W U /, they will be equal and opposite, and / V K /, being a radius, belongs to a hexagon; therefore / W U / also belongs to a hexagon.
assert / W Z / belongs to a decagon, and < U W Z ) is right;
∵ equilateral_pentagon_inscribedassert Therefore / U Z / belongs to a pentagon.
assert / Q U / also belongs to a pentagon;
assert Therefore < Q U Z > is equilateral.
assert For the same reason each of the remaining triangles of which / Q R /, / R S /, / S T /, / T U / are the bases, and Z the vertex, is also equilateral.
∵ equilateral_pentagon_inscribedassert Since / V L / belongs to a hexagon, and / V X / to a decagon, and < L V X ) is right → therefore / L X / belongs to a pentagon.
assert For the same reason, if we join / M V /, which belongs to a hexagon, / M X / is also inferred to belong to a pentagon.
assert / L M / also belongs to a pentagon;
assert Therefore < L M X > is equilateral.
assert Similarly it can be proved that each of the remaining triangles of which / M N /, / N O /, / O P /, / P L / are the bases, and X the vertex, is also equilateral.
assert Therefore an icosahedron has been constructed which is contained by twenty equilateral triangles.
assert It is next required to comprehend it in the given sphere, and to prove that the side of the icosahedron is the irrational straight line called minor.
∵ side_hexagon_decagonassert Since / V W / belongs to a hexagon, and / W Z / to a decagon, therefore / V Z / has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at W, and / V W / is its greater segment
assert Therefore, as / Z V / is to / V W /, so is / V W / to / W Z /.
assert / V W / is equal to / V E /, and / W Z / to / V X /;
assert Therefore, as / Z V / is to / V E /, so is / E V / to / V X /.
assert < Z V E ), < E V X ) are right
assert Therefore, if we join / E Z /, < X E Z ) will be right because of the similarity of < X E Z >, < V E Z >.
assert For the same reason, since, as / Z V / is to / V W /, so is / V W / to / W Z /, and / Z V / is equal to / X W /, and / V W / to / W Q /,
assert Therefore, as / X W / is to / W Q /, so is / Q W / to / W Z /.
∵ right_angled_triangleassert For this reason again, if we join / Q X /, the angle at Q will be right.
∵ thales_semicircle_theoremassert Therefore the semicircle described on / X Z / will also pass through Q.
assert If, / X Z / remaining fixed, the semicircle be carried round and restored to the same position from which it began to be moved, it will also pass through Q and the remaining angular points of the icosahedron, and the icosahedron will have been comprehended in a sphere.
assert I say next that it is also comprehended in the given sphere.
construct bisect_segment(/ V W /) → A' (bisected)
assert Since / V Z / has been cut in extreme and mean ratio at W, and / Z W / is its lesser segment,
∵ straight_line_cut_xiii_3assert Therefore the square on / Z W / added to the half of the greater segment, that is / W A' /, is five times the square on the half of the greater segment; therefore the square on / Z A' / is five times the square on / A' W /.
assert / Z X / is double of / Z A' /, and / V W / double of / A' W /;
assert Therefore the square on / Z X / is five times the square on / W V /.
assert And, since / A C / is quadruple of / C B /,
assert Therefore / A B / is five times / B C /.
∵ right_angled_triangle, magnitudes_proportional_said_defassert But, as / A B / is to / B C /, so is the square on / A B / to the square on / B D /.
assert Therefore the square on / A B / is five times the square on / B D /.
assert The square on / Z X / was also proved to be five times the square on / V W /.
assert / D B / is equal to / V W /, for each of them is equal to the radius of ( E F G H K );
assert Therefore / A B / is also equal to / X Z /.
assert / A B / is the diameter of the given sphere;
assert Therefore / X Z / is also equal to the diameter of the given sphere.
assert Therefore the icosahedron has been comprehended in the given sphere
assert I say next that the side of the icosahedron is the irrational straight line called minor.
assert Since the diameter of the sphere is rational, and the square on it is five times the square on the radius of ( E F G H K ),
assert Therefore the radius of ( E F G H K ) is also rational; hence its diameter is also rational.
∵ circle_diameter_rationalassert But, if an equilateral pentagon be inscribed in a circle which has its diameter rational, the side of the pentagon is the irrational straight line called minor.
assert The side of the pentagon < E F G H K > is the side of the icosahedron.
assert Therefore the side of the icosahedron is the irrational straight line called minor.
conclude Porism. From this it is manifest that the square on the diameter of the sphere is five times the square on the radius of the circle from which the icosahedron has been described, and that the diameter of the sphere is composed of the side of the hexagon and two of the sides of the decagon inscribed in the same circle.
