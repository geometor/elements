G.204 there magnitudes others¶

V.21

If there be three magnitudes, and others equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two together are in the same ratio, and the proportion of them be perturbed, then, if ex aequali the first magnitude is greater than the third, the fourth is greater than the sixth; if equal, equal; and if less, less.
there_magnitudes_others_v_21(A, B, C, D)
given there be three magnitudes A, B, C, others D, E, F equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two are in the same ratio, let the proportion of them be perturbed, so that
prove D will also be greater than F
given there be three magnitudes A, B, C, others D, E, F equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two are in the same ratio, let the proportion of them be perturbed, so that
assert As A is to B, so is E to F,
assert And, as B is to C, so is D to E,
assert And let A be greater than C ex aequali
assert If A is equal to C, equal
assert And if less, less.
∵ unequal_magnitudes_greaterassert Since A is greater than C, and B is some other magnitude → therefore A has to B a greater ratio than C has to B.
assert But, as A is to B, so is E to F, and, as C is to B, inversely, so is E to D.
∵ magnitude_ratio_greaterassert Therefore also E has to F a greater ratio than E has to D.
∵ magnitudes_ratio_greaterassert That to which the same has a greater ratio is less;
assert Therefore F is less than D;
assert Therefore D is greater than F.
assert Similarly we can prove that, if A be equal to C, D will also be equal to F
conclude And if less, less.
