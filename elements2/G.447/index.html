XI.4

If a straight line be set up at right angles to two straight lines which cut one another, at their common point of section, it is at right angles to the plane through them.
straight_line_set()
prove / E F / is also at right angles to the plane through / A B /, / C D /
construct Let a straight line / E F / be set up at right angles to / A B /, / C D /, which cut one another at E, from E
construct Let / A E /, / E B /, / C E /, / E D / be cut off equal to one another
construct Let any straight line / G E H / be drawn across through E, at random
construct Let / A D /, / C B / be joined, and further let / F A /, / F G /, / F D /, / F C /, / F H /, / F B / be joined from F taken at random &lt
assert On / E F / &gt
assert .
∵ vertical_angles_equalassert Since / A E /, / E D / are equal to / C E /, / E B /, and contain equal angles.
∵ sas_congruenceassert Therefore the base / A D / is equal to the base / C B /, and < A E D > will be equal to < C E B >; so that < D A E ) is also equal to < E B C ).
∵ vertical_angles_equalassert < A E G ) is also equal to < B E H );
∵ asa_aas_congruenceassert Therefore < A G E >, < B E H > are two triangles which have two angles equal to two angles respectively, and one side equal to one side, namely that adjacent to the equal angles, that is to say, / A E / to / E B /; therefore they will also have the remaining sides equal to the remaining sides.
assert Therefore / G E / is equal to / E H /, and / A G / to / B H /.
assert And, since / A E / is equal to / E B /, while / F E / is common and at right angles,
∵ sas_congruenceassert Therefore the base / F A / is equal to the base / F B /.
assert For the same reason / F C / is also equal to / F D /.
assert And, since / A D / is equal to / C B /, and / F A / is also equal to / F B /, the two sides / F A /, / A D / are equal to the two sides / F B /, / B C / respectively; and the base / F D / was proved equal to the base / F C /;
∵ sss_congruenceassert Therefore < F A D ) is also equal to < F B C ).
assert Since, again, / A G / was proved equal to / B H /, and further / F A / also equal to / F B /, the two sides / F A /, / A G / are equal to the two sides / F B /, / B H /. And < F A G ) was proved equal to < F B H );
∵ sas_congruenceassert Therefore the base / F G / is equal to the base / F H /.
assert Now since, again, / G E / was proved equal to / E H /, and / E F / is common, the two sides / G E /, / E F / are equal to the two sides / H E /, / E F /; and the base / F G / is equal to the base / F H /;
∵ sss_congruenceassert Therefore < G E F ) is equal to < H E F ).
assert Therefore each of < G E F ), < H E F ) is right.
assert Therefore / F E / is at right angles to / G H / drawn at random through E.
assert Similarly we can prove that / F E / will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference.
∵ straight_line_right_defassert A straight line is at right angles to a plane when it makes right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in that same plane.
assert Therefore / F E / is at right angles to the plane of reference.
assert The plane of reference is the plane through / A B /, / C D /.
conclude Therefore / F E / is at right angles to the plane through / A B /, / C D /.
