XIII.15

Construct a cube and comprehend it in a sphere, like the pyramid; and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square on the side of the cube.
construct_cube_comprehend() → / K G /
construct the cube has been comprehended in the given sphere, and it has been demonstrated at the same time that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square on the side of the cube
construct Let the diameter / A B / of the given sphere be set out, and let it be cut at C so that / A C / is double of / C B /
construct Let the semicircle ( A D B ) be described on / A B /
construct perpendicular_at(/ A B /, C) → / C D /
construct segment(D, B) → / D B /
construct Let the square < E F G H > having its side equal to / D B / be set out, from E, F, G, H let / E K /, / F L /, / G M /, / H N / be drawn at right angles to the plane of the square < E F G H >
construct From / E K /, / F L /, / G M /, / H N / let / E K /, / F L /, / G M /, / H N / respectively be cut off equal to one of / E F /, / F G /, / G H /, / H E /, and let / K L /, / L M /, / M N /, / N K / be joined
assert Therefore the cube < F N > has been constructed which is contained by six equal squares.
assert It is then required to comprehend it in the given sphere, and to prove that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square on the side of the cube.
construct segment(K, G) → / K G /, segment(E, G) → / E G /
∵ straight_line_right_defassert Since < K E G ) is right, because / K E / is also at right angles to the plane < E F G H > and of course to / E G / also, therefore the semicircle described on / K G / will also pass through E.
assert Since / G F / is at right angles to each of / F L /, / F E /, / G F / is also at right angles to the plane < E F L K >
assert Hence also, if we join / F K /, / G F / will be at right angles to / F K /
assert And for this reason again the semicircle described on / G K / will also pass through F.
assert Similarly it will also pass through the remaining angular points of the cube.
assert If then, / K G / remaining fixed, the semicircle be carried round and restored to the same position from which it began to be moved, the cube will be comprehended in a sphere.
assert I say next that it is also comprehended in the given sphere.
assert Since / G F / is equal to / F E /, and the angle at < E F G ) is right, therefore the square on / E G / is double of the square on / E F /.
assert / E F / is equal to / E K /
assert Therefore the square on / E G / is double of the square on / E K /
∵ pythagorean_theoremassert Hence the squares on / G E /, / E K /, that is the square on / G K /, is triple of the square on / E K /.
assert And, since / A B / is triple of / B C /, while, as / A B / is to / B C /, so is the square on / A B / to the square on / B D /, therefore the square on / A B / is triple of the square on / B D /.
assert The square on / G K / was also proved triple of the square on / K E /.
assert / K E / was made equal to / D B /
assert Therefore / K G / is also equal to / A B /.
assert / A B / is the diameter of the given sphere
assert Therefore / K G / is also equal to the diameter of the given sphere.
conclude Therefore the cube has been comprehended in the given sphere, and it has been demonstrated at the same time that the square on the diameter of the sphere is triple of the square on the side of the cube.
