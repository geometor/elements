G.519 parallel planes cut¶

XI.16

If two parallel planes be cut by any plane, their common sections are parallel.
parallel_planes_cut()
prove / E F / is parallel to / G H /
construct Let the two parallel planes < A B >, < C D > be cut by the plane < E F G H >
assert Let / E F /, / G H / be their common sections
construct If not, / E F /, / G H / will, when produced, meet either in the direction of F, H or of E, G. Let them be produced, as in the direction of F, H, and let them, first, meet at K.
assert / E F K / is in the plane < A B >,
∵ part_segment_planeassert all the points on / E F K / are also in the plane < A B >
assert K is one of the points on / E F K /
assert K is in the plane < A B >
assert For the same reason K is also in the plane < C D >
assert the planes < A B >, < C D > will meet when produced
assert They do not meet, because they are, by hypothesis, parallel
assert / E F /, / G H / will not meet when produced in the direction of F, H
assert Similarly we can prove that neither will / E F /, / G H / meet when produced in the direction of E, G
∵ parallel_lines_defassert Segments which do not meet in either direction are parallel
assert / E F / is parallel to / G H /
∴ etc
