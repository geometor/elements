G.86¶

III.11

If two circles touch one another internally, and their centres be taken, the straight line joining their centres, if it be also produced, will fall on the point of contact of the circles.
circles_touch_internally()
prove the straight line joined from G to F and produced will fall on A
construct Let the two circles ( A B C ), ( A D E ) touch one another internally at A, and let the center F of ( A B C ), and the center G of ( A D E ), be taken
construct Suppose it does not, but, if possible, let it fall as / F G H /, and let / A F /, / A G / be joined.
assert Since / A G /, / G F / are greater than / F A /, that is, than / F H / → let / F G / be subtracted from each;
assert Therefore the remainder / A G / is greater than the remainder / G H /.
assert / A G / is equal to / G D /
assert Therefore / G D / is also greater than / G H /,
assert The less than the greater: which is impossible.
assert Therefore the straight line joined from F to G will not fall outside;
conclude Therefore it will fall at A on the point of contact.
