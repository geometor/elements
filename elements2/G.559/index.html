G.559 similar pyramids triangular¶

XII.8

Similar pyramids which have triangular bases are in the triplicate ratio of their corresponding sides.
similar_pyramids_triangular(< A B C >, < D E F >, G, H)
given there be similar and similarly situated pyramids of which < A B C >, < D E F > are the bases and G, H the vertices
prove the pyramid < A B C G > has to the pyramid < D E F H > the ratio triplicate of that which / B C / has to / E F /
given there be similar and similarly situated pyramids of which < A B C >, < D E F > are the bases and G, H the vertices
assert Let the parallelepipedal solids < B G M L >, < E H Q P > be completed.
assert Since the pyramid < A B C G > is similar to the pyramid < D E F H >,
assert Therefore < A B C ) is equal to < D E F ), < G B C ) to < H E F ), and < A B G ) to < D E H ); and, as / A B / is to / D E /, so is / B C / to / E F /, and / B G / to / E H /.
assert Since, as / A B / is to / D E /, so is / B C / to / E F /, and the sides are proportional about equal angles,
assert Therefore the parallelogram < B C M A > is similar to the parallelogram < E F Q D >.
assert For the same reason < B C N G > is also similar to < E F R H >, and < B G K A > to < E H O D >;
assert Therefore the three parallelograms < B C M A >, < B G K A >, < B C N G > are similar to the three < E F Q D >, < E H O D >, < E F R H >.
∵ solid_contained_parallelassert The three parallelograms < B C M A >, < B G K A >, < B C N G > are equal and similar to their three opposites, and the three < E F Q D >, < E H O D >, < E F R H > are equal and similar to their three opposites.
assert Therefore the solids < B G M L >, < E H Q P > are contained by similar planes equal in multitude.
assert Therefore the solid < B G M L > is similar to the solid < E H Q P >.
∵ similar_parallelepipedal_solidsassert Similar parallelepipedal solids are in the triplicate ratio of their corresponding sides.
assert Therefore the solid < B G M L > has to the solid < E H Q P > the ratio triplicate of that which the corresponding side / B C / has to the corresponding side / E F /.
∵ parallelepipedal_solid_cut_xi_28, prism_triangular_baseassert But, as the solid < B G M L > is to the solid < E H Q P >, so is the pyramid < A B C G > to the pyramid < D E F H >, inasmuch as the pyramid is a sixth part of the solid, because the prism which is half of the parallelepipedal solid is also triple of the pyramid.
∵ Q. E. D.assert Therefore the pyramid < A B C G > also has to the pyramid < D E F H > the ratio triplicate of that which / B C / has to / E F /.
assert PORISM. From this it is manifest that similar pyramids which have polygonal bases are also to one another in the triplicate ratio of their corresponding sides.
∵ similar_polygons_divided, number_magnitudes_proportionalassert If they are divided into the pyramids contained in them which have triangular bases, by virtue of the fact that the similar polygons forming their bases are also divided into similar triangles equal in multitude and corresponding to the wholes, then, as the one pyramid which has a triangular base in the one complete pyramid is to the one pyramid which has a triangular base in the other complete pyramid, so also will all the pyramids which have triangular bases contained in the one pyramid be to all the pyramids which have triangular bases contained in the other pyramid, that is, the pyramid itself which has a polygonal base to the pyramid which has a polygonal base.
assert The pyramid which has a triangular base is to the pyramid which has a triangular base in the triplicate ratio of the corresponding sides;
conclude Therefore also the pyramid which has a polygonal base has to the pyramid which has a similar base the ratio triplicate of that which the side has to the side.
