G.392¶

X.71

If a rational and a medial area be added together, four irrational straight lines arise, namely a binomial or a first bimedial or a major or a side of a rational plus a medial area.
rational_medial_area(< A B >, < C D >)
given < A B > be rational, < C D > medial
prove the “side” of the area < A D > is a binomial or a first bimedial or a major or a side of a rational plus a medial area
given < A B > be rational, < C D > medial
assert < A B > is either greater or less than < C D >.
assert First, let it be greater
construct Let a rational straight line / E F / be set out, let there be applied to / E F / the rectangle < E G > equal to < A B >, producing / E H / as breadth, and let < H I >, equal to < D C >, be applied to / E F /, producing / H K / as breadth.
assert Since < A B > is rational and is equal to < E G >,
assert Therefore < E G > is also rational.
assert It has been applied to / E F /, producing / E H / as breadth;
∵ rational_area_appliedassert Therefore / E H / is rational and commensurable in length with / E F /.
assert Since < C D > is medial and is equal to < H I >,
assert Therefore < H I > is also medial.
assert It is applied to the rational straight line / E F /, producing / H K / as breadth;
∵ square_medial_straightassert Therefore / H K / is rational and incommensurable in length with / E F /.
assert And, since < C D > is medial, while < A B > is rational,
assert Therefore < A B > is incommensurable with < C D >, so that < E G > is also incommensurable with < H I >.
∵ triangles_parallelograms_heightassert But, as < E G > is to < H I >, so is / E H / to / H K /;
∵ magnitudes_proportional_commensurableassert Therefore / E H / is also incommensurable in length with / H K /.
assert Both are rational;
∵ rational_straight_linesassert Therefore / E H /, / H K / are rational straight lines commensurable in square only; therefore / E K / is a binomial straight line, divided at H.
assert And, since < A B > is greater than < C D >, while < A B > is equal to < E G > and < C D > to < H I >,
assert Therefore < E G > is also greater than < H I > ; therefore / E H / is also greater than / H K /.
assert The square, then, on / E H / is greater than the square on / H K / either by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with / E H / or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.
assert First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a straight line commensurable with itself.
assert Now the greater straight line / H E / is commensurable in length with the rational straight line / E F / set out ;
∵ rational_straight_line_defassert Therefore / E K / is a first binomial.
∵ area_contained_rationalassert / E F / is rational ; and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the first binomial, the side of the square equal to the area is binomial.
assert Therefore the “side” of < E I > is binomial ; so that the “side” of < A D > is also binomial.
assert Next, let the square on / E H / be greater than the square on / H K / by the square on a straight line incommensurable with / E H /.
assert Now the greater straight line / E H / is commensurable in length with the rational straight line / E F / set out ;
∵ again_square_greater_defassert Therefore / E K / is a fourth binomial.
∵ area_contained_rational_x_57assert / E F / is rational ; and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the fourth binomial, the “side” of the area is the irrational straight line called major.
assert Therefore the “side” of the area < E I > is major ; so that the “side” of the area < A D > is also major.
assert Next, let < A B > be less than < C D > ;
assert Therefore < E G > is also less than < H I >, so that / E H / is also less than / H K /.
assert Now the square on / H K / is greater than the square on / E H / either by the square on a straight line commensurable with / H K / or by the square on a straight line incommensurable with it.
assert First, let the square on it be greater by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with itself.
assert Now the lesser straight line / E H / is commensurable in length with the rational straight line / E F / set out;
∵ lesser_term_commensurable_defassert Therefore / E K / is a second binomial.
∵ area_contained_rational_x_55assert / E F / is rational, and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the second binomial, the side of the square equal to it is a first bimedial.
assert Therefore the “side” of the area < E I > is a first bimedial, so that the “side” of < A D > is also a first bimedial.
assert Next, let the square on / H K / be greater than the square on / H E / by the square on a straight line incommensurable with / H K /.
assert Now the lesser straight line / E H / is commensurable with the rational straight line / E F / set out;
∵ lesser_fifth_binomial_defassert Therefore / E K / is a fifth binomial.
∵ area_contained_rational_x_58assert / E F / is rational; and, if an area be contained by a rational straight line and the fifth binomial, the side of the square equal to the area is a side of a rational plus a medial area.
conclude Therefore the “side” of the area < E I > is a side of a rational plus a medial area, so that the “side” of the area < A D > is also a side of a rational plus a medial area.
