G.500¶

XII.13

If a cylinder be cut by a plane which is parallel to its opposite planes, then, as the cylinder is to the cylinder, so will the axis be to the axis.
cylinder_cut_plane()
prove as the axis / E K / is to the axis / K F /, so is the cylinder < B G > to the cylinder < G D >
construct Let the cylinder < A D > be cut by the plane ( G H ) which is parallel to the opposite planes ( A B ), ( C D ), and let the plane ( G H ) meet the axis at K
assert I say that, as the cylinder < B G > is to the cylinder < G D >, so is the axis / E K / to the axis / K F /.
construct extend(the axis / E F /) → L, M, and let there be set out any number whatever of axes / E N /, / N L / equal to the axis / E K /, and any number whatever / F O /, / O M / equal to / F K /
assert And let the cylinder < P W > on the axis / L M / be conceived of which ( P Q ), ( V W ) are the bases.
assert Let planes be carried through N, O parallel to ( A B ), ( C D ) and to the bases of the cylinder < P W >, and let them produce ( R S ), ( T U ) about the centres N, O.
assert Since the axes / L N /, / N E /, / E K / are equal to one another,
∵ cones_cylinders_heightassert Therefore the cylinders < Q R >, < R B >, < B G > are to one another as their bases.
assert The bases are equal;
assert Therefore the cylinders < Q R >, < R B >, < B G > are also equal to one another.
assert Since then the axes / L N /, / N E /, / E K / are equal to one another, and the cylinders < Q R >, < R B >, < B G > are also equal to one another, and the multitude of the former is equal to the multitude of the latter,
assert Therefore, whatever multiple the axis / K L / is of the axis / E K /, the same multiple also will the cylinder < Q G > be of the cylinder < G B >.
assert For the same reason, whatever multiple the axis / M K / is of the axis / K F /, the same multiple also is the cylinder < W G > of the cylinder < G D >.
assert And, if the axis / K L / is equal to the axis / K M /, the cylinder < Q G > will also be equal to the cylinder < G W >, if the axis is greater than the axis, the cylinder will also be greater than the cylinder, and if less, less.
assert Thus, there being four magnitudes, the axes / E K /, / K F / and the cylinders < B G >, < G D >, there have been taken equimultiples of the axis / E K / and of the cylinder < B G >, namely the axis / L K / and the cylinder < Q G >, and equimultiples of the axis / K F / and of the cylinder < G D >, namely the axis / K M / and the cylinder < G W >; and it has been proved that, if the axis / K L / is in excess of the axis / K M /, the cylinder < Q G > is also in excess of the cylinder < G W >, if equal, equal, and if less, less.
∵ magnitudes_said_ratio_defconclude Therefore, as the axis / E K / is to the axis / K F /, so is the cylinder < B G > to the cylinder < G D >.
