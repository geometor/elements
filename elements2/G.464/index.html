G.464¶

XI.19

If two planes which cut one another be at right angles to any plane, their common section is at right angles to the same plane.
planes_cut_right()
prove / B D / is at right angles to the plane of reference
assert Let the two planes < A B >, < B C > be at right angles to the plane of reference, and let / B D / be their common section
construct Suppose it is not, and from D let / D E / be drawn in the plane < A B > at right angles to / A D /, and / D F / in the plane < B C > at right angles to / C D /.
assert Since the plane < A B > is at right angles to the plane of reference, and / D E / has been drawn in the plane < A B > at right angles to / A D /, their common section,
∵ plane_right_angles_defassert Therefore / D E / is at right angles to the plane of reference.
assert Similarly we can prove that / D F / is also at right angles to the plane of reference.
∵ point_straight_linesassert Therefore from the same point D two straight lines have been set up at right angles to the plane of reference on the same side: which is impossible.
conclude Therefore no straight line except the common section / D B / of the planes < A B >, < B C > can be set up from D at right angles to the plane of reference.
