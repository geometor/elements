G.73 apply a parallelogram equal to a triangle on a segment — Heath¶

I.44

To a given straight line to apply, in a given rectilineal angle, a parallelogram equal to a given triangle.

Let / A B / be the given straight line, < C > the given triangle and < D ) the given rectilineal angle;

thus it is required to apply to the given straight line / A B /, in an angle equal to the angle < D ), a parallelogram equal to the given triangle < C >.

Let the parallelogram < B E F G > be constructed equal to the triangle < C >, in the angle < E B G ) which is equal to < D ) [G.71]; let it be placed so that / B E / is in a straight line with / A B /; let / F G / be drawn through to H, and let / A H / be drawn through A parallel to either / B G / or / E F /. [G.58]

Let / H B / be joined.

Then, since the straight line / H F / falls upon the parallels / A H /, / E F /,

[G.56]the angles < A H F ), < H F E ) are equal to two right angles.

Therefore the angles < B H G ), < G F E ) are less than two right angles; and straight lines produced indefinitely from angles less than two right angles meet; [G.55]

therefore / H B /, / F E /, when produced, will meet.

Let them be produced and meet at K; through the point K let / K L / be drawn parallel to either / E A / or / F H /, [G.58]

and let / H A /, / G B / be produced to the points L, M.

Then < H L K F > is a parallelogram, / H K / is its diameter, and < H A B G >, < B M K E > are parallelograms, and < A B M L >, < B E F G > the so-called complements, about / H K /;

[G.72]therefore < A B M L > is equal to < B E F G >.

But < B E F G > is equal to the triangle < C >;

[G.16]therefore < A B M L > is also equal to < C >.

And, since the angle < G B E ) is equal to the angle < A B M ), [G.40]

while the angle < G B E ) is equal to < D ),

the angle < A B M ) is also equal to the angle < D ).
Therefore the parallelogram < A B M L > equal to the given triangle < C > has been applied to the given straight line / A B /, in the angle < A B M ) which is equal to < D ). Q. E. F.
