G.290 numbers find least¶

VII.34

Given two numbers, to find the least number which they measure.
numbers_find_least(A, B) → C, A
given A, B be the two given numbers
construct C is the least that is measured by A, B
given A, B be the two given numbers
assert Now A, B are either prime to one another or not
assert First, let A, B be prime to one another, and let A by multiplying B make C
∵ numbers_multiplying_makeassert also B by multiplying A has made C
assert A, B measure C
assert I say next that it is also the least number they measure
assert If not, A, B will measure some number which < C
assert Let them measure D
assert As many times as A measures D, so many units let there be in E, and, as many times as B measures D, so many units let there be in F
∵ number_said_multiply_defassert A by multiplying E has made D, and B by multiplying F has made D
assert the product of A, E is equal to the product of B, F
∵ numbers_proportional_numberassert as A is to B, so is F to E
∵ numbers_prime_least, least_numbers_ratioassert A, B are prime, primes are also least, and the least measure the numbers which have the same ratio the same number of times, the greater the greater and the less the less
assert B measures E, as consequent consequent
assert since A by multiplying B, E has made C, D,
∵ number_multiplying_numbersassert as B is to E, so is C to D
assert B measures E
assert C also measures D, the greater the less ⇒⇐
assert A, B do not measure any number less than C; therefore C is the least that is measured by A, B
construct many_numbers_please: Next, let A, B not be prime to one another, and let F, E, the least numbers of those which have the same ratio with A, B, be taken
∵ numbers_proportional_numberassert the product of A, E is equal to the product of B, F
assert Let A by multiplying E make C
assert also B by multiplying F has made C
assert A, B measure C
assert I say next that it is also the least number that they measure
assert If not, A, B will measure some number which < C
assert Let them measure D
assert as many times as A measures D, so many units let there be in G, and, as many times as B measures D, so many units let there be in H
assert A by multiplying G has made D, and B by multiplying H has made D
assert the product of A, G is equal to the product of B, H
∵ numbers_proportional_numberassert as A is to B, so is H to G
assert But, as A is to B, so is F to E
assert also, as F is to E, so is H to G
∵ least_numbers_ratioassert F, E are least, and the least measure the numbers which have the same ratio the same number of times, the greater the greater and the less the less
assert E measures G
assert since A by multiplying E, G has made C, D,
∵ number_multiplying_numbersassert as E is to G, so is C to D
assert E measures G
assert C also measures D, the greater the less ⇒⇐
assert A, B will not measure any number which < C
∴ C is the least that is measured by A, B
