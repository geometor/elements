G.335 number multiplying itself — Heath¶

IX.6

If a number by multiplying itself make a cube number, it will itself also be cube.

For let the number A by multiplying itself make the cube number B;

I say that A is also cube.

For let A by multiplying B make C.

Since, then, A by multiplying itself has made B, and by multiplying B has made C,
therefore C is cube.
And, since A by multiplying itself has made B,
therefore A measures B according to the units in itself.
But the unit also measures A according to the units in it.
[G.263]Therefore, as the unit is to A, so is A to B.
And, since A by multiplying B has made C,
therefore B measures C according to the units in A.
But the unit also measures A according to the units in it.
[G.263]Therefore, as the unit is to A, so is B to C.
But, as the unit is to A, so is A to B;
therefore also, as A is to B, so is B to C.
And, since B, C are cube,
they are similar solid numbers.
Therefore there are two mean proportional numbers between B, C. [G.321]
And, as B is to C, so is A to B.
Therefore there are two mean proportional numbers between A, B also. [G.305]
And B is cube;
[G.325]therefore A is also cube.
Q. E. D.
