Book Three, Proposition Twenty-One. In a circle, angles in the same segment are equal to one another. <pause> Let A B C D be a circle, and let angles B A D and B E D lie in the same circle segment B A E D. We are to prove that angle B A D equals angle B E D.

[bell:construction]
[style: methodical, precise constructive cadence]
Take the center F of circle A B C D by Three One, and join the radii B F and F D by Postulate One.

[bell:insight]

Because angle B F D is at the center and angle B A D is at the circumference, and both stand on the same base arc B C D, by Three Twenty angle B F D is double angle B A D. <pause> For the exact same reason, angle B F D is also double angle B E D. <pause> Since both inscribed angles are halves of the same central angle B F D, angle B A D equals angle B E D, as was required to prove.
