Book Four, Proposition Twelve. About a given circle to circumscribe an equilateral and equiangular pentagon. <pause> Let A B C D E be the given circle; it is required to circumscribe an equilateral and equiangular pentagon about the circle A B C D E.

[bell:construction]
[style: methodical, precise constructive cadence]
By Four Eleven, let A, B, C, D, and E be the vertices of an inscribed regular pentagon, so that the arcs A B, B C, C D, D E, and E A are equal. Through A, B, C, D, and E draw G H, H K, K L, L M, and M G tangent to the circle by the Porism to Three Sixteen. <pause> By Three One, take the center F of circle A B C D E, and join F B, F K, F C, F L, and F D.

[bell:insight]
[style: luminous, deductive geometric clarity]
By Three Eighteen, each radius to a point of contact is perpendicular to its tangent, so the angles at B, C, and D are right angles. In right triangles F B K and F C K, by One Forty-Seven the square on F K equals the squares on F C and C K, and also the squares on F B and B K. Since radii F C and F B are equal, the remaining squares on C K and B K are equal, so B K equals C K. By One Eight, triangles F B K and F C K are congruent, making central angle B F C double angle K F C, and corner angle B K C double angle F K C; similarly angle C F D is double angle C F L, and angle D L C is double angle F L C. <pause> Because arc B C equals arc C D, by Three Twenty-Seven central angle B F C equals angle C F D, so their halves—angles K F C and L F C—are equal. Since right angles F C K and F C L are also equal and side F C is common, by One Twenty-Six triangles F K C and F L C are congruent, so K C equals C L and angle F K C equals angle F L C. <pause> Because K C equals C L, tangent side K L is double K C, and similarly H K is double B K. Since B K equals K C, side H K equals K L, and by the same reasoning all five sides G H, H K, K L, L M, and M G are equal. Moreover, since angle F K C equals angle F L C, their doubles—interior angles H K L and K L M—are equal, and all five angles of pentagon G H K L M are equal. <pause> Therefore an equilateral and equiangular pentagon G H K L M has been circumscribed about the given circle A B C D E, as was required to do.
