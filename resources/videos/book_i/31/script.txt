[style:brisk]
Euclid's Elements, Book One, Proposition Thirty-One. <pause>
Construct through a given point a line parallel to a given line. <pause>
Let A be the given point, and segment B C the given line. <pause>
We construct through point A a line parallel to segment B C.

[bell:construction]
[style:brisk]
Take a point D at random on segment B C, and construct segment A D. <pause>
By Proposition Twenty-Three, on segment D A and at point A on it, construct angle D A E equal to angle A D C. <pause>
Then extend segment E A in a straight line to A F.

[bell:insight]
[style:brisk]
Since transversal A D falling on segments B C and E F makes the alternate interior angles D A E and A D C equal, <pause> by Proposition Twenty-Seven, segment E F is parallel to segment B C. <pause>
Therefore through the given point A, line E A F has been constructed parallel to the given line B C, <pause> as was required to do.
