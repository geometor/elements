[bell:proposition] [style:brisk] Euclid's Elements, Book One, Proposition Ten. <pause> Bisect a given segment. <pause> Given segment A B, we are to bisect it.

[bell:construction] [style:brisk] On segment A B, construct the equilateral triangle A B C. <pause> Next, bisect angle A C B by segment C D, meeting segment A B at point D.

[bell:insight] [style:brisk] In triangles A C D and B C D, side A C equals side B C, side C D is common, and the included angle A C D equals angle B C D. <pause> By Side-Angle-Side congruence, the base A D equals the base B D. <pause> Therefore segment A B has been bisected at point D, <pause> as was required to do.
