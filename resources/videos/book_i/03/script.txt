[bell:proposition] [style:brisk] Euclid's Elements, Book One, Proposition Three. <pause> Cut off from the greater of two unequal segments a segment equal to the less. <pause> Given two unequal segments A B and C, where A B is greater, we are to cut off from A B a segment equal to C.

[bell:construction] [style:brisk] At point A, construct segment A D equal to segment C. <pause> Next, construct circle A D, with center A and defining radius point D, intersecting segment A B at point E.

[bell:insight] [style:brisk] Since A is the center of circle A D, segment A E equals segment A D. <pause> And segment C also equals segment A D by construction. <pause> Because both A E and C equal A D, things equal to the same thing are equal to one another. <pause> Therefore segment A E has been cut off from the greater segment A B equal to the lesser segment C, <pause> as was required to do.
