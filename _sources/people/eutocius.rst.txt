Eutocius of Ascalon
===================

*Commentator on Archimedes and Apollonius*

Preserved priceless historical solutions to the duplication of the cube, trisection of the angle, and ancient commentaries on Archimedes' Sphere and Cylinder and Apollonius's Conics.
