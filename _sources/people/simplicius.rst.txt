Simplicius of Cilicia
=====================

*Neoplatonic Commentator on Aristotle & Euclid*

Preserved Eudemus's verbatim account of the quadrature of lunes by Hippocrates of Chios (in his Commentary on Aristotle's Physics) and ancient proofs of the Parallel Postulate (in an-Nairīzī).
