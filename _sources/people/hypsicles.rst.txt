Hypsicles of Alexandria
=======================

*Alexandrian Mathematician & Astronomer*

Author of the treatise on regular polyhedra appended to Euclid's Elements as 'Book XIV', building on Aristaeus and Apollonius on the dodecahedron and icosahedron inscribed in the same sphere.
