G.200 convertendo (conversion of ratio) — Heath
===============================================

.. rubric:: `V.def.16 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_v/definitions/#v-def-16>`_

.. enunciation::

   :term:`**Conversion of a ratio** <convertendo>` means taking the :term:`antecedent <G.181>` in relation to the excess by which the antecedent exceeds the consequent.
