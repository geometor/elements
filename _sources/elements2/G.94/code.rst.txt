G.94 law of cosines for obtuse triangles — Code
===============================================

.. rubric:: `II.12 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_ii/propositions/12/>`_

.. enunciation::

   In :term:`obtuse-angled triangles <G.78>`, the :term:`square <G.63>` on the side subtending the :term:`obtuse angle <G.76>` is greater than the sum of the squares on the sides containing the obtuse angle by twice the :term:`rectangle contained <G.81>` by one of those sides and the projection of the other upon it.
