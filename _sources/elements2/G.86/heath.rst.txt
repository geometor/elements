G.86 gnomon — Heath
===================

.. rubric:: `II.def.2 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_ii/definitions/#ii-def-2>`_

.. enunciation::

   And in any parallelogrammic area let any one whatever of the parallelograms about its :term:`diameter <G.61>` with the two complements be called a :term:`**gnomon** <gnomon>`.
