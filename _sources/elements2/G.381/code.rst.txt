G.381 squares segments commensurable — Code
===========================================

.. rubric:: `X.9 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_x/propositions/009/>`_

.. enunciation::

   The :term:`squares <G.63>` on :term:`segments <G.4>` :term:`commensurable <G.372>` in length have to one another the :term:`ratio <G.173>` which a :term:`square number <G.308>` has to a square number; and squares which have to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number will also have their sides commensurable in length. But the squares on segments incommensurable in length have not to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number; and squares which have not to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number will not have their sides commensurable in length either.
