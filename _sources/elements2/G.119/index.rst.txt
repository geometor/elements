G.119
=====

.. rubric:: `IV.3 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_iv/propositions/03/>`_

.. enunciation::

   About a given circle to circumscribe a triangle equiangular with a given triangle.

Let :g:`( A B C ) <0089.g1:( A B C )>` be the given circle, and :g:`< D E F > <0089.g1:< D E F >>` the given
triangle;
thus it is required to circumscribe about the circle :g:`( A B C ) <0089.g1:( A B C )>` a
triangle equiangular with the triangle :g:`< D E F > <0089.g1:< D E F >>`.

.. picture:: 0089.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram for Proposition 3

Let :g:`/ E F / <0089.g1:/ E F />` be produced in both directions to the points
:g:`G <0089.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0089.g1:H>`,
let the centre :g:`K <0089.g1:K>` of the circle :g:`( A B C ) <0089.g1:( A B C )>` be taken :just:`[G.76] <G.76>`, and let
the straight line :g:`/ K B / <0089.g1:/ K B />` be drawn across at random;
on the straight line :g:`/ K B / <0089.g1:/ K B />`, and at the point :g:`K <0089.g1:K>` on it, let the
angle :g:`< B K A ) <0089.g1:< B K A )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< B K A ) = 0089.g1:< D E G )>` to the angle :g:`< D E G ) <0089.g1:< D E G )>`,
and the angle :g:`< B K C ) <0089.g1:< B K C )>` :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< B K C ) = 0089.g1:< D F H )>` to the angle :g:`< D F H ) <0089.g1:< D F H )>`; :just:`[G.34] <G.34>`
and through the points :g:`A <0089.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0089.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0089.g1:C>` let :g:`/ L A M / <0089.g1:/ L A M />`, :g:`/ M B N / <0089.g1:/ M B N />`, :g:`/ N C L / <0089.g1:/ N C L />` be
drawn touching the circle :g:`( A B C ) <0089.g1:( A B C )>`. :just:`[G.94] <G.94>`

Now, since :g:`/ L M / <0089.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`/ M N / <0089.g1:/ M N />`, :g:`/ N L / <0089.g1:/ N L />` touch the circle :g:`( A B C ) <0089.g1:( A B C )>` at the
points :g:`A <0089.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0089.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0089.g1:C>`,
and :g:`/ K A / <0089.g1:/ K A />`, :g:`/ K B / <0089.g1:/ K B />`, :g:`/ K C / <0089.g1:/ K C />` have been joined from the centre :g:`K <0089.g1:K>` to
the points :g:`A <0089.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0089.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0089.g1:C>`,
therefore the angles at the points :g:`A <0089.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0089.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0089.g1:C>` are right. :just:`[G.96] <G.96>`

And, since the four angles of the quadrilateral :g:`< A M B K > <0089.g1:< A M B K >>`
are equal to four right angles, inasmuch as :g:`< A M B K > <0089.g1:< A M B K >>` is in fact
divisible into two triangles,

.. subclause::

   and the angles :g:`< K A M ) <0089.g1:< K A M )>`, :g:`< K B M ) <0089.g1:< K B M )>` are right,

therefore the remaining angles :g:`< A K B ) <0089.g1:< A K B )>`, :g:`< A M B ) <0089.g1:< A M B )>` are equal to two
right angles.

But the angles :g:`< D E G ) <0089.g1:< D E G )>`, :g:`< D E F ) <0089.g1:< D E F )>` are also equal to two right
angles; :just:`[G.22] <G.22>`
therefore the angles :g:`< A K B ) <0089.g1:< A K B )>`, :g:`< A M B ) <0089.g1:< A M B )>` are equal to the angles
:g:`< D E G ) <0089.g1:< D E G )>`, :g:`< D E F ) <0089.g1:< D E F )>`,
of which the angle :g:`< A K B ) <0089.g1:< A K B )>` is :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< A K B ) = 0089.g1:< D E G )>` to the angle :g:`< D E G ) <0089.g1:< D E G )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`< A M B ) <0089.g1:< A M B )>` which remains is :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< A M B ) = 0089.g1:< D E F )>` to the angle :g:`< D E F ) <0089.g1:< D E F )>` which remains.

Similarly it can be proved that the angle :g:`< L N B ) <0089.g1:< L N B )>` is also
:eq:`equal <0089.g1:< L N B ) = 0089.g1:< D F E )>` to the angle :g:`< D F E ) <0089.g1:< D F E )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remaining angle :g:`< M L N ) <0089.g1:< M L N )>` is :eq:`equal <0089.g1:< M L N ) = 0089.g1:< E D F )>` to the angle :g:`< E D F ) <0089.g1:< E D F )>`. :just:`[G.45] <G.45>`

Therefore the triangle :g:`< L M N > <0089.g1:< L M N >>` is :eq:`equiangular <0089.g1:< A M B ) = 0089.g1:< D E F ); 0089.g1:< L N B ) = 0089.g1:< D F E ); 0089.g1:< M L N ) = 0089.g1:< E D F )>` with the
triangle :g:`< D E F > <0089.g1:< D E F >>`; and it has been circumscribed about the
circle :g:`( A B C ) <0089.g1:( A B C )>`.

Therefore about a given circle there has been circumscribed
a triangle equiangular with the given triangle.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. F.`
