G.484
=====

.. rubric:: `XI.37 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xi/propositions/37/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If four straight lines be proportional, the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described will also be proportional; and, if the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described be proportional, the straight lines will themselves also be proportional.

Let :g:`/ A B / <0362.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <0362.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`/ E F / <0362.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`/ G H / <0362.g1:/ G H />` be four straight lines in proportion, so that, as :g:`/ A B / <0362.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`/ C D / <0362.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`/ E F / <0362.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`/ G H / <0362.g1:/ G H />`; and let there be described on :g:`/ A B / <0362.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <0362.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`/ E F / <0362.g1:/ E F />`, :g:`/ G H / <0362.g1:/ G H />` the similar and similarly situated parallelepipedal solids :g:`< K A > <0362.g1:< K A >>`, :g:`< L C > <0362.g1:< L C >>`, :g:`< M E > <0362.g1:< M E >>`, :g:`< N G > <0362.g1:< N G >>`; I say that, as :g:`< K A > <0362.g1:< K A >>` is to :g:`< L C > <0362.g1:< L C >>`, so is :g:`< M E > <0362.g1:< M E >>` to :g:`< N G > <0362.g1:< N G >>`.

.. picture:: 0362.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 37 diagram

.. subclause::

   For, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`< K A > <0362.g1:< K A >>` is similar to :g:`< L C > <0362.g1:< L C >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< K A > <0362.g1:< K A >>` has to :g:`< L C > <0362.g1:< L C >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`/ A B / <0362.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`/ C D / <0362.g1:/ C D />`. :just:`[G.480] <G.480>`

.. assertion::

   For the same reason :g:`< M E > <0363.g1:< M E >>` also has to :g:`< N G > <0363.g1:< N G >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`/ E F / <0363.g1:/ E F />` has to :g:`/ G H / <0363.g1:/ G H />`. :just:`[G.480] <G.480>`

.. subclause::

   And, as :g:`/ A B / <0363.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`/ C D / <0363.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`/ E F / <0363.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`/ G H / <0363.g1:/ G H />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore also, as :g:`< A K > <0363.g1:< A K >>` is to :g:`< L C > <0363.g1:< L C >>`, so is :g:`< M E > <0363.g1:< M E >>` to :g:`< N G > <0363.g1:< N G >>`.

Next, as the solid :g:`< A K > <0363.g1:< A K >>` is to the solid :g:`< L C > <0363.g1:< L C >>`, so let the solid :g:`< M E > <0363.g1:< M E >>` be to the solid :g:`< N G > <0363.g1:< N G >>`; I say that, as the straight line :g:`/ A B / <0363.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`/ C D / <0363.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`/ E F / <0363.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`/ G H / <0363.g1:/ G H />`.

.. subclause::

   For since, again, :g:`< K A > <0363.g1:< K A >>` has to :g:`< L C > <0363.g1:< L C >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`/ A B / <0363.g1:/ A B />` has to :g:`/ C D / <0363.g1:/ C D />`, :just:`[G.480] <G.480>` and :g:`< M E > <0363.g1:< M E >>` also has to :g:`< N G > <0363.g1:< N G >>` the ratio triplicate of that which :g:`/ E F / <0363.g1:/ E F />` has to :g:`/ G H / <0363.g1:/ G H />`, :just:`[G.480] <G.480>` and, as :g:`< K A > <0363.g1:< K A >>` is to :g:`< L C > <0363.g1:< L C >>`, so is :g:`< M E > <0363.g1:< M E >>` to :g:`< N G > <0363.g1:< N G >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as :g:`/ A B / <0363.g1:/ A B />` is to :g:`/ C D / <0363.g1:/ C D />`, so is :g:`/ E F / <0363.g1:/ E F />` to :g:`/ G H / <0363.g1:/ G H />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.

.. qed::

   Q. E. D.
