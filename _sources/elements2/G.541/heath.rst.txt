G.541 parallelepipedal solids equal — Heath
===========================================

.. rubric:: `XI.31 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xi/propositions/31/>`_

.. enunciation::

   Parallelepipedal :term:`solids <G.529>` which are on equal bases and of the same :term:`height <G.209>` are equal to one another.

Let the parallelepipedal solids :g:`< A E > <g1:< A E >>`, :g:`< C F > <g1:< C F >>`, of the same height, be on :eq:`equal <g1:< A B > = g1:< C D >>` bases :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>`, :g:`< C D > <g1:< C D >>`.

I say that the solid :g:`< A E > <g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< A E > = g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< C F > <g1:< C F >>`.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 31

First, let the sides which stand up, :g:`/ H K / <g1:/ H K />`, :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />`, :g:`/ L M / <g1:/ L M />`, :g:`/ P Q / <g1:/ P Q />`, :g:`/ D F / <g1:/ D F />`, :g:`/ C O / <g1:/ C O />`, :g:`/ R S / <g1:/ R S />`, be at :eq:`right angles <g1:/ H K / = g1:< A B >; g1:/ B E / = g1:< A B >; g1:/ A G / = g1:< A B >; g1:/ L M / = g1:< A B >; g1:/ P Q / = g1:< C D >; g1:/ D F / = g1:< C D >; g1:/ C O / = g1:< C D >; g1:/ R S / = g1:< C D >>` to the bases :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>`, :g:`< C D > <g1:< C D >>`; let the :term:`straight line <G.4>` :g:`/ R T / <g1:/ R T />` be produced in a straight line with :g:`/ C R / <g1:/ C R />`; on the straight line :g:`/ R T / <g1:/ R T />`, and at the :term:`point <G.1>` :g:`R <g1:R>` on it, let the :term:`angle <G.22>` :g:`< T R U ) <g1:< T R U )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <g1:< T R U ) = g1:< A L B )>` to the angle :g:`< A L B ) <g1:< A L B )>`, :just:`[G.48] <G.48>` let :g:`/ R T / <g1:/ R T />` be made :eq:`equal <g1:/ R T / = g1:/ A L />` to :g:`/ A L / <g1:/ A L />`, and :g:`/ R U / <g1:/ R U />` :eq:`equal <g1:/ R U / = g1:/ L B />` to :g:`/ L B / <g1:/ L B />`, and let the base :g:`< R W > <g1:< R W >>` and the solid :g:`< X U > <g1:< X U >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two sides :g:`/ T R / <g1:/ T R />`, :g:`/ R U / <g1:/ R U />` are :eq:`equal <g1:/ T R / = g1:/ A L /; g1:/ R U / = g1:/ L B />` to the two sides :g:`/ A L / <g1:/ A L />`, :g:`/ L B / <g1:/ L B />`, and they contain equal angles,

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`< R W > <g1:< R W >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< R W > = g1:< H L >>` and similar to the parallelogram :g:`< H L > <g1:< H L >>`.

.. subclause::

   Since again :g:`/ A L / <g1:/ A L />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ A L / = g1:/ R T /; g1:/ L M / = g1:/ R S />` to :g:`/ R T / <g1:/ R T />`, and :g:`/ L M / <g1:/ L M />` to :g:`/ R S / <g1:/ R S />`, and they contain :term:`right angles <G.32>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`< R X > <g1:< R X >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< R X > = g1:< A M >>` and similar to the parallelogram :g:`< A M > <g1:< A M >>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason :g:`< L E > <g1:< L E >>` is also :eq:`equal <g1:< L E > = g1:< S U >>` and similar to :g:`< S U > <g1:< S U >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore three parallelograms of the solid :g:`< A E > <g1:< A E >>` are :term:`equal and similar <G.535>` to three parallelograms of the solid :g:`< X U > <g1:< X U >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the former three are equal and similar to the three opposite, and the latter three to the three opposite; :just:`[G.530] <G.530>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole parallelepipedal solid :g:`< A E > <g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< A E > = g1:< X U >>` to the whole parallelepipedal solid :g:`< X U > <g1:< X U >>`. :just:`[G.535] <G.535>`

Let :g:`/ D R / <g1:/ D R />`, :g:`/ W U / <g1:/ W U />` be drawn through and meet one another at :g:`Y <g1:Y>`, let :g:`/ a T b / <g1:/ a T b />` be drawn through :g:`T <g1:T>` :term:`parallel <G.52>` to :g:`/ D Y / <g1:/ D Y />`, let :g:`/ P D / <g1:/ P D />` be produced to :g:`a <g1:a>`, and let the solids :g:`< Y X > <g1:< Y X >>`, :g:`< R I > <g1:< R I >>` be completed.

.. assertion::

   Then the solid :g:`< X Y > <g1:< X Y >>`, of which the parallelogram :g:`< R X > <g1:< R X >>` is the base and :g:`< Y c > <g1:< Y c >>` its opposite, is :eq:`equal <g1:< X Y > = g1:< X U >>` to the solid :g:`< X U > <g1:< X U >>` of which the parallelogram :g:`< R X > <g1:< R X >>` is the base and :g:`< U V > <g1:< U V >>` its opposite, for they are on the same base :g:`< R X > <g1:< R X >>` and of the same height, and the :term:`extremities <G.6>` of their sides which stand up, namely :g:`/ R Y / <g1:/ R Y />`, :g:`/ R U / <g1:/ R U />`, :g:`/ T b / <g1:/ T b />`, :g:`/ T W / <g1:/ T W />`, :g:`/ S e / <g1:/ S e />`, :g:`/ S d / <g1:/ S d />`, :g:`/ X c / <g1:/ X c />`, :g:`/ X V / <g1:/ X V />`, are on the same straight lines :g:`/ Y W / <g1:/ Y W />`, :g:`/ e V / <g1:/ e V />`. :just:`[G.539] <G.539>`

.. subclause::

   But the solid :g:`< X U > <g1:< X U >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< X U > = g1:< A E >>` to :g:`< A E > <g1:< A E >>` :

.. assertion::

   therefore the solid :g:`< X Y > <g1:< X Y >>` is also :eq:`equal <g1:< X Y > = g1:< A E >>` to the solid :g:`< A E > <g1:< A E >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the parallelogram :g:`< R U W T > <g1:< R U W T >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< R U W T > = g1:< Y T >>` to the parallelogram :g:`< Y T > <g1:< Y T >>` for they are on the same base :g:`/ R T / <g1:/ R T />` and in the same parallels :g:`/ R T / <g1:/ R T />`, :g:`/ Y W / <g1:/ Y W />`, :just:`[G.64] <G.64>` while :g:`< R U W T > <g1:< R U W T >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< R U W T > = g1:< C D >>` to :g:`< C D > <g1:< C D >>`, since it is also :eq:`equal <g1:< R U W T > = g1:< A B >>` to :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`< Y T > <g1:< Y T >>` is also :eq:`equal <g1:< Y T > = g1:< C D >>` to :g:`< C D > <g1:< C D >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< D T > <g1:< D T >>` is another parallelogram;

.. assertion:: [G.183] <G.183>

   therefore, as the base :g:`< C D > <g1:< C D >>` is to :g:`< D T > <g1:< D T >>`, so is :g:`< Y T > <g1:< Y T >>` to :g:`< D T > <g1:< D T >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`< C I > <g1:< C I >>` has been cut by the :term:`plane <G.7>` :g:`< R F > <g1:< R F >>` which is parallel to opposite planes,

.. assertion::

   as the base :g:`< C D > <g1:< C D >>` is to the base :g:`< D T > <g1:< D T >>`, so is the solid :g:`< C F > <g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< R I > <g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[G.531] <G.531>`

.. subclause::

   For the same reason, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`< Y I > <g2:< Y I >>` has been cut by the plane :g:`< R X > <g2:< R X >>` which is parallel to opposite planes,

.. assertion::

   as the base :g:`< Y T > <g2:< Y T >>` is to the base :g:`< T D > <g2:< T D >>`, so is the solid :g:`< Y X > <g2:< Y X >>` to the solid :g:`< R I > <g2:< R I >>`. :just:`[G.531] <G.531>`

.. subclause::

   But, as the base :g:`< C D > <g2:< C D >>` is to :g:`< D T > <g2:< D T >>`, so is :g:`< Y T > <g2:< Y T >>` to :g:`< D T > <g2:< D T >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the solid :g:`< C F > <g2:< C F >>` is to the solid :g:`< R I > <g2:< R I >>`, so is the solid :g:`< Y X > <g2:< Y X >>` to :g:`< R I > <g2:< R I >>`. :just:`[G.189] <G.189>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the solids :g:`< C F > <g2:< C F >>`, :g:`< Y X > <g2:< Y X >>` has to :g:`< R I > <g2:< R I >>` the :term:`same ratio <G.176>`; therefore the solid :g:`< C F > <g2:< C F >>` is :eq:`equal <g2:< C F > = g2:< Y X >>` to the solid :g:`< Y X > <g2:< Y X >>`. :just:`[G.187] <G.187>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< Y X > <g2:< Y X >>` was proved :eq:`equal <g2:< Y X > = g2:< A E >>` to :g:`< A E > <g2:< A E >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< A E > <g2:< A E >>` is also :eq:`equal <g2:< A E > = g2:< C F >>` to :g:`< C F > <g2:< C F >>`.

Next, let the sides standing up, :g:`/ A G / <g2:/ A G />`, :g:`/ H K / <g2:/ H K />`, :g:`/ B E / <g2:/ B E />`, :g:`/ L M / <g2:/ L M />`, :g:`/ C N / <g2:/ C N />`, :g:`/ P Q / <g2:/ P Q />`, :g:`/ D F / <g2:/ D F />`, :g:`/ R S / <g2:/ R S />`, not be at right angles to the bases :g:`< A B > <g2:< A B >>`, :g:`< C D > <g2:< C D >>`; I say again that the solid :g:`< A E > <g2:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <g2:< A E > = g2:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< C F > <g2:< C F >>`.

.. picture:: g2.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 31, Case 2

For from the points :g:`K <g2:K>`, :g:`E <g2:E>`, :g:`G <g2:G>`, :g:`M <g2:M>`, :g:`Q <g2:Q>`, :g:`F <g2:F>`, :g:`N <g2:N>`, :g:`S <g2:S>` let :g:`/ K O / <g2:/ K O />`, :g:`/ E T / <g2:/ E T />`, :g:`/ G U / <g2:/ G U />`, :g:`/ M V / <g2:/ M V />`, :g:`/ Q W / <g2:/ Q W />`, :g:`/ F X / <g2:/ F X />`, :g:`/ N Y / <g2:/ N Y />`, :g:`/ S I / <g2:/ S I />` be drawn perpendicular to the plane of reference, and let them meet the plane at the points :g:`O <g2:O>`, :g:`T <g2:T>`, :g:`U <g2:U>`, :g:`V <g2:V>`, :g:`W <g2:W>`, :g:`X <g2:X>`, :g:`Y <g2:Y>`, :g:`I <g2:I>`, and let :g:`/ O T / <g2:/ O T />`, :g:`/ O U / <g2:/ O U />`, :g:`/ U V / <g2:/ U V />`, :g:`/ T V / <g2:/ T V />`, :g:`/ W X / <g2:/ W X />`, :g:`/ W Y / <g2:/ W Y />`, :g:`/ Y I / <g2:/ Y I />`, :g:`/ I X / <g2:/ I X />` be joined.

.. assertion::

   Then the solid :g:`< K V > <g2:< K V >>` is :eq:`equal <g2:< K V > = g2:< Q I >>` to the solid :g:`< Q I > <g2:< Q I >>`, for they are on the :eq:`equal <g2:< K M > = g2:< Q S >>` bases :g:`< K M > <g2:< K M >>`, :g:`< Q S > <g2:< Q S >>` and of the same height, and their sides which stand up are at right angles to their bases. :just:`[First part of this Prop.]`

.. subclause::

   But the solid :g:`< K V > <g2:< K V >>` is :eq:`equal <g2:< K V > = g2:< A E >; g2:< Q I > = g2:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< A E > <g2:< A E >>`, and :g:`< Q I > <g2:< Q I >>` to :g:`< C F > <g2:< C F >>`; for they are on the same base and of the same height, while the extremities of their sides which stand up are not on the same straight lines. :just:`[G.540] <G.540>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the solid :g:`< A E > <g2:< A E >>` is also :eq:`equal <g2:< A E > = g2:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< C F > <g2:< C F >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
