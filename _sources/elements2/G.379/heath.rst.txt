G.379 magnitudes ratio number — Heath
=====================================

.. rubric:: `X.8 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_x/propositions/008/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If two magnitudes have not to one another the :term:`ratio <G.173>` which a :term:`number <G.248>` has to a number, the magnitudes will be :term:`incommensurable <G.372>`.

For let the two magnitudes :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>` not have to one another the ratio which a number has to a number; I say that the magnitudes :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>` are incommensurable.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 8 diagram

.. subclause::

   For, if they are commensurable, :g:`A <g1:A>` will have to :g:`B <g1:B>` the ratio which a number has to a number. :just:`[G.376] <G.376>`

.. subclause::

   But it has not;

.. assertion::

   therefore the magnitudes :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>` are incommensurable.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc.
