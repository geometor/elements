G.478 area contained rational — Heath
=====================================

.. rubric:: `X.92 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_x/propositions/092/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If an area be contained by a :term:`rational <G.392>` :term:`straight line <G.4>` and a :term:`second apotome <G.456>`, the "side" of the area is a :term:`first apotome <G.454>` of a medial straight line.

For let the area :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` be contained by the rational straight line :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` and the second apotome :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />`;

I say that the "side" of the area :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` is a first apotome of a medial straight line.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Book X, Proposition 92

For let :g:`/ D G / <g1:/ D G />` be the annex to :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />`, :g:`/ G D / <g1:/ G D />` are rational straight lines :term:`commensurable in square only <G.380>`, :just:`[G.453] <G.453>` and the annex :g:`/ D G / <g1:/ D G />` is :term:`commensurable <G.372>` with the rational straight line :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` set out, while the :term:`square <G.63>` on the whole :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on the annex :g:`/ G D / <g1:/ G D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />`. :just:`[G.456] <G.456>`

.. subclause::

   Since then the square on :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` is greater than the square on :g:`/ G D / <g1:/ G D />` by the square on a straight line commensurable with :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore, if there be applied to :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the fourth :term:`part of <G.253>` the square on :g:`/ G D / <g1:/ G D />` and deficient by a square :term:`figure <G.9>`, it divides it into commensurable parts. :just:`[G.389] <G.389>`

Let then :g:`/ D G / <g1:/ D G />` be :eq:`bisected <g1:/ D E / = g1:/ E G />` at :g:`E <g1:E>`, let there be applied to :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` a parallelogram equal to the square on :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />` and deficient by a square figure, and let it be the :term:`rectangle <G.81>` :g:`/ A F / <g1:/ A F />`, :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ A F / <g1:/ A F />` is commensurable in length with :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` is also commensurable in length with each of the straight lines :g:`/ A F / <g1:/ A F />`, :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />`. :just:`[G.387] <G.387>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` is rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the straight lines :g:`/ A F / <g1:/ A F />`, :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />` is also rational and incommensurable in length with :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`; :just:`[G.385] <G.385>` therefore each of the rectangles :g:`< A I > <g1:< A I >>`, :g:`< F K > <g1:< F K >>` is medial. :just:`[G.395] <G.395>`

.. subclause::

   Again, since :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` is commensurable with :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ D G / <g1:/ D G />` is also commensurable with each of the straight lines :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />`. :just:`[G.387] <G.387>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`/ D G / <g1:/ D G />` is commensurable in length with :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the rectangles :g:`< D H > <g1:< D H >>`, :g:`< E K > <g1:< E K >>` is rational. :just:`[G.393] <G.393>`

Let then the square :g:`< L M > <g1:< L M >>` be constructed :eq:`equal <g1:< L M > = g1:< A I >>` to :g:`< A I > <g1:< A I >>`, and let there be subtracted :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>` :eq:`equal <g1:< N O > = g1:< F K >>` to :g:`< F K > <g1:< F K >>` and being about the same :term:`angle <G.22>` with :g:`< L M > <g1:< L M >>`, namely the angle :g:`< L P M ) <g1:< L P M )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares :g:`< L M > <g1:< L M >>`, :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>` are about the same :term:`diameter <G.61>`. :just:`[G.238] <G.238>`

Let :g:`/ P R / <g1:/ P R />` be their diameter, and let the figure be drawn.

.. subclause::

   Since then :g:`< A I > <g1:< A I >>`, :g:`< F K > <g1:< F K >>` are medial and are equal to the squares on :g:`/ L P / <g1:/ L P />`, :g:`/ P N / <g1:/ P N />`, the squares on :g:`/ L P / <g1:/ L P />`, :g:`/ P N / <g1:/ P N />` are also medial;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ L P / <g1:/ L P />`, :g:`/ P N / <g1:/ P N />` are also medial straight lines commensurable in square only.

.. subclause::

   And, since the rectangle :g:`/ A F / <g1:/ A F />`, :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />` is equal to the square on :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />`,

.. assertion:: [G.228] <G.228>

   therefore, as :g:`/ A F / <g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />` to :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />`,

.. subclause::

   while, as :g:`/ A F / <g1:/ A F />` is to :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />`, so is :g:`< A I > <g1:< A I >>` to :g:`< E K > <g1:< E K >>`, and, as :g:`/ E G / <g1:/ E G />` is to :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />`, so is :g:`< E K > <g1:< E K >>` to :g:`< F K > <g1:< F K >>`; :just:`[G.210] <G.210>`

.. assertion:: [G.189] <G.189>

   therefore :g:`< E K > <g1:< E K >>` is a mean :term:`proportional <G.180>` between :g:`< A I > <g1:< A I >>`, :g:`< F K > <g1:< F K >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< M N > <g1:< M N >>` is also a mean proportional between the squares :g:`< L M > <g1:< L M >>`, :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>`, and :g:`< A I > <g1:< A I >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< A I > = g1:< L M >; g1:< F K > = g1:< N O >>` to :g:`< L M > <g1:< L M >>`, and :g:`< F K > <g1:< F K >>` to :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< M N > <g1:< M N >>` is also :eq:`equal <g1:< M N > = g1:< E K >>` to :g:`< E K > <g1:< E K >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< D H > <g1:< D H >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< D H > = g1:< E K >>` to :g:`< E K > <g1:< E K >>`, and :g:`< L O > <g1:< L O >>` :eq:`equal <g1:< L O > = g1:< M N >>` to :g:`< M N > <g1:< M N >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`< D K > <g1:< D K >>` is equal to the :term:`gnomon <G.86>` :g:`< U V W > <g1:< U V W >>` and :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>`.

.. subclause::

   Since then the whole :g:`< A K > <g1:< A K >>` is equal to :g:`< L M > <g1:< L M >>`, :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>`, and, in these, :g:`< D K > <g1:< D K >>` is equal to the gnomon :g:`< U V W > <g1:< U V W >>` and :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< A B > = g1:< T S >>` to :g:`< T S > <g1:< T S >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< T S > <g1:< T S >>` is the square on :g:`/ L N / <g1:/ L N />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`/ L N / <g1:/ L N />` is equal to the area :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>`; therefore :g:`/ L N / <g1:/ L N />` is the "side" of the area :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>`.

I say that :g:`/ L N / <g1:/ L N />` is a first apotome of a medial straight line.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`< E K > <g1:< E K >>` is rational and is :eq:`equal <g1:< E K > = g1:< L O >>` to :g:`< L O > <g1:< L O >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< L O > <g1:< L O >>`, that is, the rectangle :g:`/ L P / <g1:/ L P />`, :g:`/ P N / <g1:/ P N />`, is rational.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>` was proved medial;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< L O > <g1:< L O >>` is incommensurable with :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>`.

.. subclause:: [G.210] <G.210>

   But, as :g:`< L O > <g1:< L O >>` is to :g:`< N O > <g1:< N O >>`, so is :g:`/ L P / <g1:/ L P />` to :g:`/ P N / <g1:/ P N />`;

.. assertion:: [G.382] <G.382>

   therefore :g:`/ L P / <g1:/ L P />`, :g:`/ P N / <g1:/ P N />` are incommensurable in length.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`/ L P / <g1:/ L P />`, :g:`/ P N / <g1:/ P N />` are medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a rational rectangle; therefore :g:`/ L N / <g1:/ L N />` is a first apotome of a medial straight line. :just:`[G.455] <G.455>`

.. subclause::

   And it is the "side" of the area :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore the "side" of the area :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` is a first apotome of a medial straight line. :just:`Q. E. D.`
