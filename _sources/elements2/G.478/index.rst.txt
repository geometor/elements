G.478
=====

.. rubric:: `XI.31 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xi/propositions/31/>`_

.. enunciation::

   Parallelepipedal solids which are on equal bases and of the same height are equal to one another.

Let the parallelepipedal solids :g:`< A E > <0341.g1:< A E >>`, :g:`< C F > <0341.g1:< C F >>`, of the same height, be on :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< A B > = 0341.g1:< C D >>` bases :g:`< A B > <0341.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`< C D > <0341.g1:< C D >>`.

I say that the solid :g:`< A E > <0341.g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< A E > = 0341.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< C F > <0341.g1:< C F >>`.

.. picture:: 0341.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 31

First, let the sides which stand up, :g:`/ H K / <0341.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`/ B E / <0341.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ A G / <0341.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`/ L M / <0341.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`/ P Q / <0341.g1:/ P Q />`, :g:`/ D F / <0341.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`/ C O / <0341.g1:/ C O />`, :g:`/ R S / <0341.g1:/ R S />`, be at :eq:`right angles <0341.g1:/ H K / = 0341.g1:< A B >; 0341.g1:/ B E / = 0341.g1:< A B >; 0341.g1:/ A G / = 0341.g1:< A B >; 0341.g1:/ L M / = 0341.g1:< A B >; 0341.g1:/ P Q / = 0341.g1:< C D >; 0341.g1:/ D F / = 0341.g1:< C D >; 0341.g1:/ C O / = 0341.g1:< C D >; 0341.g1:/ R S / = 0341.g1:< C D >>` to the bases :g:`< A B > <0341.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`< C D > <0341.g1:< C D >>`; let the straight line :g:`/ R T / <0341.g1:/ R T />` be produced in a straight line with :g:`/ C R / <0341.g1:/ C R />`; on the straight line :g:`/ R T / <0341.g1:/ R T />`, and at the point :g:`R <0341.g1:R>` on it, let the angle :g:`< T R U ) <0341.g1:< T R U )>` be constructed :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< T R U ) = 0341.g1:< A L B )>` to the angle :g:`< A L B ) <0341.g1:< A L B )>`, :just:`[G.34] <G.34>` let :g:`/ R T / <0341.g1:/ R T />` be made :eq:`equal <0341.g1:/ R T / = 0341.g1:/ A L />` to :g:`/ A L / <0341.g1:/ A L />`, and :g:`/ R U / <0341.g1:/ R U />` :eq:`equal <0341.g1:/ R U / = 0341.g1:/ L B />` to :g:`/ L B / <0341.g1:/ L B />`, and let the base :g:`< R W > <0341.g1:< R W >>` and the solid :g:`< X U > <0341.g1:< X U >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two sides :g:`/ T R / <0341.g1:/ T R />`, :g:`/ R U / <0341.g1:/ R U />` are :eq:`equal <0341.g1:/ T R / = 0341.g1:/ A L /; 0341.g1:/ R U / = 0341.g1:/ L B />` to the two sides :g:`/ A L / <0341.g1:/ A L />`, :g:`/ L B / <0341.g1:/ L B />`, and they contain equal angles,

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`< R W > <0341.g1:< R W >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R W > = 0341.g1:< H L >>` and similar to the parallelogram :g:`< H L > <0341.g1:< H L >>`.

.. subclause::

   Since again :g:`/ A L / <0341.g1:/ A L />` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:/ A L / = 0341.g1:/ R T /; 0341.g1:/ L M / = 0341.g1:/ R S />` to :g:`/ R T / <0341.g1:/ R T />`, and :g:`/ L M / <0341.g1:/ L M />` to :g:`/ R S / <0341.g1:/ R S />`, and they contain right angles,

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`< R X > <0341.g1:< R X >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R X > = 0341.g1:< A M >>` and similar to the parallelogram :g:`< A M > <0341.g1:< A M >>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason :g:`< L E > <0341.g1:< L E >>` is also :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< L E > = 0341.g1:< S U >>` and similar to :g:`< S U > <0341.g1:< S U >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore three parallelograms of the solid :g:`< A E > <0341.g1:< A E >>` are equal and similar to three parallelograms of the solid :g:`< X U > <0341.g1:< X U >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the former three are equal and similar to the three opposite, and the latter three to the three opposite; :just:`[G.469] <G.469>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole parallelepipedal solid :g:`< A E > <0341.g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< A E > = 0341.g1:< X U >>` to the whole parallelepipedal solid :g:`< X U > <0341.g1:< X U >>`. :just:`[G.474] <G.474>`

Let :g:`/ D R / <0341.g1:/ D R />`, :g:`/ W U / <0341.g1:/ W U />` be drawn through and meet one another at :g:`Y <0341.g1:Y>`, let :g:`/ a T b / <0341.g1:/ a T b />` be drawn through :g:`T <0341.g1:T>` parallel to :g:`/ D Y / <0341.g1:/ D Y />`, let :g:`/ P D / <0341.g1:/ P D />` be produced to :g:`a <0341.g1:a>`, and let the solids :g:`< Y X > <0341.g1:< Y X >>`, :g:`< R I > <0341.g1:< R I >>` be completed.

.. assertion::

   Then the solid :g:`< X Y > <0341.g1:< X Y >>`, of which the parallelogram :g:`< R X > <0341.g1:< R X >>` is the base and :g:`< Y c > <0341.g1:< Y c >>` its opposite, is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< X Y > = 0341.g1:< X U >>` to the solid :g:`< X U > <0341.g1:< X U >>` of which the parallelogram :g:`< R X > <0341.g1:< R X >>` is the base and :g:`< U V > <0341.g1:< U V >>` its opposite, for they are on the same base :g:`< R X > <0341.g1:< R X >>` and of the same height, and the extremities of their sides which stand up, namely :g:`/ R Y / <0341.g1:/ R Y />`, :g:`/ R U / <0341.g1:/ R U />`, :g:`/ T b / <0341.g1:/ T b />`, :g:`/ T W / <0341.g1:/ T W />`, :g:`/ S e / <0341.g1:/ S e />`, :g:`/ S d / <0341.g1:/ S d />`, :g:`/ X c / <0341.g1:/ X c />`, :g:`/ X V / <0341.g1:/ X V />`, are on the same straight lines :g:`/ Y W / <0341.g1:/ Y W />`, :g:`/ e V / <0341.g1:/ e V />`. :just:`[G.476] <G.476>`

.. subclause::

   But the solid :g:`< X U > <0341.g1:< X U >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< X U > = 0341.g1:< A E >>` to :g:`< A E > <0341.g1:< A E >>` :

.. assertion::

   therefore the solid :g:`< X Y > <0341.g1:< X Y >>` is also :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< X Y > = 0341.g1:< A E >>` to the solid :g:`< A E > <0341.g1:< A E >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the parallelogram :g:`< R U W T > <0341.g1:< R U W T >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R U W T > = 0341.g1:< Y T >>` to the parallelogram :g:`< Y T > <0341.g1:< Y T >>` for they are on the same base :g:`/ R T / <0341.g1:/ R T />` and in the same parallels :g:`/ R T / <0341.g1:/ R T />`, :g:`/ Y W / <0341.g1:/ Y W />`, :just:`[G.48] <G.48>` while :g:`< R U W T > <0341.g1:< R U W T >>` is :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R U W T > = 0341.g1:< C D >>` to :g:`< C D > <0341.g1:< C D >>`, since it is also :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< R U W T > = 0341.g1:< A B >>` to :g:`< A B > <0341.g1:< A B >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`< Y T > <0341.g1:< Y T >>` is also :eq:`equal <0341.g1:< Y T > = 0341.g1:< C D >>` to :g:`< C D > <0341.g1:< C D >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< D T > <0341.g1:< D T >>` is another parallelogram;

.. assertion:: [G.142] <G.142>

   therefore, as the base :g:`< C D > <0341.g1:< C D >>` is to :g:`< D T > <0341.g1:< D T >>`, so is :g:`< Y T > <0341.g1:< Y T >>` to :g:`< D T > <0341.g1:< D T >>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`< C I > <0341.g1:< C I >>` has been cut by the plane :g:`< R F > <0341.g1:< R F >>` which is parallel to opposite planes,

.. assertion::

   as the base :g:`< C D > <0341.g1:< C D >>` is to the base :g:`< D T > <0341.g1:< D T >>`, so is the solid :g:`< C F > <0341.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< R I > <0341.g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[G.470] <G.470>`

.. subclause::

   For the same reason, since the parallelepipedal solid :g:`< Y I > <0343.g1:< Y I >>` has been cut by the plane :g:`< R X > <0343.g1:< R X >>` which is parallel to opposite planes,

.. assertion::

   as the base :g:`< Y T > <0343.g1:< Y T >>` is to the base :g:`< T D > <0343.g1:< T D >>`, so is the solid :g:`< Y X > <0343.g1:< Y X >>` to the solid :g:`< R I > <0343.g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[G.470] <G.470>`

.. subclause::

   But, as the base :g:`< C D > <0343.g1:< C D >>` is to :g:`< D T > <0343.g1:< D T >>`, so is :g:`< Y T > <0343.g1:< Y T >>` to :g:`< D T > <0343.g1:< D T >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore also, as the solid :g:`< C F > <0343.g1:< C F >>` is to the solid :g:`< R I > <0343.g1:< R I >>`, so is the solid :g:`< Y X > <0343.g1:< Y X >>` to :g:`< R I > <0343.g1:< R I >>`. :just:`[G.148] <G.148>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the solids :g:`< C F > <0343.g1:< C F >>`, :g:`< Y X > <0343.g1:< Y X >>` has to :g:`< R I > <0343.g1:< R I >>` the same ratio; therefore the solid :g:`< C F > <0343.g1:< C F >>` is :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< C F > = 0343.g1:< Y X >>` to the solid :g:`< Y X > <0343.g1:< Y X >>`. :just:`[G.146] <G.146>`

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< Y X > <0343.g1:< Y X >>` was proved :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< Y X > = 0343.g1:< A E >>` to :g:`< A E > <0343.g1:< A E >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< A E > <0343.g1:< A E >>` is also :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< A E > = 0343.g1:< C F >>` to :g:`< C F > <0343.g1:< C F >>`.

Next, let the sides standing up, :g:`/ A G / <0343.g1:/ A G />`, :g:`/ H K / <0343.g1:/ H K />`, :g:`/ B E / <0343.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ L M / <0343.g1:/ L M />`, :g:`/ C N / <0343.g1:/ C N />`, :g:`/ P Q / <0343.g1:/ P Q />`, :g:`/ D F / <0343.g1:/ D F />`, :g:`/ R S / <0343.g1:/ R S />`, not be at right angles to the bases :g:`< A B > <0343.g1:< A B >>`, :g:`< C D > <0343.g1:< C D >>`; I say again that the solid :g:`< A E > <0343.g1:< A E >>` is :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< A E > = 0343.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< C F > <0343.g1:< C F >>`.

.. picture:: 0343.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 31, Case 2

For from the points :g:`K <0343.g1:K>`, :g:`E <0343.g1:E>`, :g:`G <0343.g1:G>`, :g:`M <0343.g1:M>`, :g:`Q <0343.g1:Q>`, :g:`F <0343.g1:F>`, :g:`N <0343.g1:N>`, :g:`S <0343.g1:S>` let :g:`/ K O / <0343.g1:/ K O />`, :g:`/ E T / <0343.g1:/ E T />`, :g:`/ G U / <0343.g1:/ G U />`, :g:`/ M V / <0343.g1:/ M V />`, :g:`/ Q W / <0343.g1:/ Q W />`, :g:`/ F X / <0343.g1:/ F X />`, :g:`/ N Y / <0343.g1:/ N Y />`, :g:`/ S I / <0343.g1:/ S I />` be drawn perpendicular to the plane of reference, and let them meet the plane at the points :g:`O <0343.g1:O>`, :g:`T <0343.g1:T>`, :g:`U <0343.g1:U>`, :g:`V <0343.g1:V>`, :g:`W <0343.g1:W>`, :g:`X <0343.g1:X>`, :g:`Y <0343.g1:Y>`, :g:`I <0343.g1:I>`, and let :g:`/ O T / <0343.g1:/ O T />`, :g:`/ O U / <0343.g1:/ O U />`, :g:`/ U V / <0343.g1:/ U V />`, :g:`/ T V / <0343.g1:/ T V />`, :g:`/ W X / <0343.g1:/ W X />`, :g:`/ W Y / <0343.g1:/ W Y />`, :g:`/ Y I / <0343.g1:/ Y I />`, :g:`/ I X / <0343.g1:/ I X />` be joined.

.. assertion::

   Then the solid :g:`< K V > <0343.g1:< K V >>` is :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< K V > = 0343.g1:< Q I >>` to the solid :g:`< Q I > <0343.g1:< Q I >>`, for they are on the :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< K M > = 0343.g1:< Q S >>` bases :g:`< K M > <0343.g1:< K M >>`, :g:`< Q S > <0343.g1:< Q S >>` and of the same height, and their sides which stand up are at right angles to their bases. :just:`[First part of this Prop.]`

.. subclause::

   But the solid :g:`< K V > <0343.g1:< K V >>` is :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< K V > = 0343.g1:< A E >; 0343.g1:< Q I > = 0343.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< A E > <0343.g1:< A E >>`, and :g:`< Q I > <0343.g1:< Q I >>` to :g:`< C F > <0343.g1:< C F >>`; for they are on the same base and of the same height, while the extremities of their sides which stand up are not on the same straight lines. :just:`[G.477] <G.477>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the solid :g:`< A E > <0343.g1:< A E >>` is also :eq:`equal <0343.g1:< A E > = 0343.g1:< C F >>` to the solid :g:`< C F > <0343.g1:< C F >>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
