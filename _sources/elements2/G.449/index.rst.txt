G.449
=====

.. rubric:: `XI.6 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xi/propositions/06/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines be at right angles to the same plane, the straight lines will be parallel.

For let the two straight lines :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <0287.g1:/ C D />` be at right angles to the plane of reference; I say that :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`/ C D / <0287.g1:/ C D />`.

.. picture:: 0287.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI - Proposition 6

For let them meet the plane of reference at the points :g:`B <0287.g1:B>`, :g:`D <0287.g1:D>`, let the straight line :g:`/ B D / <0287.g1:/ B D />` be joined, let :g:`/ D E / <0287.g1:/ D E />` be drawn, in the plane of reference, at right angles to :g:`/ B D / <0287.g1:/ B D />`, let :g:`/ D E / <0287.g1:/ D E />` be made :eq:`equal <0287.g1:/ D E / = 0287.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />`, and let :g:`/ B E / <0287.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ A E / <0287.g1:/ A E />`, :g:`/ A D / <0287.g1:/ A D />` be joined.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />` is at right angles to the plane of reference, it will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the plane of reference. :just:`[G.446] <G.446>`

.. subclause::

   But each of the straight lines :g:`/ B D / <0287.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`/ B E / <0287.g1:/ B E />` is in the plane of reference and meets :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore each of the angles :g:`< A B D ) <0287.g1:< A B D )>`, :g:`< A B E ) <0287.g1:< A B E )>` is :eq:`right <0287.g1:< A B D ) = 0287.g1:< A B E )>`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason each of the angles :g:`< C D B ) <0287.g1:< C D B )>`, :g:`< C D E ) <0287.g1:< C D E )>` is also :eq:`right <0287.g1:< C D B ) = 0287.g1:< C D E )>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />` is :eq:`equal <0287.g1:/ A B / = 0287.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`/ D E / <0287.g1:/ D E />`, and :g:`/ B D / <0287.g1:/ B D />` is common, the two sides :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B D / <0287.g1:/ B D />` are :eq:`equal <0287.g1:/ A B / = 0287.g1:/ E D />` to the two sides :g:`/ E D / <0287.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`/ D B / <0287.g1:/ D B />`; and they include :eq:`right angles <0287.g1:< A B D ) = 0287.g1:< E D B )>`;

.. assertion:: [G.11] <G.11>

   therefore the base :g:`/ A D / <0287.g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <0287.g1:/ A D / = 0287.g1:/ B E />` to the base :g:`/ B E / <0287.g1:/ B E />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />` is :eq:`equal <0287.g1:/ A B / = 0287.g1:/ D E />` to :g:`/ D E / <0287.g1:/ D E />`, while :g:`/ A D / <0287.g1:/ A D />` is also :eq:`equal <0287.g1:/ A D / = 0287.g1:/ B E />` to :g:`/ B E / <0287.g1:/ B E />`, the two sides :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B E / <0287.g1:/ B E />` are :eq:`equal <0287.g1:/ A B / = 0287.g1:/ E D /; 0287.g1:/ B E / = 0287.g1:/ D A />` to the two sides :g:`/ E D / <0287.g1:/ E D />`, :g:`/ D A / <0287.g1:/ D A />`; and :g:`/ A E / <0287.g1:/ A E />` is their common base;

.. assertion:: [G.15] <G.15>

   therefore the angle :g:`< A B E ) <0287.g1:< A B E )>` is :eq:`equal <0287.g1:< A B E ) = 0287.g1:< E D A )>` to the angle :g:`< E D A ) <0287.g1:< E D A )>`.

.. subclause::

   But the angle :g:`< A B E ) <0287.g1:< A B E )>` is right;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`< E D A ) <0287.g1:< E D A )>` is also right; therefore :g:`/ E D / <0287.g1:/ E D />` is at right angles to :g:`/ D A / <0287.g1:/ D A />`.

.. subclause::

   But it is also at right angles to each of the straight lines :g:`/ B D / <0287.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`/ D C / <0287.g1:/ D C />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ E D / <0287.g1:/ E D />` is set up at right angles to the three straight lines :g:`/ B D / <0287.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`/ D A / <0287.g1:/ D A />`, :g:`/ D C / <0287.g1:/ D C />` at their point of meeting; therefore the three straight lines :g:`/ B D / <0287.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`/ D A / <0287.g1:/ D A />`, :g:`/ D C / <0287.g1:/ D C />` are in one plane. :just:`[G.448] <G.448>`

.. subclause::

   But, in whatever plane :g:`/ D B / <0287.g1:/ D B />`, :g:`/ D A / <0287.g1:/ D A />` are, in that plane is :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />` also, for every triangle is in one plane; :just:`[G.444] <G.444>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the straight lines :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B D / <0287.g1:/ B D />`, :g:`/ D C / <0287.g1:/ D C />` are in one plane.

.. subclause::

   And each of the angles :g:`< A B D ) <0287.g1:< A B D )>`, :g:`< B D C ) <0287.g1:< B D C )>` is :eq:`right <0287.g1:< A B D ) = 0287.g1:< B D C )>`;

.. assertion:: [G.40] <G.40>

   therefore :g:`/ A B / <0287.g1:/ A B />` is parallel to :g:`/ C D / <0287.g1:/ C D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
