G.95 law of cosines acute — Code
================================

.. rubric:: `II.13 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_ii/propositions/13/>`_

.. enunciation::

   In :term:`acute-angled triangles <G.78>` the :term:`square <G.63>` on the side subtending the :term:`acute angle <G.77>` is less than the squares on the sides containing the acute angle by twice the :term:`rectangle contained <G.81>` by one of the sides about the acute angle, namely that on which the :term:`perpendicular <G.32>` falls, and the :term:`segment <G.4>` cut off within by the perpendicular towards the acute angle.
