G.596 icosahedron — Heath
=========================

.. rubric:: `XI.def.27 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xi/definitions/#xi-def-27>`_

.. enunciation::

   An :term:`**icosahedron** <icosahedron>` is a :term:`solid figure <G.529>` contained by twenty equal and :term:`equilateral triangles <G.13>`.
