G.554 pyramid triangular base — Code
====================================

.. rubric:: `XII.3 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xii/propositions/03/>`_

.. enunciation::

   Any :term:`pyramid <G.553>` which has a triangular base is divided into two pyramids :term:`equal and similar <G.535>` to one another, similar to the whole and having triangular bases, and into two equal :term:`prisms <G.537>`; and the two prisms are greater than the half of the whole pyramid.
