G.390 there unequal segments — Code
===================================

.. rubric:: `X.18 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_x/propositions/018/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If there be two unequal :term:`segments <G.4>`, and to the greater there be applied a parallelogram equal to the fourth :term:`part of <G.253>` the :term:`square <G.63>` on the less and deficient by a square :term:`figure <G.9>`, and if it divide it into parts which are :term:`incommensurable <G.372>`, the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a segment incommensurable with the greater. And, if the square on the greater be greater than the square on the less by the square on a segment incommensurable with the greater, and if there be applied to the greater a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on the less and deficient by a square figure, it divides it into parts which are incommensurable.
