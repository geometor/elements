==================================

.. rubric:: `VI.15 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_vi/propositions/15/>`_

.. enunciation::

   In equal :term:`triangles <G.12>` which have one :term:`angle <G.22>` equal to one angle the sides about the equal angles are :term:`reciprocally proportional <G.224>`; and those triangles which have one angle equal to one angle, and in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional, are equal.
