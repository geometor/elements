G.126
=====

.. rubric:: `IV.8 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_iv/propositions/08/>`_

.. enunciation::

   In a given square to inscribe a circle.

Let :g:`< A B C D > <0100.g1:< A B C D >>` be the given square;
thus it is required to inscribe a circle in the given square
:g:`< A B C D > <0100.g1:< A B C D >>`.

Let the straight lines :g:`/ A D / <0100.g1:/ A D />`, :g:`/ A B / <0100.g1:/ A B />` be
bisected at the points :g:`E <0100.g1:E>`, :g:`F <0100.g1:F>` respectively
:just:`[G.17] <G.17>`,
through :g:`E <0100.g1:E>` let :g:`/ E H / <0100.g1:/ E H />` be drawn parallel
to either :g:`/ A B / <0100.g1:/ A B />` or :g:`/ C D / <0100.g1:/ C D />`, and through
:g:`F <0100.g1:F>` let :g:`/ F K / <0100.g1:/ F K />` be drawn parallel to either
:g:`/ A D / <0100.g1:/ A D />` or :g:`/ B C / <0100.g1:/ B C />`; :just:`[G.44] <G.44>`
therefore each of the figures :g:`< A F K D > <0100.g1:< A F K D >>`, :g:`< F B C K > <0100.g1:< F B C K >>`,
:g:`< A B H E > <0100.g1:< A B H E >>`, :g:`< E H C D > <0100.g1:< E H C D >>`, :g:`< A F G E > <0100.g1:< A F G E >>`, :g:`< G H C K > <0100.g1:< G H C K >>`, :g:`< F B H G > <0100.g1:< F B H G >>`, :g:`< E G K D > <0100.g1:< E G K D >>` is a parallelogram,
and their opposite sides are evidently equal. :just:`[G.47] <G.47>`

.. picture:: 0100.g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition IV. 8 Diagram

Now, since :g:`/ A D / <0100.g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ A D / = 0100.g1:/ A B />` to :g:`/ A B / <0100.g1:/ A B />`,
and :g:`/ A E / <0100.g1:/ A E />` is half of :g:`/ A D / <0100.g1:/ A D />`, and :g:`/ A F / <0100.g1:/ A F />` half of :g:`/ A B / <0100.g1:/ A B />`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ A E / <0100.g1:/ A E />` is :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ A E / = 0100.g1:/ A F />` to :g:`/ A F / <0100.g1:/ A F />`,

so that the opposite sides are also equal;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ F G / <0100.g1:/ F G />` is :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ F G / = 0100.g1:/ G E />` to :g:`/ G E / <0100.g1:/ G E />`.

Similarly we can prove that each of the straight lines :g:`/ G H / <0100.g1:/ G H />`,
:g:`/ G K / <0100.g1:/ G K />` is :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ G H / = 0100.g1:/ G K / = 0100.g1:/ F G / = 0100.g1:/ G E />` to each of the straight lines :g:`/ F G / <0100.g1:/ F G />`, :g:`/ G E / <0100.g1:/ G E />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the four straight lines :g:`/ G E / <0100.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`/ G F / <0100.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`/ G H / <0100.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`/ G K / <0100.g1:/ G K />` are :eq:`equal <0100.g1:/ G E / = 0100.g1:/ G F / = 0100.g1:/ G H / = 0100.g1:/ G K />` to one another.

Therefore the circle described with centre :g:`G <0100.g1:G>` and distance
one of the straight lines :g:`/ G E / <0100.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`/ G F / <0100.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`/ G H / <0100.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`/ G K / <0100.g1:/ G K />` will pass also
through the remaining points.

And it will touch the straight lines :g:`/ A B / <0100.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B C / <0100.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`/ C D / <0100.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`/ D A / <0100.g1:/ D A />`,
because the angles at :g:`E <0100.g1:E>`, :g:`F <0100.g1:F>`, :g:`H <0100.g1:H>`, :g:`K <0100.g1:K>` are right.

For, if the circle cuts :g:`/ A B / <0100.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B C / <0100.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`/ C D / <0100.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`/ D A / <0100.g1:/ D A />`, the straight
line drawn at right angles to the diameter of the circle from
its extremity will fall within the circle: which was proved
absurd; :just:`[G.94] <G.94>`
therefore the circle described with centre :g:`G <0100.g1:G>` and distance
one of the straight lines :g:`/ G E / <0100.g1:/ G E />`, :g:`/ G F / <0100.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`/ G H / <0100.g1:/ G H />`, :g:`/ G K / <0100.g1:/ G K />` will not cut
the straight lines :g:`/ A B / <0100.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ B C / <0100.g1:/ B C />`, :g:`/ C D / <0100.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`/ D A / <0100.g1:/ D A />`.

Therefore it will touch them, and will have been inscribed
in the square :g:`< A B C D > <0100.g1:< A B C D >>`.

Therefore in the given square a circle has been inscribed.
