G.194
=====

.. rubric:: `VI.29 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_vi/propositions/29/>`_

.. enunciation::

   To a given straight line to apply a parallelogram equal to a given rectilineal figure and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure similar to a given one.

Let :g:`/ A B / <0271.g1:/ A B />` be the given straight line, :g:`< C > <0271.g1:< C >>` the given rectilineal figure to which the figure to be applied to :g:`/ A B / <0271.g1:/ A B />` is required to be equal, and :g:`< D > <0271.g1:< D >>` that to which the excess is required to be similar ;

thus it is required to apply to the straight line :g:`/ A B / <0271.g1:/ A B />` a parallelogram equal to the rectilineal figure :g:`< C > <0271.g1:< C >>` and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure similar to :g:`< D > <0271.g1:< D >>`.

.. picture:: 0271.g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 29

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`/ A B / <0271.g1:/ A B />` be :eq:`bisected <0271.g1:/ A E / = 0271.g1:/ E B />` at :g:`E <0271.g1:E>` ; let there be described on :g:`/ E B / <0271.g1:/ E B />` the parallelogram :g:`< B F > <0271.g1:< B F >>` similar and similarly situated to :g:`< D > <0271.g1:< D >>` ; and let :g:`< G H > <0271.g1:< G H >>` be constructed at once equal to the sum of :g:`< B F > <0271.g1:< B F >>`, :g:`< C > <0271.g1:< C >>` and similar and similarly situated to :g:`< D > <0271.g1:< D >>`. :just:`[G.190] <G.190>`

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`/ K H / <0271.g1:/ K H />` correspond to :g:`/ F L / <0271.g1:/ F L />` and :g:`/ K G / <0271.g1:/ K G />` to :g:`/ F E / <0271.g1:/ F E />`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`< G H > <0271.g1:< G H >>` is greater than :g:`< F B > <0271.g1:< F B >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ K H / <0271.g1:/ K H />` is also greater than :g:`/ F L / <0271.g1:/ F L />`, and :g:`/ K G / <0271.g1:/ K G />` than :g:`/ F E / <0271.g1:/ F E />`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`[ F L ] <0271.g1:[ F L ]>`, :g:`[ F E ] <0271.g1:[ F E ]>` be produced, let :g:`/ F L M / <0271.g1:/ F L M />` be :eq:`equal <0271.g1:/ F L M / = 0271.g1:/ K H />; 0271.g1:/ F E N / = 0271.g1:/ K G />` to :g:`/ K H / <0271.g1:/ K H />`, and :g:`/ F E N / <0271.g1:/ F E N />` to :g:`/ K G / <0271.g1:/ K G />`, and let :g:`< M N > <0271.g1:< M N >>` be completed ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< M N > <0271.g1:< M N >>` is both :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< M N > = 0271.g1:< G H >>` and similar to :g:`< G H > <0271.g1:< G H >>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`< G H > <0271.g1:< G H >>` is similar to :g:`< E L > <0271.g1:< E L >>` ;

.. assertion:: [G.186] <G.186>

   therefore :g:`< M N > <0271.g1:< M N >>` is also similar to :g:`< E L > <0271.g1:< E L >>` ;

.. assertion:: [G.191] <G.191>

   therefore :g:`< E L > <0271.g1:< E L >>` is about the same diameter with :g:`< M N > <0271.g1:< M N >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let their diameter :g:`/ F O / <0271.g1:/ F O />` be drawn, and let the figure be described.

.. subclause::

   Since :g:`< G H > <0271.g1:< G H >>` is equal to :g:`< E L > <0271.g1:< E L >>`, :g:`< C > <0271.g1:< C >>`, while :g:`< G H > <0271.g1:< G H >>` is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< G H > = 0271.g1:< M N >>` to :g:`< M N > <0271.g1:< M N >>`,

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< M N > <0271.g1:< M N >>` is also equal to :g:`< E L > <0271.g1:< E L >>`, :g:`< C > <0271.g1:< C >>`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`< E L > <0271.g1:< E L >>` be subtracted from each ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the remainder, the gnomon :g:`< X W V > <0271.g1:< X W V >>`, is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< X W V > = 0271.g1:< C >>` to :g:`< C > <0271.g1:< C >>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`/ A E / <0271.g1:/ A E />` is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:/ A E / = 0271.g1:/ E B />` to :g:`/ E B / <0271.g1:/ E B />`,

.. assertion::

   :g:`< A N > <0271.g1:< A N >>` is also :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< A N > = 0271.g1:< N B > = 0271.g1:< L P >>` to :g:`< N B > <0271.g1:< N B >>` :just:`[G.49] <G.49>`, that is, to :g:`< L P > <0271.g1:< L P >>` :just:`[G.56] <G.56>`.

.. subclause::

   Let :g:`< E O > <0271.g1:< E O >>` be added to each ;

.. assertion::

   therefore the whole :g:`< A O > <0271.g1:< A O >>` is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< A O > = 0271.g1:< V W X >>` to the gnomon :g:`< V W X > <0271.g1:< V W X >>`.

.. subclause::

   But the gnomon :g:`< V W X > <0271.g1:< V W X >>` is :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< V W X > = 0271.g1:< C >>` to :g:`< C > <0271.g1:< C >>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< A O > <0271.g1:< A O >>` is also :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< A O > = 0271.g1:< C >>` to :g:`< C > <0271.g1:< C >>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore to the given straight line :g:`/ A B / <0271.g1:/ A B />` there has been applied the parallelogram :g:`< A O > <0271.g1:< A O >>` :eq:`equal <0271.g1:< A O > = 0271.g1:< C >>` to the given rectilineal figure :g:`< C > <0271.g1:< C >>` and exceeding by a parallelogrammic figure :g:`< Q P > <0271.g1:< Q P >>` which is similar to :g:`< D > <0271.g1:< D >>`, since :g:`< P Q > <0271.g1:< P Q >>` is also similar to :g:`< E L > <0271.g1:< E L >>` :just:`[G.189] <G.189>`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. F.`
