G.415 segments incommensurable square — Code
============================================

.. rubric:: `X.41 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_x/propositions/041/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If two :term:`segments <G.4>` :term:`incommensurable in square <G.380>` which make the sum of the :term:`squares <G.63>` on them medial, and the :term:`rectangle contained <G.81>` by them medial and also :term:`incommensurable <G.372>` with the sum of the squares on them, be added together, the whole segment is :term:`irrational <G.391>`; and let it be called the **side of the sum of two medial areas**.
