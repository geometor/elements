G.234
=====

.. rubric:: `VII.33 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_vii/propositions/33/>`_

.. enunciation::

   Given as many numbers as we please, to find the least of those which have the same ratio with them.

Let :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` be the given numbers, as many as we please;

thus it is required to find the least of those which have the same ratio with :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>`.

.. subclause::

   :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` are either prime to one another or not.

.. subclause::

   Now, if :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` are prime to one another, they are the least of those which have the same ratio with them. :just:`[G.221] <G.221>`

.. picture:: 0339.g2.svg
   :alt: Proposition 33 diagram

But, if not, let :g:`D <0339.g2:D>` the greatest common measure of :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` be taken, :just:`[G.202] <G.202>`
and, as many times as :g:`D <0339.g2:D>` measures the numbers :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` respectively, so many units let there be in the numbers :g:`E <0339.g2:E>`, :g:`F <0339.g2:F>`, :g:`G <0339.g2:G>` respectively.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the numbers :g:`E <0339.g2:E>`, :g:`F <0339.g2:F>`, :g:`G <0339.g2:G>` measure the numbers :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` respectively according to the units in :g:`D <0339.g2:D>`. :just:`[G.216] <G.216>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`E <0339.g2:E>`, :g:`F <0339.g2:F>`, :g:`G <0339.g2:G>` measure :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` the same number of times; therefore :g:`E <0339.g2:E>`, :g:`F <0339.g2:F>`, :g:`G <0339.g2:G>` are in the same ratio with :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>`. :just:`[G.210] <G.210>`

I say next that they are the least that are in that ratio.

.. subclause::

   For, if :g:`E <0339.g2:E>`, :g:`F <0339.g2:F>`, :g:`G <0339.g2:G>` are not the least of those which have the same ratio with :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>`, there will be numbers less than :g:`E <0339.g2:E>`, :g:`F <0339.g2:F>`, :g:`G <0339.g2:G>` which are in the same ratio with :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>`.

Let them be :g:`H <0339.g2:H>`, :g:`K <0339.g2:K>`, :g:`L <0339.g2:L>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`H <0339.g2:H>` measures :g:`A <0339.g2:A>` the same number of times that the numbers :g:`K <0339.g2:K>`, :g:`L <0339.g2:L>` measure the numbers :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` respectively.

Now, as many times as :g:`H <0339.g2:H>` measures :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, so many units let there be in :g:`M <0339.g2:M>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the numbers :g:`K <0339.g2:K>`, :g:`L <0339.g2:L>` also measure the numbers :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` respectively according to the units in :g:`M <0339.g2:M>`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`H <0339.g2:H>` measures :g:`A <0339.g2:A>` according to the units in :g:`M <0339.g2:M>`,

.. assertion:: [G.216] <G.216>

   therefore :g:`M <0339.g2:M>` also measures :g:`A <0339.g2:A>` according to the units in :g:`H <0339.g2:H>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason :g:`M <0339.g2:M>` also measures the numbers :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` according to the units in the numbers :g:`K <0339.g2:K>`, :g:`L <0339.g2:L>` respectively ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`M <0339.g2:M>` measures :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>`.

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`H <0339.g2:H>` measures :g:`A <0339.g2:A>` according to the units in :g:`M <0339.g2:M>`,

.. assertion:: [G.222] <G.222>

   therefore :g:`H <0339.g2:H>` by multiplying :g:`M <0339.g2:M>` has made :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`.

.. subclause::

   For the same reason also

.. assertion::

   :g:`E <0339.g2:E>` by multiplying :g:`D <0339.g2:D>` has made :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the product of :g:`E <0339.g2:E>`, :g:`D <0339.g2:D>` is equal to the product of :g:`H <0339.g2:H>`, :g:`M <0339.g2:M>`.

.. assertion:: [G.219] <G.219>

   Therefore, as :g:`E <0339.g2:E>` is to :g:`H <0339.g2:H>`, so is :g:`M <0339.g2:M>` to :g:`D <0339.g2:D>`.

.. subclause::

   But :g:`E <0339.g2:E>` is greater than :g:`H <0339.g2:H>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`M <0339.g2:M>` is also greater than :g:`D <0339.g2:D>`.

.. subclause::

   And it measures :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>` :

.. assertion::

   which is impossible, for by hypothesis :g:`D <0339.g2:D>` is the greatest common measure of :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore there cannot be any numbers less than :g:`E <0339.g2:E>`, :g:`F <0339.g2:F>`, :g:`G <0339.g2:G>` which are in the same ratio with :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`E <0339.g2:E>`, :g:`F <0339.g2:F>`, :g:`G <0339.g2:G>` are the least of those which have the same ratio with :g:`A <0339.g2:A>`, :g:`B <0339.g2:B>`, :g:`C <0339.g2:C>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
