G.456 second apotome — Heath
============================

.. rubric:: `X.def.iii.2 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_x/definitions_iii/#x-def-iii-2>`_

.. enunciation::

   But if the annex be :term:`commensurable <G.372>` in length with the :term:`rational <G.392>` :term:`straight line <G.4>` set out, and the :term:`square <G.63>` on the whole be greater than that on the annex by the square on a straight line commensurable with the whole, let the apotome be called a :term:`**second apotome** <second-apotome>`.
