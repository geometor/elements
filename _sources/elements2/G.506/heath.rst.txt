G.506 segment set right — Heath
===============================

.. rubric:: `XI.4 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xi/propositions/04/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If a :term:`straight line <G.4>` be set up :term:`at right angles <G.33>` to two straight lines which cut one another, at their common :term:`point <G.1>` of section, it will also be :term:`at right angles to the plane <G.505>` through them.

For let a straight line :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` be set up at right angles to the two straight lines :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />`, which cut one another at the point :g:`E <g1:E>`, from :g:`E <g1:E>`; I say that :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` is also at right angles to the plane through :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />`.

For let :g:`/ A E / <g1:/ A E />`, :g:`/ E B / <g1:/ E B />`, :g:`/ C E / <g1:/ C E />`, :g:`/ E D / <g1:/ E D />` be cut off :eq:`equal <g1:/ A E / = g1:/ E B / = g1:/ C E / = g1:/ E D />` to one another, and let any straight line :g:`/ G E H / <g1:/ G E H />` be drawn across through :g:`E <g1:E>`, at random; let :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />`, :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />` be joined, and further let :g:`/ F A / <g1:/ F A />`, :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />`, :g:`/ F D / <g1:/ F D />`, :g:`/ F C / <g1:/ F C />`, :g:`/ F H / <g1:/ F H />`, :g:`/ F B / <g1:/ F B />` be joined from the point :g:`F <g1:F>` taken at random &lt;on :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` &gt;.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 4

.. subclause::

   Now, since the two straight lines :g:`/ A E / <g1:/ A E />`, :g:`/ E D / <g1:/ E D />` are :eq:`equal <g1:/ A E / = g1:/ C E /; g1:/ E D / = g1:/ E B />` to the two straight lines :g:`/ C E / <g1:/ C E />`, :g:`/ E B / <g1:/ E B />`, and contain :eq:`equal <g1:< A E D ) = g1:< C E B )>` :term:`angles <G.22>`, :just:`[G.40] <G.40>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the base :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ A D / = g1:/ C B />` to the base :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />`, and the :term:`triangle <G.12>` :g:`< A E D > <g1:< A E D >>` will be :eq:`equal <g1:< A E D > = g1:< C E B >>` to the triangle :g:`< C E B > <g1:< C E B >>`; :just:`[G.24] <G.24>` so that the angle :g:`< D A E ) <g1:< D A E )>` is also :eq:`equal <g1:< D A E ) = g1:< E B C )>` to the angle :g:`< E B C ) <g1:< E B C )>`.

.. subclause:: [G.40] <G.40>

   But the angle :g:`< A E G ) <g1:< A E G )>` is also :eq:`equal <g1:< A E G ) = g1:< B E H )>` to the angle :g:`< B E H ) <g1:< B E H )>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`< A G E > <g1:< A G E >>`, :g:`< B E H > <g1:< B E H >>` are two triangles which have two angles :eq:`equal <g1:< D A E ) = g1:< E B C ); g1:< A E G ) = g1:< B E H )>` to two angles respectively, and one side :eq:`equal <g1:/ A E / = g1:/ E B />` to one side, namely that adjacent to the :eq:`equal <g1:< D A E ) = g1:< E B C ); g1:< A E G ) = g1:< B E H )>` angles, that is to say, :g:`/ A E / <g1:/ A E />` to :g:`/ E B / <g1:/ E B />`; therefore they will also have the remaining sides :eq:`equal <g1:/ G E / = g1:/ E H /; g1:/ A G / = g1:/ B H />` to the remaining sides. :just:`[G.51] <G.51>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`/ G E / <g1:/ G E />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ G E / = g1:/ E H /; g1:/ A G / = g1:/ B H />` to :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`, and :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` to :g:`/ B H / <g1:/ B H />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`/ A E / <g1:/ A E />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ A E / = g1:/ E B />` to :g:`/ E B / <g1:/ E B />`, while :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />` is common and at right angles,

.. assertion:: [G.24] <G.24>

   therefore the base :g:`/ F A / <g1:/ F A />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ F A / = g1:/ F B />` to the base :g:`/ F B / <g1:/ F B />`.

.. assertion::

   For the same reason :g:`/ F C / <g1:/ F C />` is also :eq:`equal <g1:/ F C / = g1:/ F D />` to :g:`/ F D / <g1:/ F D />`.

.. subclause::

   And, since :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ A D / = g1:/ C B />` to :g:`/ C B / <g1:/ C B />`, and :g:`/ F A / <g1:/ F A />` is also :eq:`equal <g1:/ F A / = g1:/ F B />` to :g:`/ F B / <g1:/ F B />`, the two sides :g:`/ F A / <g1:/ F A />`, :g:`/ A D / <g1:/ A D />` are :eq:`equal <g1:/ F A / = g1:/ F B /; g1:/ A D / = g1:/ B C />` to the two sides :g:`/ F B / <g1:/ F B />`, :g:`/ B C / <g1:/ B C />` respectively; and the base :g:`/ F D / <g1:/ F D />` was proved :eq:`equal <g1:/ F D / = g1:/ F C />` to the base :g:`/ F C / <g1:/ F C />`;

.. assertion:: [G.29] <G.29>

   therefore the angle :g:`< F A D ) <g1:< F A D )>` is also :eq:`equal <g1:< F A D ) = g1:< F B C )>` to the angle :g:`< F B C ) <g1:< F B C )>`.

.. subclause::

   And since, again, :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` was proved :eq:`equal <g1:/ A G / = g1:/ B H />` to :g:`/ B H / <g1:/ B H />`, and further :g:`/ F A / <g1:/ F A />` also :eq:`equal <g1:/ F A / = g1:/ F B />` to :g:`/ F B / <g1:/ F B />`, the two sides :g:`/ F A / <g1:/ F A />`, :g:`/ A G / <g1:/ A G />` are :eq:`equal <g1:/ F A / = g1:/ F B /; g1:/ A G / = g1:/ B H />` to the two sides :g:`/ F B / <g1:/ F B />`, :g:`/ B H / <g1:/ B H />`. And the angle :g:`< F A G ) <g1:< F A G )>` was proved :eq:`equal <g1:< F A G ) = g1:< F B H )>` to the angle :g:`< F B H ) <g1:< F B H )>`;

.. assertion:: [G.24] <G.24>

   therefore the base :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ F G / = g1:/ F H />` to the base :g:`/ F H / <g1:/ F H />`.

.. subclause::

   Now since, again, :g:`/ G E / <g1:/ G E />` was proved :eq:`equal <g1:/ G E / = g1:/ E H />` to :g:`/ E H / <g1:/ E H />`, and :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` is common, the two sides :g:`/ G E / <g1:/ G E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` are :eq:`equal <g1:/ G E / = g1:/ H E />` to the two sides :g:`/ H E / <g1:/ H E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`; and the base :g:`/ F G / <g1:/ F G />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ F G / = g1:/ F H />` to the base :g:`/ F H / <g1:/ F H />`;

.. assertion:: [G.29] <G.29>

   therefore the angle :g:`< G E F ) <g1:< G E F )>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< G E F ) = g1:< H E F )>` to the angle :g:`< H E F ) <g1:< H E F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore each of the angles :g:`< G E F ) <g1:< G E F )>`, :g:`< H E F ) <g1:< H E F )>` is :eq:`right <g1:< G E F ) = g1:< H E F )>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />` is at :eq:`right angles <g1:< G E F ) = g1:< H E F )>` to :g:`/ G H / <g1:/ G H />` drawn at random through :g:`E <g1:E>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />` will also make right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in the :term:`plane <G.7>` of reference.

.. subclause::

   But a straight line is at right angles to a plane when it makes right angles with all the straight lines which meet it and are in that same plane; :just:`[G.505] <G.505>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to the plane of reference.

.. subclause::

   But the plane of reference is the plane through the straight lines :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />` is at right angles to the plane through :g:`/ A B / <g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <g1:/ C D />`.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
