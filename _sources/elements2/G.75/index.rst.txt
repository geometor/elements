G.75
====

.. rubric:: `II.14 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_ii/propositions/14/>`_

.. enunciation::

   To construct a square equal to a given rectilineal figure.

Let :g:`A <0423.g1:A>` be the given rectilineal figure;

thus it is required to construct a square equal to the rectilineal figure :g:`A <0423.g1:A>`.

.. picture:: 0423.g1.svg
   :alt: Diagram

For let there be constructed the rectangular parallelogram :g:`< B D E C > <0423.g1:< B D E C >>` :eq:`equal <0423.g1:< B D E C > = 0423.g1:A>` to the rectilineal figure :g:`A <0423.g1:A>`. :just:`[G.58] <G.58>`

Then, if :g:`/ B E / <0423.g1:/ B E />` is :eq:`equal <0423.g1:/ B E / = 0423.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`/ E D / <0423.g1:/ E D />`, that which was enjoined will have been done; for a square :g:`< B D E C > <0423.g1:< B D E C >>` has been constructed :eq:`equal <0423.g1:< B D E C > = 0423.g1:A>` to the rectilineal figure :g:`A <0423.g1:A>`.

But, if not, one of the straight lines :g:`/ B E / <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ E D / <0423.g1:/ E D />` is greater.

Let :g:`/ B E / <0423.g1:/ B E />` be greater, and let it be produced to :g:`F <0423.g1:F>`; let :g:`/ E F / <0423.g1:/ E F />` be made :eq:`equal <0423.g1:/ E F / = 0423.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`/ E D / <0423.g1:/ E D />`, and let :g:`/ B F / <0423.g1:/ B F />` be :eq:`bisected <0423.g1:/ G B / = 0423.g1:/ G F />` at :g:`G <0423.g1:G>`.

With centre :g:`G <0423.g1:G>` and distance one of the straight lines :g:`/ G B / <0423.g1:/ G B />`, :g:`/ G F / <0423.g1:/ G F />` let the semicircle :g:`( B H F ) <0423.g1:( B H F )>` be described; let :g:`[ D E ] <0423.g1:[ D E ]>` be produced to :g:`H <0423.g1:H>`, and let :g:`/ G H / <0423.g1:/ G H />` be joined.

Then, since the straight line :g:`/ B F / <0423.g1:/ B F />` has been cut into :eq:`equal <0423.g1:/ G B / = 0423.g1:/ G F />` segments at :g:`G <0423.g1:G>`, and into unequal segments at :g:`E <0423.g1:E>`,

.. assertion:: [G.66] <G.66>

   the rectangle contained by :g:`/ B E / <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ E F / <0423.g1:/ E F />` together with the square on :g:`/ E G / <0423.g1:/ E G />` is equal to the square on :g:`/ G F / <0423.g1:/ G F />`.

But :g:`/ G F / <0423.g1:/ G F />` is :eq:`equal <0423.g1:/ G F / = 0423.g1:/ G H />` to :g:`/ G H / <0423.g1:/ G H />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`/ B E / <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ E F / <0423.g1:/ E F />` together with the square on :g:`/ G E / <0423.g1:/ G E />` is equal to the square on :g:`/ G H / <0423.g1:/ G H />`.

But the squares on :g:`/ H E / <0423.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`/ E G / <0423.g1:/ E G />` are equal to the square on :g:`/ G H / <0423.g1:/ G H />`; :just:`[G.60] <G.60>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle :g:`/ B E / <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ E F / <0423.g1:/ E F />` together with the square on :g:`/ G E / <0423.g1:/ G E />` is equal to the squares on :g:`/ H E / <0423.g1:/ H E />`, :g:`/ E G / <0423.g1:/ E G />`.

Let the square on :g:`/ G E / <0423.g1:/ G E />` be subtracted from each;

.. assertion::

   therefore the rectangle contained by :g:`/ B E / <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ E F / <0423.g1:/ E F />` which remains is equal to the square on :g:`/ E H / <0423.g1:/ E H />`.

But the rectangle :g:`/ B E / <0423.g1:/ B E />`, :g:`/ E F / <0423.g1:/ E F />` is :g:`< B D E C > <0423.g1:< B D E C >>`, for :g:`/ E F / <0423.g1:/ E F />` is :eq:`equal <0423.g1:/ E F / = 0423.g1:/ E D />` to :g:`/ E D / <0423.g1:/ E D />`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`< B D E C > <0423.g1:< B D E C >>` is :eq:`equal <0423.g1:< B D E C > = 0423.g1:/ H E />` to the square on :g:`/ H E / <0423.g1:/ H E />`.

And :g:`< B D E C > <0423.g1:< B D E C >>` is :eq:`equal <0423.g1:< B D E C > = 0423.g1:A>` to the rectilineal figure :g:`A <0423.g1:A>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the rectilineal figure :g:`A <0423.g1:A>` is also :eq:`equal <0423.g1:A = 0423.g1:/ E H />` to the square which can be described on :g:`/ E H / <0423.g1:/ E H />`.

Therefore a square, namely that which can be described on :g:`/ E H / <0423.g1:/ E H />`, has been constructed :eq:`equal <0423.g1:/ E H / = 0423.g1:A>` to the given rectilineal figure :g:`A <0423.g1:A>`.

.. qed::

   Q. E. F.
