G.469 third apotome
===================

.. rubric:: `X.def.iii.3 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_x/definitions_iii/#x-def-iii-3>`_

.. enunciation::

   But if neither be:term:`commensurable <G.372>` in length with the:term:`rational <G.392>`:term:`segment <G.4>` set out, and the:term:`square <G.63>` on the whole be greater than the square on the annex by the square on a segment commensurable with the whole, let the apotome be called a:term:`**third apotome** <third-apotome>`.
