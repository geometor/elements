G.177 — Heath
=============

.. rubric:: `VI.14 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_vi/propositions/14/>`_

.. enunciation::

   In equal and equiangular parallelograms the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional; and equiangular parallelograms in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional are equal.

Let :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>`, :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>` be :eq:`equal <g1:< A B > = g1:< B C >>` and equiangular parallelograms having the angles at :g:`< D B F ) <g1:< D B F )>` :eq:`equal <g1:< D B F ) = g1:< G B E )>`, and let :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />`, :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />` be placed in a straight line;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ F B / <g1:/ F B />`, :g:`/ B G / <g1:/ B G />` are also in a straight line. :just:`[G.24] <G.24>`

I say that, in :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>`, :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>`, the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional, that is to say, that, as :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`, so is :g:`/ G B / <g1:/ G B />` to :g:`/ B F / <g1:/ B F />`.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Book VI Proposition 14

For let the parallelogram :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>` be completed.

.. subclause::

   Since, then, the parallelogram :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< A B > = g1:< B C >>` to the parallelogram :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>`,

.. subclause::

   and :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>` is another area,

.. assertion:: [G.142] <G.142>

   therefore, as :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` is to :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>`, so is :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>` to :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>`.

.. subclause::

   But, as :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` is to :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>`, so is :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />` to :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`, :just:`[G.163] <G.163>` and, as :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>` is to :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>`, so is :g:`/ G B / <g1:/ G B />` to :g:`/ B F / <g1:/ B F />`. :just:`[G.163] <G.163>`

.. assertion:: [G.148] <G.148>

   therefore also, as :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`, so is :g:`/ G B / <g1:/ G B />` to :g:`/ B F / <g1:/ B F />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore in the parallelograms :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>`, :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>` the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional.

Next, let :g:`/ G B / <g1:/ G B />` be to :g:`/ B F / <g1:/ B F />` as :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />` to :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`;

I say that the parallelogram :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< A B > = g1:< B C >>` to the parallelogram :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>`.

.. subclause::

   For since, as :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`, so is :g:`/ G B / <g1:/ G B />` to :g:`/ B F / <g1:/ B F />`, while, as :g:`/ D B / <g1:/ D B />` is to :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />`, so is the parallelogram :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` to the parallelogram :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>`, :just:`[G.163] <G.163>` and, as :g:`/ G B / <g1:/ G B />` is to :g:`/ B F / <g1:/ B F />`, so is the parallelogram :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>` to the parallelogram :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>`, :just:`[G.163] <G.163>`

.. assertion:: [G.148] <G.148>

   therefore also, as :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` is to :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>`, so is :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>` to :g:`< F E > <g1:< F E >>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the parallelogram :g:`< A B > <g1:< A B >>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< A B > = g1:< B C >>` to the parallelogram :g:`< B C > <g1:< B C >>`. :just:`[G.146] <G.146>`

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
