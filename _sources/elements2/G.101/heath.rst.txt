G.101 circle segments cut — Heath
=================================

.. rubric:: `III.4 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_iii/propositions/04/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If in a :term:`circle <G.10>` two :term:`straight lines <G.4>` cut one another which are not through the :term:`centre <G.11>`, they do not bisect one another.

Let :g:`( A B C D ) <g1:( A B C D )>` be a circle, and in it let the two straight lines :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`, :g:`/ B D / <g1:/ B D />`, which are not through the centre, cut one another at :g:`E <g1:E>`;

I say that they do not bisect one another.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 4

For, if possible, let them :eq:`bisect <g1:/ A E / = g1:/ E C /; g1:/ B E / = g1:/ E D />` one another, so that :g:`/ A E / <g1:/ A E />` is :eq:`equal <g1:/ A E / = g1:/ E C /; g1:/ B E / = g1:/ E D />` to :g:`/ E C / <g1:/ E C />`, and :g:`/ B E / <g1:/ B E />` to :g:`/ E D / <g1:/ E D />`; let the centre of the circle :g:`( A B C D ) <g1:( A B C D )>` be taken :just:`[G.98] <G.98>`, and let it be :g:`F <g1:F>`; let :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />` be joined.

Then, since a straight line :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />` through the centre :eq:`bisects <g1:/ A E / = g1:/ E C />` a straight line :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />` not through the centre,

.. subclause:: [G.100] <G.100>

   it also cuts it :term:`at right angles <G.33>`;

.. assertion::

   therefore the :term:`angle <G.22>` :g:`< F E A ) <g1:< F E A )>` is right.

Again, since a straight line :g:`/ F E / <g1:/ F E />` :eq:`bisects <g1:/ B E / = g1:/ E D />` a straight line :g:`/ B D / <g1:/ B D />`,

.. subclause:: [G.100] <G.100>

   it also cuts it at right angles;

.. assertion::

   therefore the angle :g:`< F E B ) <g1:< F E B )>` is right.

But the angle :g:`< F E A ) <g1:< F E A )>` was also proved right; therefore the angle :g:`< F E A ) <g1:< F E A )>` is :eq:`equal <g1:< F E A ) = g1:< F E B )>` to the angle :g:`< F E B ) <g1:< F E B )>`, the less to the greater: which is impossible.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`/ A C / <g1:/ A C />`, :g:`/ B D / <g1:/ B D />` do not bisect one another.

.. qed::

   Therefore etc. :just:`Q. E. D.`
