G.96 semicircle — Heath
=======================

.. rubric:: `I.def.18 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_I/book_i/definitions/#i-def-18>`_

.. enunciation::

   A :term:`**semicircle** <semicircle>` is the :term:`figure <G.9>` contained by the :term:`diameter <G.61>` and the circumference cut off by it. And the :term:`centre <G.14>` of the semicircle is the same as that of the :term:`circle <G.10>`.
