G.386 segments proportional square — Code
=========================================

.. rubric:: `X.14 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_x/propositions/014/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If four :term:`segments <G.4>` be :term:`proportional <G.180>`, and the :term:`square <G.63>` on the first be greater than the square on the second by the square on a segment :term:`commensurable <G.372>` with the first, the square on the third is greater than the square on the fourth by the square on a segment commensurable with the third. And, if the square on the first be greater than the square on the second by the square on a segment incommensurable with the first, the square on the third is greater than the square on the fourth by the square on a segment incommensurable with the third.
