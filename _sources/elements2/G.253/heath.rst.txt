G.253 part of a number — Heath
==============================

.. rubric:: `VII.def.3 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_vii/definitions/#vii-def-3>`_

.. enunciation::

   A :term:`number <G.248>` is a :term:`**part** <part-aliquot>` of a number, the less of the greater, when it :term:`measures <part-aliquot>` the greater;
