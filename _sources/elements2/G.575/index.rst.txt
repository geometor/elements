G.575 sphere
============

.. rubric:: `XI.def.14 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xi/definitions/#xi-def-14>`_

.. enunciation::

   When, the:term:`diameter <G.61>` of a:term:`semicircle <G.96>` remaining fixed, the semicircle is carried round and restored again to the same position from which it began to be moved, the:term:`figure <G.9>` so comprehended is a:term:`**sphere** <sphere>`.
