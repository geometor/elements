G.286 — Heath
=============

.. rubric:: `IX.15 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_II/book_ix/propositions/15/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If three numbers in continued proportion be the least of those which have the same ratio with them, any two whatever added together will be prime to the remaining number.

Let :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`C <g1:C>`, three numbers in continued proportion, be the least of those which have the same ratio with them ;

I say that any two of the numbers :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`C <g1:C>` whatever added together are prime to the remaining number, namely :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>` to :g:`C <g1:C>` ; :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`C <g1:C>` to :g:`A <g1:A>` ; and further :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`C <g1:C>` to :g:`B <g1:B>`.

.. picture:: g1.svg
   :alt: Proposition 15 diagram

For let two numbers :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`, the least of those which have the same ratio with :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`C <g1:C>`, be taken. :just:`[G.242] <G.242>`

.. assertion::

   It is then manifest that :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` by multiplying itself has made :g:`A <g1:A>`, and by multiplying :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` has made :g:`B <g1:B>`, and, further, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` by multiplying itself has made :g:`C <g1:C>`. :just:`[G.242] <G.242>`

.. subclause::

   Now, since :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` are least,

.. assertion:: [G.223] <G.223>

   they are prime to one another.

.. subclause::

   But, if two numbers be prime to one another, their sum is also prime to each ; :just:`[G.229] <G.229>`

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ D F / <g1:/ D F />` is also prime to each of the numbers :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   But further :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` is also prime to :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`/ D F / <g1:/ D F />`, :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` are prime to :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   But, if two numbers be prime to any number, their product is also prime to the other ; :just:`[G.225] <G.225>`

.. assertion::

   so that the product of :g:`/ D F / <g1:/ D F />`, :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` is prime to :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` ; hence the product of :g:`/ D F / <g1:/ D F />`, :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` is also prime to the square on :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`. :just:`[G.226] <G.226>`

.. subclause::

   But the product of :g:`/ D F / <g1:/ D F />`, :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` is the square on :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` together with the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` ; :just:`[G.64] <G.64>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the square on :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` together with the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` is prime to the square on :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   And the square on :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` is :g:`A <g1:A>`, the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` is :g:`B <g1:B>`, and the square on :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` is :g:`C <g1:C>` ;

.. assertion::

   therefore :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`B <g1:B>` added together are prime to :g:`C <g1:C>`.

.. assertion::

   Similarly we can prove that :g:`B <g1:B>`, :g:`C <g1:C>` added together are prime to :g:`A <g1:A>`.

I say next that :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`C <g1:C>` added together are also prime to :g:`B <g1:B>`.

.. subclause::

   For, since :g:`/ D F / <g1:/ D F />` is prime to each of the numbers :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`,

.. assertion:: [G.225, G.226] <G.225, G.226>

   the square on :g:`/ D F / <g1:/ D F />` is also prime to the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   But the squares on :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` together with twice the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` are equal to the square on :g:`/ D F / <g1:/ D F />` ; :just:`[G.65] <G.65>`

.. assertion::

   therefore the squares on :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` together with twice the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` are prime to the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`.

.. assertion::

   *Separando*, the squares on :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` together with once the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` are prime to the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore, :term:`*separando* <separando>` again, the squares on :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` are prime to the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />`.

.. subclause::

   And the square on :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />` is :g:`A <g1:A>`, the product of :g:`/ D E / <g1:/ D E />`, :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` is :g:`B <g1:B>`, and the square on :g:`/ E F / <g1:/ E F />` is :g:`C <g1:C>`.

.. assertion::

   Therefore :g:`A <g1:A>`, :g:`C <g1:C>` added together are prime to :g:`B <g1:B>`.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
