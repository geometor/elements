G.444
=====

.. rubric:: `XI.2 <https://geometor.github.io/euclid/heath/volume_III/book_xi/propositions/02/>`_

.. enunciation::

   If two straight lines cut one another, they are in one plane, and every triangle is in one plane.

For let the two straight lines :g:`/ A B / <0278.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <0278.g1:/ C D />` cut one another at the point :g:`E <0278.g1:E>`; I say that :g:`/ A B / <0278.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <0278.g1:/ C D />` are in one plane, and every triangle is in one plane.

.. picture:: 0278.g1.svg
   :alt: Book XI, Proposition 2

For let points :g:`F <0278.g1:F>`, :g:`G <0278.g1:G>` be taken at random on :g:`/ E C / <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`/ E B / <0278.g1:/ E B />`, let :g:`/ C B / <0278.g1:/ C B />`, :g:`/ F G / <0278.g1:/ F G />` be joined, and let :g:`/ F H / <0278.g1:/ F H />`, :g:`/ G K / <0278.g1:/ G K />` be drawn across; I say first that the triangle :g:`< E C B > <0278.g1:< E C B >>` is in one plane.

.. subclause::

   For, if part of the triangle :g:`< E C B > <0278.g1:< E C B >>`, either :g:`< F H C > <0278.g1:< F H C >>` or :g:`< G B K > <0278.g1:< G B K >>`, is in the plane of reference, and the rest in another,

.. assertion::

   a part also of one of the straight lines :g:`/ E C / <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`/ E B / <0278.g1:/ E B />` will be in the plane of reference, and a part in another.

.. subclause::

   But, if the part :g:`< F C B G > <0278.g1:< F C B G >>` of the triangle :g:`< E C B > <0278.g1:< E C B >>` be in the plane of reference, and the rest in another,

.. assertion::

   a part also of both the straight lines :g:`/ E C / <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`/ E B / <0278.g1:/ E B />` will be in the plane of reference and a part in another:

.. assertion:: [G.443] <G.443>

   which was proved absurd.

.. assertion::

   Therefore the triangle :g:`< E C B > <0278.g1:< E C B >>` is in one plane.

.. subclause::

   But, in whatever plane the triangle :g:`< E C B > <0278.g1:< E C B >>` is, in that plane also is each of the straight lines :g:`/ E C / <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`/ E B / <0278.g1:/ E B />`, and, in whatever plane each of the straight lines :g:`/ E C / <0278.g1:/ E C />`, :g:`/ E B / <0278.g1:/ E B />` is, in that plane are :g:`/ A B / <0278.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <0278.g1:/ C D />` also. :just:`[G.443] <G.443>`

.. assertion::

   Therefore the straight lines :g:`/ A B / <0278.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`/ C D / <0278.g1:/ C D />` are in one plane, and every triangle is in one plane.

.. qed::

   :just:`Q. E. D.`
