==============================================================================
Defined-Term Dependency Wiring, Short Verb Titles & Two-Point Circle Notation
==============================================================================

Released ``geometor.elements`` ``v0.4.0``, integrating defined-term dependency wiring across all 13 books (``G.1`` through ``G.601``), concise lowercase verb-phrase titles, foundational SVG constructions, the ``≃`` coincidence operator, and the two-point circle convention ``( A B )``.

Highlights
----------

- **Defined-Term Dependency Graph (G.1–G.601)**: Wired ``:term:`` occurrences in definitions, postulates, common notions, and propositions into the G-Index dependency graph, promoting 76 previously orphaned definitions into the active sequence (leaving only 5 genuinely unreferenced definitions in ``orphans.rst``: ``rhombus``, ``rhomboid``, ``trapezia``, ``oblong``, and ``apotheme``).
- **Short Verb Titles**: Replaced verbose titles with lowercase action and concept titles across all 601 G-Index entries (e.g., ``G.17 construct an equilateral triangle``, ``G.20 transfer a segment to a given point``).
- **Segment / Line / Arc Terminology**: Normalized modern ``elements`` enunciations to distinguish infinite lines (``/ A B /``), segments (``[ A B ]``), and arcs while preserving Heath's historical translation in the Heath track.
- **Foundational Rule Constructions & Coincidence (≃)**: Added ``geometor.model`` construction scripts and calibrated SVGs for foundational Book I definitions, postulates, and Common Notion 4 (``G.23``), plus the ``:coincide:`` (``≃``) superposition role for ``I.4`` and ``I.8``.
- **Two-Point Circle Notation ``( A B )``**: Standardized circle constructions and references to ``( A B )`` where ``A`` is the center and ``B`` is the defining radius point, while preserving ``<g1:( B C D )>`` SVG element targets for interactive highlighting.
