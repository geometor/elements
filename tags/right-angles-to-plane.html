G.505 line at right angles to a plane

A:term:segment <G.4> is:term:**at right angles to a plane** <right-angles-to-plane>, when it makes:term:right angles <G.32> with all the segments which meet it and are in the:term:plane <G.7>.
definition solid-geometry book-xi straight-line right-angles-to-plane right-angle plane-surface plane

G.506 segment set right

If a segment be set up at right angles to two segments which cut one another, at their common point of section, it is at right angles to the plane through them.

G.507 segment set right

If a segment be set up at right angles to three segments which meet one another, at their common point of section, the three segments are in one plane.
