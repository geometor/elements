G.1 point

A point A is that which has no part.
definition plane-geometry book-i point

G.2 line

A line [ A B ] is a projection of a relationship of two points A, B.
definition plane-geometry book-i line

G.3 extremities of a segment

The extremities of a segment / A B / are points A, B.
definition plane-geometry book-i extremity line point

G.4 segment

A segment / A B / is a portion of a line [ A B ] between two points A, B on the line.
definition plane-geometry book-i straight-line line point

G.5 surface

A surface is that which has length and breadth only.
definition plane-geometry book-i surface

G.6 extremities of a surface

The extremities of a surface are lines or arcs.

G.7 plane surface

A plane surface lies evenly with the lines on itself.

G.8 boundary

A boundary is the extremity of anything.
definition plane-geometry book-i boundary extremity

G.9 figure

A figure is that which is contained by any boundary or boundaries.
definition plane-geometry book-i figure boundary

G.10 circle

A circle ( A B ) is a plane figure contained by one arc defined by a center A and a point B establishing its defining radius / A B /, such that all segments / A B /, / A C /, / A D / falling upon the arc from A are equal.
definition plane-geometry book-i circle plane-surface extremity straight-line point figure plane

G.11 center of a circle

The point A within the circle ( A B ) from which all segments / A B /, / A C / to the arc are equal is called the center of the circle.
definition plane-geometry book-i point centre circle

G.12 rectilineal figures

Rectilineal figures are contained by segments: trilateral by three, quadrilateral by four, and multilateral by more than four.

G.13 equilateral, isosceles, and scalene triangles
