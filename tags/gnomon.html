G.86 gnomon

And in any parallelogrammic area let any one whatever of the parallelograms about its:term:diameter <G.61> with the two complements be called a:term:**gnomon** <gnomon>.

G.87 difference of squares equal unequal

If a segment be cut into equal and unequal segments, the rectangle contained by the unequal segments of the whole together with the square on the segment between the points of section is equal to the square on the half.

G.88 bisected and extended segment

If a segment be bisected and a segment be added to it in a segment, the rectangle contained by the whole with the added segment and the added segment together with the square on the half is equal to the square on the segment made up of the half and the added segment.

G.89 square of difference

If a segment be cut at random, the square on the whole and that on one of the segments both together are equal to twice the rectangle contained by the whole and the said segment and the square on the remaining segment.

G.90 four rectangles plus square

If a segment be cut at random, four times the rectangle contained by the whole and one of the segments together with the square on the remaining segment is equal to the square described on the whole and the aforesaid segment as on one segment.

G.239 parallelograms applied segment

Of all the parallelograms applied to the same segment and deficient by parallelogrammic figures similar and similarly situated to that described on the half of the segment, that parallelogram is greatest which is applied to the half of the segment and is similar to the defect.

G.240 segment apply parallelogram

To a given segment to apply a parallelogram equal to a given polygon and deficient by a parallelogrammic figure similar to a given one: thus the given polygon must not be greater than the parallelogram described on the half of the segment and similar to the defect.

G.579 segment cut extreme

If a segment be cut in extreme and mean ratio, the square on the greater segment added to the half of the whole is five times the square on the half.

G.580 square segment five

If the square on a segment be five times the square on a segment of it, then, when the double of the said segment is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is the remaining part of the original segment.
