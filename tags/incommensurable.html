Posts tagged 'incommensurable'

G.314

Those magnitudes are said to be:term:**commensurable** <commensurable> which are measured by the same measure, and those:term:**incommensurable** <incommensurable> which cannot have any common measure.
definition incommensurables magnitudes book-x commensurable incommensurable

G.315

If, when the less of two unequal magnitudes is continually subtracted in turn from the greater, that which is left never measures the one before it, the magnitudes is incommensurable.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x point segment incommensurable reductio-ad-absurdum

G.320

Incommensurable magnitudes have not to one another the ratio which a number has to a number.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x point ratio incommensurable number

G.321

If two magnitudes have not to one another the ratio which a number has to a number, the magnitudes is incommensurable.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x point ratio incommensurable number

G.322

The squares on straight lines commensurable in length have to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number; and squares which have to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number will also have their sides commensurable in length. But the squares on straight lines incommensurable in length have not to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number; and squares which have not to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number will not have their sides commensurable in length either.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x point square ratio commensurable incommensurable number square-number reductio-ad-absurdum porism

G.323

If four magnitudes be proportional, and the first be commensurable with the second, the third is commensurable with the fourth; and, if the first be incommensurable with the second, the third is incommensurable with the fourth.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x point proportion commensurable incommensurable

G.324

Find two straight lines incommensurable, the one in length only, and the other in square also, with an assigned straight line.
problem finding incommensurables magnitudes book-x point square incommensurable

G.326

If two magnitudes be commensurable, and the one of them be incommensurable with any magnitude, the remaining one is incommensurable with the same.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x point segment circle angle commensurable incommensurable equality reductio-ad-absurdum lemma

G.329

If two incommensurable magnitudes be added together, the whole is incommensurable with each of them; and, if the whole be incommensurable with one of them, the original magnitudes is incommensurable.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x point segment quadrilateral incommensurable equality reductio-ad-absurdum lemma

G.331

If there be two unequal straight lines, and to the greater there be applied a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on the less and deficient by a square figure, and if it divide it into parts which are incommensurable, the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line incommensurable with the greater. And, if the square on the greater be greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line incommensurable with the greater, and if there be applied to the greater a parallelogram equal to the fourth part of the square on the less and deficient by a square figure, it divides it into parts which are incommensurable.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x point segment separando parallelogram square incommensurable

G.332

And let the square on the assigned straight line be called:term:**rational** <rational> and those areas which are commensurable with it:term:**rational** <rational>, but those which are incommensurable with it:term:**irrational** <irrational>, and the straight lines which produce them:term:**irrational** <irrational>, that is, in case the areas are squares, the sides themselves, but in case they are any other polygons, the straight lines on which are described squares equal to them.
definition incommensurables magnitudes book-x rational irrational square commensurable incommensurable

G.336

The square on a medial straight line, if applied to a rational straight line, produces as breadth a straight line rational and incommensurable in length with that to which it is applied.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x point segment polygon square incommensurable rational medial equality

G.337

With these hypotheses, it is proved that there exist straight lines infinite in multitude which are commensurable and incommensurable respectively, some in length only, and others in square also, with an assigned straight line. Let then the assigned straight line be called:term:**rational** <rational>, and those straight lines which are commensurable with it, whether in length and in square or in square only,:term:**rational** <rational>, but those which are incommensurable with it:term:**irrational** <irrational>.
definition incommensurables magnitudes book-x rational irrational square commensurable incommensurable

G.345

Find two rational straight lines commensurable in square only and such that the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line incommensurable in length with the greater.
problem finding incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment circle convertendo square commensurable incommensurable rational

G.348

Find two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational but the rectangle contained by them medial.
problem finding incommensurables magnitudes book-x point segment circle square rectangle incommensurable rational medial equality

G.349

Find two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial but the rectangle contained by them rational.
problem finding incommensurables magnitudes book-x point segment circle square rectangle incommensurable rational medial equality

G.350

Find two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial and the rectangle contained by them medial and moreover incommensurable with the sum of the squares on them.
problem finding incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment circle square rectangle incommensurable medial equality

G.354

If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational: and let it be called:term:**major** <major>.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment major square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.355

If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational; and let it be called the:term:**side of a rational plus a medial area** <side-of-a-rational-plus-a-medial-area>.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment side-of-a-rational-plus-a-medial-area square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.356

If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, and the rectangle contained by them medial and also incommensurable with the sum of the squares on them, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational; and let it be called the:term:**side of the sum of two medial areas** <side-of-the-sum-of-two-medial-areas>.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment polygon side-of-the-sum-of-two-medial-areas square rectangle incommensurable irrational medial

G.375

Again, if the square on the greater term be greater than the square on the lesser by the square on a straight line incommensurable in length with the greater, then, if the greater term be commensurable in length with the rational straight line set out, let the whole be called a:term:**fourth binomial** <fourth-binomial>;
definition incommensurables magnitudes book-x fourth-binomial square commensurable incommensurable rational binomial

G.393

If two medial areas incommensurable with one another be added together, the remaining two irrational straight lines arise, namely either a second bimedial or a side of the sum of two medial areas.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment polygon incommensurable irrational medial equality

G.397

If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which with the whole makes the squares on them added together rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called minor.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment convertendo minor square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.398

If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line which is incommensurable in square with the whole, and which with the whole makes the sum of the squares on them medial, but twice the rectangle contained by them rational, the remainder is irrational: and let it be called:term:**that which produces with a rational area a medial whole** <side-of-a-medial-minus-a-rational-area>.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment side-of-a-medial-minus-a-rational-area square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.399

If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which with the whole makes the sum of the squares on them medial, twice the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the squares on them incommensurable with twice the rectangle contained by them, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called:term:**that which produces with a medial area a medial whole** <side-of-a-medial-minus-a-medial-area>.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment polygon side-of-a-medial-minus-a-medial-area square rectangle incommensurable irrational medial

G.403

To a minor straight line only one straight line can be annexed which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which makes, with the whole, the sum of the squares on them rational but twice the rectangle contained by them medial.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment square rectangle incommensurable rational medial reductio-ad-absurdum

G.404

To a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole only one straight line can be annexed which is incommensurable in square with the whole straight line and which with the whole straight line makes the sum of the squares on them medial, but twice the rectangle contained by them rational.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment square rectangle incommensurable rational medial reductio-ad-absurdum

G.405

To a straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole only one straight line can be annexed which is incommensurable in square with the whole straight line and which with the whole straight line makes the sum of the squares on them medial and twice the rectangle contained by them both medial and also incommensurable with the sum of the squares on them.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment polygon square incommensurable medial reductio-ad-absurdum

G.412

Again, if the square on the whole be greater than the square on the annex by the square on a straight line incommensurable with the whole, then, if the whole be commensurable in length with the rational straight line set out, let the apotome be called a:term:**fourth apotome** <fourth-apotome>;
definition incommensurables magnitudes book-x fourth-apotome square commensurable incommensurable rational apotome

G.437

If from a medial area there be subtracted a medial area incommensurable with the whole, the two remaining irrational straight lines arise, either a second apotome of a medial straight line or a straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole.
theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes book-x segment polygon incommensurable irrational medial apotome
