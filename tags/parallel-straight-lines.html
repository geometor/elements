G.52 parallel lines

Parallel lines are lines in the same plane which, extended indefinitely in both directions, do not meet.
definition plane-geometry book-i parallel-straight-lines straight-line plane-surface parallel plane

G.53 equal alternate interior angles imply parallel lines

If a transversal cutting two lines makes the alternate interior angles equal, the lines are parallel.
