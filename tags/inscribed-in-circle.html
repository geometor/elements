G.148 polygon inscribed in a circle

A:term:polygon <G.12> is said to be:term:**inscribed in a circle** <inscribed-in-circle> when each:term:angle <G.22> of the inscribed:term:figure <G.9> lies on the arc of the:term:circle <G.10>.

G.587 side hexagon decagon
