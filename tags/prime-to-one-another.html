G.249 numbers prime to one another

Numbers prime to one another are those which are:term:measured <G.253> by an:term:unit <G.247> alone as a common measure.

Two unequal numbers being set out, and the less being continually subtracted in turn from the greater, if the number which is left never measures the one before it until an unit is left, the original numbers is prime to one another.

G.280 numbers prime numbers

G.281 numbers prime multiplying

If two numbers be prime to one another, and each by multiplying itself make a certain number, the products is prime to one another; and, if the original numbers by multiplying the products make certain numbers, the latter is prime to one another [and this is always the case with the extremes].
theorem proof number-theory book-vii point number prime-to-one-another multiply prime

G.282 numbers prime sum

If two numbers be prime to one another, the sum is prime to each of them; and, if the sum of two numbers be prime to any one of them, the original numbers is prime to one another.
