G.174 ex aequali (equality of ratios)

A:term:ratio <G.173>ex aequali arises when, there being several magnitudes and another set equal to them in multitude which taken two and two are in the same proportion, as the first is to the last among the first magnitudes, so is the first to the last among the second magnitudes; Or, in other words, it means taking the extreme terms by virtue of the removal of the intermediate terms.

definition proportions magnitudes book-v ratio ex-aequali proportion

G.175 magnitude multiple equimultiples

If a first magnitude be the same multiple of a second that a third is of a fourth, and if equimultiples be taken of the first and third, then also :term:`ex aequali <G.174>` the magnitudes taken is equimultiples respectively, the one of the second and the other of the fourth.

G.202 there magnitudes others

If there be three magnitudes, and others equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two are in the same ratio, and if :term:`ex aequali <G.174>` the first be greater than the third, the fourth is greater than the sixth; if equal, equal; and, if less, less.
