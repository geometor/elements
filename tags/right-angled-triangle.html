G.561 cone

When, one side of those about the:term:right angle <G.32> in a:term:right-angled triangle <G.78> remaining fixed, the:term:triangle <G.11> is carried round and restored again to the same position from which it began to be moved, the:term:figure <G.9> so comprehended is a:term:**cone** <cone>.
