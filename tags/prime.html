G.312

If as many numbers as we please beginning from an unit be set out continuously in double proportion, until the sum of all becomes prime, and if the sum multiplied into the last make some number, the product is perfect.

G.288

If there be as many numbers as we please in continued proportion, and the extremes of them be prime to one another, the last will not be to any other number as the first to the second.

G.287

If two numbers be prime to one another, the second will not be to any other number as the first is to the second.

G.286

If three numbers in continued proportion be the least of those which have the same ratio with them, any two whatever added together is prime to the remaining number.

G.285

If a number be the least that is measured by prime numbers, it will not be measured by any other prime number except those originally measuring it.

G.284

If as many numbers as we please beginning from an unit be in continued proportion, and the number after the unit be prime, the greatest will not be measured by any except those which have a place among the proportional numbers.
Category: theorem proof number-theory Tags: book-ix point proportion unit number prime continued-proportion reductio-ad-absurdum

G.283

If as many numbers as we please beginning from an unit be in continued proportion, by however many prime numbers the last is measured, the next to the unit is measured by the same.
Category: theorem proof number-theory Tags: book-ix point proportion unit number prime continued-proportion reductio-ad-absurdum

G.249

If two numbers be prime to one another, and numbers fall between them in continued proportion, then, however many numbers fall between them in continued proportion, so many will also fall between each of them and an unit in continued proportion.
Category: theorem proof number-theory proportions Tags: book-viii point proportion unit number prime continued-proportion equality

G.243

If as many numbers as we please in continued proportion be the least of those which have the same ratio with them, the extremes of them are prime to one another.

G.241

G.233

G.231

If two numbers by multiplying one another make some number, and any prime number measure the product, it will also measure one of the original numbers.

G.230

Any prime number is prime to any number which it does not measure.

G.229

If two numbers be prime to one another, the sum is prime to each of them; and, if the sum of two numbers be prime to any one of them, the original numbers is prime to one another.
Category: theorem proof number-theory Tags: book-vii point segment number prime reductio-ad-absurdum

G.228

If two numbers be prime to one another, and each by multiplying itself make a certain number, the products is prime to one another; and, if the original numbers by multiplying the products make certain numbers, the latter is prime to one another [and this is always the case with the extremes].
Category: theorem proof number-theory Tags: book-vii point number prime

G.227

If two numbers be prime to two numbers, both to each, their products also is prime to one another.
Category: theorem proof number-theory Tags: book-vii point number prime

G.226

If two numbers be prime to one another, the product of one of them into itself is prime to the remaining one.
Category: theorem proof number-theory Tags: book-vii point number prime equality

If two numbers be prime to any number, their product also is prime to the same.

The least numbers of those which have the same ratio with them are prime to one another.

G.200

Two unequal numbers being set out, and the less being continually subtracted in turn from the greater, if the number which is left never measures the one before it until an unit is left, the original numbers is prime to one another.
