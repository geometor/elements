G.380 commensurable in square

Straight lines are:term:**commensurable in square** <commensurable-in-square> when the:term:squares <G.63> on them are:term:measured <G.253> by the same area, and:term:**incommensurable in square** <incommensurable-in-square> when the squares on them cannot possibly have any area as a common measure.
