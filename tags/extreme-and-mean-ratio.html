G.242 extreme and mean ratio

A segment is said to have been cut in extreme and mean ratio when, as the whole:term:line <G.2> is to the greater segment, so is the greater to the less.

G.579 segment cut extreme

If a segment be cut in extreme and mean ratio, the square on the greater segment added to the half of the whole is five times the square on the half.

G.580 square segment five

If the square on a segment be five times the square on a segment of it, then, when the double of the said segment is cut in extreme and mean ratio, the greater segment is the remaining part of the original segment.

G.583 segment cut extreme

If a segment be cut in extreme and mean ratio, and there be added to it a segment equal to the greater segment, the whole segment has been cut in extreme and mean ratio, and the original segment is the greater segment.
