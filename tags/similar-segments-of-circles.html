Posts tagged 'similar-segments-of-circles'

G.127 similar circle segments

Similar segments of circles are those which admit equal:term:angles <G.22>, or in which the angles are equal.
definition plane-geometry circles book-iii similar-segments-of-circles plane-angle similar plane

G.128 segment there constructed

On the same segment there cannot be constructed two similar and unequal segments of circles on the same side.
theorem proof plane-geometry circles book-iii segment circle angle straight-line segment-of-circle similar-segments-of-circles plane-angle similar equality reductio-ad-absurdum

G.129 similar segments circles

Similar segments of circles on equal segments are equal.
theorem proof plane-geometry circles book-iii point segment circle similar-segments-of-circles straight-line similar equality coincidence reductio-ad-absurdum

G.133 equal circles equal

In equal circles equal angles stand on equal arcs, whether they stand at the centres or at the arcs.
theorem proof plane-geometry circles book-iii point segment circle angle equal-circles plane-angle stand-upon-circumference centre straight-line similar-segments-of-circles arc plane equality
