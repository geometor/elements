G.181 corresponding magnitudes

The term:term:**corresponding magnitudes** <corresponding-magnitudes> is used of antecedents in relation to antecedents, and of consequents in relation to consequents.
definition proportions magnitudes book-v corresponding-magnitudes

G.182 invertendo (inverse ratio)

Inverse ratio means taking the:term:consequent <G.181> as antecedent in relation to the antecedent as consequent.
definition proportions magnitudes book-v invertendo corresponding-magnitudes ratio

G.190 number magnitudes proportional

If any number of magnitudes be proportional, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so will all the antecedents be to all the consequents.
theorem proof proportions magnitudes book-v point proportional corresponding-magnitudes multiple proportion number equality

G.193 parts ratio multiples

Parts have the same ratio as the same multiples of them taken in corresponding order.
theorem proof proportions magnitudes book-v point segment in-the-same-ratio multiple corresponding-magnitudes ratio equality

G.194 alternando (alternate ratio)

Alternate ratio means taking the:term:antecedent <G.181> in relation to the antecedent and the consequent in relation to the consequent.
definition proportions magnitudes book-v alternando corresponding-magnitudes ratio

G.196 componendo (composition of ratio)

Composition of a ratio means taking the:term:antecedent <G.181> together with the consequent as one in relation to the consequent by itself.
definition proportions magnitudes book-v componendo corresponding-magnitudes ratio

G.197 separando (separation of ratio)

Separation of a ratio means taking the excess by which the:term:antecedent <G.181> exceeds the consequent in relation to the consequent by itself.
definition proportions magnitudes book-v separando corresponding-magnitudes ratio

G.200 convertendo (conversion of ratio)

Conversion of a ratio means taking the:term:antecedent <G.181> in relation to the excess by which the antecedent exceeds the consequent.
definition proportions magnitudes book-v convertendo corresponding-magnitudes ratio

G.203 perturbed proportion

A:term:**perturbed proportion** <perturbed-proportion> arises when, there being three magnitudes and another set equal to them in multitude, as:term:antecedent <G.181> is to consequent among the first magnitudes, so is antecedent to consequent among the second magnitudes, while, as the consequent is to a third among the first magnitudes, so is a third to the antecedent among the second magnitudes.
definition proportions magnitudes book-v perturbed-proportion corresponding-magnitudes proportion

G.232 similar polygons divided

Similar polygons are divided into similar triangles, and into triangles equal in multitude and in the same ratio as the wholes, and the polygon has to the polygon a ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side.
theorem proof plane-geometry proportions similar-figures book-vi segment angle triangle polygon similar-rectilineal-figures trilateral in-the-same-ratio ratio plane-angle proportional ex-aequali corresponding-magnitudes alternando duplicate-ratio similar equality porism

G.265 there many numbers

If there be as many numbers as we please in proportion, then, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so are all the antecedents to all the consequents.
theorem proof number-theory book-vii point number corresponding-magnitudes part-aliquot parts-non-aliquot proportion

G.268 unit measure number

If an unit measure any number, and another number measure any other number the same number of times, alternately also, the unit will measure the third number the same number of times that the second measures the fourth.
theorem proof number-theory book-vii point segment unit part-aliquot number alternando corresponding-magnitudes equality

G.274 least numbers ratio

The least numbers of those which have the same ratio with them measure those which have the same ratio the same number of times, the greater the greater and the less the less.
