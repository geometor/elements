G.135

If there be any number of magnitudes whatever which are, respectively, equimultiples of any magnitudes equal in multitude, then, whatever multiple one of the magnitudes is of one, that multiple also will all be of all.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point segment number equality

G.136

If a first magnitude be the same multiple of a second that a third is of a fourth, and a fifth also be the same multiple of the second that a sixth is of the fourth, the sum of the first and fifth is the same multiple of the second that the sum of the third and sixth is of the fourth.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point segment

G.137

If a first magnitude be the same multiple of a second that a third is of a fourth, and if equimultiples be taken of the first and third, then also:term:*ex aequali* <ex-aequali> the magnitudes taken is equimultiples respectively, the one of the second and the other of the fourth.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point segment ex-aequali equality

G.138

Magnitudes are said to:term:**be in the same ratio** <in-the-same-ratio>, the first to the second and the third to the fourth, when, if any equimultiples whatever be taken of the first and third, and any equimultiples whatever of the second and fourth, the former equimultiples alike exceed, are alike equal to, or alike fall short of, the latter equimultiples respectively taken in corresponding order.
Category: definition proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v in-the-same-ratio ratio

G.139

If a first magnitude have to a second the same ratio as a third to a fourth, any equimultiples whatever of the first and third will also have the same ratio to any equimultiples whatever of the second and fourth respectively, taken in corresponding order.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point ratio equality

G.140

If a magnitude be the same multiple of a magnitude that a part subtracted is of a part subtracted, the remainder is the same multiple of the remainder that the whole is of the whole.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v segment equality

G.141

If two magnitudes be equimultiples of two magnitudes, and any magnitudes subtracted from them be equimultiples of the same, the remainders also are either equal to the same or equimultiples of them.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point segment equality

G.142

Equal magnitudes have to the same the same ratio, as also has the same to equal magnitudes.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point ratio equality porism

G.143

Magnitudes are said to:term:**have a ratio** <have-a-ratio> to one another which are capable, when multiplied, of exceeding one another.
Category: definition proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v have-a-ratio ratio

G.144

When, of the equimultiples, the multiple of the first magnitude exceeds the multiple of the second, but the multiple of the third does not exceed the multiple of the fourth, then the first is said to:term:**have a greater ratio** <greater-ratio> to the second than the third has to the fourth.
Category: definition proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v greater-ratio ratio

G.145

Of unequal magnitudes, the greater has to the same a greater ratio than the less has; and the same has to the less a greater ratio than it has to the greater.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point segment ratio equality

G.146

Magnitudes which have the same ratio to the same are equal; and magnitudes to which the same has the same ratio are equal.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point ratio equality

G.147

Of magnitudes which have a ratio to the same, that which has a greater ratio is greater; and that to which the same has a greater ratio is less.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point ratio equality reductio-ad-absurdum

G.148

Ratios which are the same with the same ratio are also the same with one another.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point ratio equality

G.149

If any number of magnitudes be proportional, as one of the antecedents is to one of the consequents, so will all the antecedents be to all the consequents.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point proportion number equality

G.150

If a first magnitude have to a second the same ratio as a third to a fourth, and the third have to the fourth a greater ratio than a fifth has to a sixth, the first will also have to the second a greater ratio than the fifth to the sixth.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point ratio equality

G.151

If a first magnitude have to a second the same ratio as a third has to a fourth, and the first be greater than the third, the second is greater than the fourth; if equal, equal; and if less, less.

G.152

G.156

If, as a whole is to a whole, so is a part subtracted to a part subtracted, the remainder is to the remainder as whole to whole.

G.157

If there be three magnitudes, and others equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two are in the same ratio, and if:term:*ex aequali* <ex-aequali> the first be greater than the third, the fourth is greater than the sixth; if equal, equal; and, if less, less.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point ex-aequali ratio equality

G.158

If there be three magnitudes, and others equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two together are in the same ratio, and the proportion of them be perturbed, then, if ex aequali the first magnitude is greater than the third, the fourth is greater than the sixth; if equal, equal; and if less, less.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point ex-aequali ratio proportion equality

G.159

If there be any number of magnitudes whatever, and others equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two together are in the same ratio, they is in the same ratio ex aequali.

G.160

If there be three magnitudes, and others equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two together are in the same ratio, and the proportion of them be perturbed, they is in the same ratio ex aequali.

G.161

If a first magnitude have to a second the same ratio as a third has to a fourth, and also a fifth have to the second the same ratio as a sixth to the fourth, the first and fifth added together will have to the second the same ratio as the third and sixth have to the fourth.
Category: theorem proof proportions magnitudes Tags: book-v point segment ex-aequali separando componendo ratio

G.162

G.182
