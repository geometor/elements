Posts tagged 'cone'

G.496

Any cone is a third part of the cylinder which has the same base with it and equal height.
theorem proof solid-geometry method-of-exhaustion book-xii point segment circle triangle quadrilateral polygon cone cylinder equality reductio-ad-absurdum

G.497

Cones and cylinders which are of the same height are to one another as their bases.
theorem proof solid-geometry method-of-exhaustion book-xii point segment circle triangle quadrilateral polygon cone cylinder reductio-ad-absurdum

G.498

Similar cones and cylinders are those in which the axes and the diameters of the bases are proportional.
definition solid-geometry book-xi similar-cones-and-cylinders proportion similar cone cylinder

G.499

Similar cones and cylinders are to one another in the triplicate ratio of the diameters in their bases.
theorem proof solid-geometry method-of-exhaustion book-xii point segment circle angle triangle quadrilateral polygon ex-aequali ratio triplicate-ratio similar cone cylinder equality reductio-ad-absurdum

G.501

Cones and cylinders which are on equal bases are to one another as their heights.
theorem proof solid-geometry method-of-exhaustion book-xii point segment circle triangle polygon cone cylinder equality

G.502

In equal cones and cylinders the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights; and those cones and cylinders in which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal.
theorem proof solid-geometry method-of-exhaustion book-xii point segment circle polygon proportion cone cylinder equality
