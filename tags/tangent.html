G.505

Given two spheres about the same centre, to inscribe in the greater sphere a polyhedral solid which does not touch the lesser sphere at its surface.

G.503

Given two circles about the same centre, to inscribe in the greater circle an equilateral polygon with an even number of sides which does not touch the lesser circle.
Category: problem construction solid-geometry method-of-exhaustion Tags: book-xii point segment circle equilateral tangent inscribed number even-number equality

G.120

Similarly a circle is said to be:term:**inscribed in a figure** <circle-inscribed-in-figure> when the circumference of the circle touches each side of the figure in which it is inscribed.
Category: definition plane-geometry circles polygons Tags: book-iv circle-inscribed-in-figure tangent arc inscribed similar

G.115

If a point be taken outside a circle and from it there fall on the circle two straight lines, and if one of them cut the circle and the other touch it, the rectangle contained by the whole of the straight line which cuts the circle and the straight line intercepted on it outside between the point and the convex circumference will equals the square on the tangent.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle angle rectangle tangent equality

G.111

If a straight line touch a circle, and from the point of contact there be drawn across, in the circle, a straight line cutting the circle, the angles which it makes with the tangent will equals the angles in the alternate segments of the circle.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle angle quadrilateral tangent equality

G.97

If a straight line touch a circle, and from the point of contact a straight line be drawn at right angles to the tangent, the centre of the circle is on the straight line so drawn.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle angle right-angle tangent equality reductio-ad-absurdum

G.96

If a straight line touch a circle, and a straight line be joined from the centre to the point of contact, the straight line so joined is perpendicular to the tangent.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle angle perpendicular tangent equality reductio-ad-absurdum

G.95

From a given point to draw a straight line touching a given circle.
Category: problem construction plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle angle triangle tangent equality

G.89

A circle does not touch a circle at more points than one, whether it touch it internally or externally.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle tangent equality reductio-ad-absurdum

G.88

Circles are said to:term:**touch one another** <circles-touching> which, meeting one another, do not cut one another.
Category: definition plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii circles-touching tangent

G.87

If two circles touch one another externally, the straight line joining their centres will pass through the point of contact.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle tangent equality reductio-ad-absurdum

G.86

If two circles touch one another internally, and their centres be taken, the straight line joining their centres, if it be also produced, will fall on the point of contact of the circles.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle tangent equality reductio-ad-absurdum

G.81

If two circles touch one another, they will not have the same centre.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle tangent equality reductio-ad-absurdum
