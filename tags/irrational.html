G.442

G.437

If from a medial area there be subtracted a medial area incommensurable with the whole, the two remaining irrational straight lines arise, either a second apotome of a medial straight line or a straight line which produces with a medial area a medial whole.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon incommensurable irrational medial apotome

G.436

If from a medial area a rational area be subtracted, there arise two other irrational straight lines, either a first apotome of a medial straight line or a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole.

G.435

If from a rational area a medial area be subtracted, the “side” of the remaining area becomes one of two irrational straight lines, either an apotome or a minor straight line.

G.399

If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which with the whole makes the sum of the squares on them medial, twice the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the squares on them incommensurable with twice the rectangle contained by them, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called:term:**that which produces with a medial area a medial whole** <side-of-a-medial-minus-a-medial-area>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon side-of-a-medial-minus-a-medial-area square rectangle incommensurable irrational medial

G.398

If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line which is incommensurable in square with the whole, and which with the whole makes the sum of the squares on them medial, but twice the rectangle contained by them rational, the remainder is irrational: and let it be called:term:**that which produces with a rational area a medial whole** <side-of-a-medial-minus-a-rational-area>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment side-of-a-medial-minus-a-rational-area square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.397

If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which with the whole makes the squares on them added together rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called minor.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment convertendo minor square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.396

If from a medial straight line there be subtracted a medial straight line which is commensurable with the whole in square only, and which contains with the whole a medial rectangle, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called a second apotome of a medial straight line.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon second-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line square rectangle commensurable irrational medial apotome

G.395

If from a medial straight line there be subtracted a medial straight line which is commensurable with the whole in square only, and which contains with the whole a rational rectangle, the remainder is irrational. And let it be called a first apotome of a medial straight line.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment first-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line square rectangle commensurable rational irrational medial apotome

G.394

If from a rational straight line there be subtracted a rational straight line commensurable with the whole in square only, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called an:term:**apotome** <apotome>.

G.393

G.356

If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, and the rectangle contained by them medial and also incommensurable with the sum of the squares on them, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational; and let it be called the:term:**side of the sum of two medial areas** <side-of-the-sum-of-two-medial-areas>.

G.354

If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational: and let it be called:term:**major** <major>.

G.353

If two medial straight lines commensurable in square only and containing a medial rectangle be added together, the whole is irrational; and let it be called a:term:**second bimedial straight line** <second-bimedial>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon second-bimedial square rectangle commensurable irrational medial

G.352

If two medial straight lines commensurable in square only and containing a rational rectangle be added together, the whole is irrational; and let it be called a:term:**first bimedial straight line** <first-bimedial>.

With these hypotheses, it is proved that there exist straight lines infinite in multitude which are commensurable and incommensurable respectively, some in length only, and others in square also, with an assigned straight line. Let then the assigned straight line be called:term:**rational** <rational>, and those straight lines which are commensurable with it, whether in length and in square or in square only,:term:**rational** <rational>, but those which are incommensurable with it:term:**irrational** <irrational>.
