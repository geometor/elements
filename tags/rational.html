Posts tagged 'rational'

G.332

And let the square on the assigned straight line be called:term:**rational** <rational> and those areas which are commensurable with it:term:**rational** <rational>, but those which are incommensurable with it:term:**irrational** <irrational>, and the straight lines which produce them:term:**irrational** <irrational>, that is, in case the areas are squares, the sides themselves, but in case they are any other polygons, the straight lines on which are described squares equal to them.
Category: definition incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x rational irrational square commensurable incommensurable

G.333

The rectangle contained by rational straight lines commensurable in length is rational.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon rectangle commensurable rational equality

G.334

If a rational area be applied to a rational straight line, it produces as breadth a straight line rational and commensurable in length with the straight line to which it is applied.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon commensurable rational equality

G.335

The rectangle contained by rational straight lines commensurable in square only is irrational, and the side of the square equal to it is irrational. Let the latter be called medial.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon medial square rectangle commensurable rational irrational equality

G.336

The square on a medial straight line, if applied to a rational straight line, produces as breadth a straight line rational and incommensurable in length with that to which it is applied.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x point segment polygon square incommensurable rational medial equality

G.337

With these hypotheses, it is proved that there exist straight lines infinite in multitude which are commensurable and incommensurable respectively, some in length only, and others in square also, with an assigned straight line. Let then the assigned straight line be called:term:**rational** <rational>, and those straight lines which are commensurable with it, whether in length and in square or in square only,:term:**rational** <rational>, but those which are incommensurable with it:term:**irrational** <irrational>.

G.354

G.355

If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational; and let it be called the:term:**side of a rational plus a medial area** <side-of-a-rational-plus-a-medial-area>.

G.369

Given a rational straight line and a binomial, divided into its terms, such that the square on the greater term is greater than the square on the lesser by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with the greater, then, if the greater term be commensurable in length with the rational straight line set out, let the whole be called a:term:**first binomial straight line** <first-binomial-straight-line>;
Category: definition incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x first-binomial-straight-line square commensurable rational binomial

G.370

G.371

G.372

G.373

G.394

G.395

G.397

G.398
