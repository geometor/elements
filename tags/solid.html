G.529 solid

A:term:**solid** <solid> is that which has length, breadth, and depth.
definition solid-geometry book-xi solid

G.530 solid contained parallel

If a solid be contained by parallel planes, the opposite planes in it are equal and parallelogrammic.

G.531 parallelepipedal solid cut

If a parallelepipedal solid be cut by a plane which is parallel to the opposite planes, then, as the base is to the base, so will the solid be to the solid.

G.537 prism

A:term:**prism** <prism> is a:term:solid figure <G.529> contained by:term:planes <G.7> two of which, namely those which are opposite, are equal, similar and:term:parallel <G.52>, while the rest are parallelograms.

G.538 parallelepipedal solid cut

If a parallelepipedal solid be cut by a plane through the diagonals of the opposite planes, the solid is bisected by the plane.

G.539 parallelepipedal solids base

G.542 parallelepipedal solids height

G.544 equal parallelepipedal solids

In equal parallelepipedal solids the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights; and those parallelepipedal solids in which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal.
theorem proof solid-geometry book-xi point segment polygon solid reciprocally-related height in-the-same-ratio right-angle plane-surface proportion parallelepiped equality

G.546 segments proportional parallelepipedal

If three segments be proportional, the parallelepipedal solid formed out of the three is equal to the parallelepipedal solid on the mean which is equilateral, but equiangular with the aforesaid solid.

G.547 segments proportional parallelepipedal

If four segments be proportional, the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described is proportional; and, if the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described be proportional, the segments will themselves also be proportional.
