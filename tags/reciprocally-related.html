G.224 reciprocally related figures

Reciprocally related figures have their sides about equal angles reciprocally proportional.
definition plane-geometry proportions similar-figures book-vi reciprocally-related

G.225 equal equiangular parallelograms

In equal and equiangular parallelograms the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional; and equiangular parallelograms in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional are equal.
theorem proof plane-geometry proportions similar-figures book-vi segment angle polygon plane-angle reciprocally-related straight-line parallelogram proportion plane equality

G.226 equal triangles angle

In equal triangles which have one angle equal to one angle the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional; and those triangles which have one angle equal to one angle, and in which the sides about the equal angles are reciprocally proportional, are equal.
theorem proof plane-geometry proportions similar-figures book-vi segment angle triangle trilateral plane-angle reciprocally-related straight-line in-the-same-ratio proportion plane equality

G.227 segments proportional rectangle

If four segments be proportional, the rectangle contained by the extremes is equal to the rectangle contained by the means; and, if the rectangle contained by the extremes equals the rectangle contained by the means, the four segments is proportional.

G.231 similar triangles duplicate

Similar triangles are to one another in the duplicate ratio of the corresponding sides.

G.396 square medial segment

The square on a medial segment, if applied to a rational segment, produces as breadth a segment rational and incommensurable in length with that to which it is applied.

G.544 equal parallelepipedal solids

In equal parallelepipedal solids the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights; and those parallelepipedal solids in which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal.

G.546 segments proportional parallelepipedal

If three segments be proportional, the parallelepipedal solid formed out of the three is equal to the parallelepipedal solid on the mean which is equilateral, but equiangular with the aforesaid solid.

G.560 equal pyramids triangular

In equal pyramids which have triangular bases the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights; and those pyramids in which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal.

G.573 equal cones cylinders

In equal cones and cylinders the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights; and those cones and cylinders in which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal.
