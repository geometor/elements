G.230 duplicate ratio

When three magnitudes are:term:proportional <G.180>, the first is said to have to the third the:term:**duplicate ratio** <duplicate-ratio> of that which it has to the second.
definition proportions magnitudes book-v proportional duplicate-ratio ratio proportion

G.231 similar triangles duplicate

Similar triangles are to one another in the duplicate ratio of the corresponding sides.

G.232 similar polygons divided

Similar polygons are divided into similar triangles, and into triangles equal in multitude and in the same ratio as the wholes, and the polygon has to the polygon a ratio duplicate of that which the corresponding side has to the corresponding side.
theorem proof plane-geometry proportions similar-figures book-vi segment angle triangle polygon similar-rectilineal-figures trilateral in-the-same-ratio ratio plane-angle proportional ex-aequali corresponding-magnitudes alternando duplicate-ratio similar equality porism

G.381 squares segments commensurable

The squares on segments commensurable in length have to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number; and squares which have to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number will also have their sides commensurable in length. But the squares on segments incommensurable in length have not to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number; and squares which have not to one another the ratio which a square number has to a square number will not have their sides commensurable in length either.
