G.82 distributive law of rectangles

If one of two segments is cut into any number of parts, the rectangle contained by the two segments equals the sum of the rectangles contained by the uncut segment and each of the parts.

G.83 square equals sum of rectangles on parts

G.94 law of cosines for obtuse triangles

G.143 intersecting chords theorem
