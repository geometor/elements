Posts tagged 'rectangle'

G.404

To a straight line which produces with a rational area a medial whole only one straight line can be annexed which is incommensurable in square with the whole straight line and which with the whole straight line makes the sum of the squares on them medial, but twice the rectangle contained by them rational.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment square rectangle incommensurable rational medial reductio-ad-absurdum

G.403

To a minor straight line only one straight line can be annexed which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which makes, with the whole, the sum of the squares on them rational but twice the rectangle contained by them medial.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment square rectangle incommensurable rational medial reductio-ad-absurdum

G.402

To a second apotome of a medial straight line only one medial straight line can be annexed which is commensurable with the whole in square only and which contains with the whole a medial rectangle.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon square rectangle commensurable medial apotome reductio-ad-absurdum

G.401

To a first apotome of a medial straight line only one medial straight line can be annexed which is commensurable with the whole in square only and which contains with the whole a rational rectangle.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment square rectangle commensurable rational medial apotome reductio-ad-absurdum

G.399

If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which with the whole makes the sum of the squares on them medial, twice the rectangle contained by them medial, and further the squares on them incommensurable with twice the rectangle contained by them, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called:term:**that which produces with a medial area a medial whole** <side-of-a-medial-minus-a-medial-area>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon side-of-a-medial-minus-a-medial-area square rectangle incommensurable irrational medial

G.398

If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line which is incommensurable in square with the whole, and which with the whole makes the sum of the squares on them medial, but twice the rectangle contained by them rational, the remainder is irrational: and let it be called:term:**that which produces with a rational area a medial whole** <side-of-a-medial-minus-a-rational-area>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment side-of-a-medial-minus-a-rational-area square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.397

If from a straight line there be subtracted a straight line which is incommensurable in square with the whole and which with the whole makes the squares on them added together rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called minor.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment convertendo minor square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.396

If from a medial straight line there be subtracted a medial straight line which is commensurable with the whole in square only, and which contains with the whole a medial rectangle, the remainder is irrational; and let it be called a second apotome of a medial straight line.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon second-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line square rectangle commensurable irrational medial apotome

G.395

If from a medial straight line there be subtracted a medial straight line which is commensurable with the whole in square only, and which contains with the whole a rational rectangle, the remainder is irrational. And let it be called a first apotome of a medial straight line.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment first-apotome-of-a-medial-straight-line square rectangle commensurable rational irrational medial apotome

G.356

If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, and the rectangle contained by them medial and also incommensurable with the sum of the squares on them, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational; and let it be called the:term:**side of the sum of two medial areas** <side-of-the-sum-of-two-medial-areas>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon side-of-the-sum-of-two-medial-areas square rectangle incommensurable irrational medial

G.355

If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial, but the rectangle contained by them rational, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational; and let it be called the:term:**side of a rational plus a medial area** <side-of-a-rational-plus-a-medial-area>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment side-of-a-rational-plus-a-medial-area square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.354

If two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational, but the rectangle contained by them medial, be added together, the whole straight line is irrational: and let it be called:term:**major** <major>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment major square rectangle incommensurable rational irrational medial

G.353

If two medial straight lines commensurable in square only and containing a medial rectangle be added together, the whole is irrational; and let it be called a:term:**second bimedial straight line** <second-bimedial>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon second-bimedial square rectangle commensurable irrational medial

G.352

If two medial straight lines commensurable in square only and containing a rational rectangle be added together, the whole is irrational; and let it be called a:term:**first bimedial straight line** <first-bimedial>.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment first-bimedial componendo square rectangle commensurable rational irrational medial

G.350

Find two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial and the rectangle contained by them medial and moreover incommensurable with the sum of the squares on them.
Category: problem finding incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment circle square rectangle incommensurable medial equality

G.349

Find two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them medial but the rectangle contained by them rational.

G.348

Find two straight lines incommensurable in square which make the sum of the squares on them rational but the rectangle contained by them medial.
Category: problem finding incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x point segment circle square rectangle incommensurable rational medial equality

G.347

Find two medial straight lines commensurable in square only, containing a medial rectangle, and such that the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line commensurable with the greater.
Category: problem finding incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x point square rectangle commensurable medial

G.346

Find two medial straight lines commensurable in square only, containing a rational rectangle, and such that the square on the greater is greater than the square on the less by the square on a straight line commensurable in length with the greater.
Category: problem finding incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x point square rectangle commensurable rational medial

G.343

Find medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a medial rectangle.

G.342

Find medial straight lines commensurable in square only which contain a rational rectangle.
Category: problem finding incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x point square rectangle commensurable rational medial

G.340

The rectangle contained by medial straight lines commensurable in square only is either rational or medial.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon square rectangle commensurable rational medial equality

G.339

The rectangle contained by medial straight lines commensurable in length is medial.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon rectangle commensurable medial equality

G.335

The rectangle contained by rational straight lines commensurable in square only is irrational, and the side of the square equal to it is irrational. Let the latter be called medial.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon medial square rectangle commensurable rational irrational equality

G.333

The rectangle contained by rational straight lines commensurable in length is rational.
Category: theorem proof incommensurables magnitudes Tags: book-x segment polygon rectangle commensurable rational equality

G.180

If three straight lines be proportional, the rectangle contained by the extremes is equal to the square on the mean; and, if the rectangle contained by the extremes equals the square on the mean, the three straight lines is proportional.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry proportions similar-figures Tags: book-vi point square rectangle proportion equality

G.179

If four straight lines be proportional, the rectangle contained by the extremes is equal to the rectangle contained by the means; and, if the rectangle contained by the extremes equals the rectangle contained by the means, the four straight lines is proportional.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry proportions similar-figures Tags: book-vi point segment polygon rectangle proportion equality

G.116

If a point be taken outside a circle and from the point there fall on the circle two straight lines, if one of them cut the circle, and the other fall on it, and if further the rectangle contained by the whole of the straight line which cuts the circle and the straight line intercepted on it outside between the point and the convex circumference equals the square on the straight line which falls on the circle, the straight line which falls on it will touch the circle.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle angle rectangle equality

G.115

If a point be taken outside a circle and from it there fall on the circle two straight lines, and if one of them cut the circle and the other touch it, the rectangle contained by the whole of the straight line which cuts the circle and the straight line intercepted on it outside between the point and the convex circumference will equals the square on the tangent.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle angle rectangle tangent equality

G.114

If in a circle two straight lines cut one another, the rectangle contained by the segments of the one is equal to the rectangle contained by the segments of the other.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry circles Tags: book-iii point segment circle rectangle equality

G.74

In acute-angled triangles the square on the side subtending the acute angle is less than the squares on the sides containing the acute angle by twice the rectangle contained by one of the sides about the acute angle, namely that on which the perpendicular falls, and the straight line cut off within by the perpendicular towards the acute angle.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment angle triangle acute-angle square rectangle perpendicular

G.73

In obtuse-angled triangles the square on the side subtending the obtuse angle is greater than the squares on the sides containing the obtuse angle by twice the rectangle contained by one of the sides about the obtuse angle, namely that on which the perpendicular falls, and the straight line cut off outside by the perpendicular towards the obtuse angle.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment line angle triangle obtuse-angle square rectangle

G.72

Cut a given straight line so that the rectangle contained by the whole and one of the segments is equal to the square on the remaining segment.
Category: problem construction plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment angle quadrilateral polygon square rectangle equality

G.69

If a straight line be cut at random, four times the rectangle contained by the whole and one of the segments together with the square on the remaining segment is equal to the square described on the whole and the aforesaid segment as on one straight line.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment triangle quadrilateral polygon square rectangle equality

G.68

If a straight line be cut at random, the square on the whole and that on one of the segments both together are equal to twice the rectangle contained by the whole and the said segment and the square on the remaining segment.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment triangle quadrilateral polygon square rectangle equality

G.67

If a straight line be bisected and a straight line be added to it in a straight line, the rectangle contained by the whole with the added straight line and the added straight line together with the square on the half is equal to the square on the straight line made up of the half and the added straight line.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment triangle quadrilateral polygon square rectangle bisect equality

G.66

If a straight line be cut into equal and unequal segments, the rectangle contained by the unequal segments of the whole together with the square on the straight line between the points of section is equal to the square on the half.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment triangle quadrilateral polygon square rectangle equality

G.65

If a straight line be cut at random, the square on the whole is equal to the squares on the segments and twice the rectangle contained by the segments.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment angle quadrilateral polygon square rectangle equality

G.64

If a straight line be cut at random, the rectangle contained by the whole and one of the segments is equal to the rectangle contained by the segments and the square on the aforesaid segment.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment quadrilateral polygon square rectangle equality

G.63

If a straight line be cut at random, the rectangle contained by the whole and both of the segments is equal to the square on the whole.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment quadrilateral polygon square rectangle equality

G.62

If there be two straight lines, and one of them be cut into any number of segments whatever, the rectangle contained by the two straight lines is equal to the rectangles contained by the uncut straight line and each of the segments.
Category: theorem proof plane-geometry geometric-algebra Tags: book-ii point segment polygon rectangle number equality
